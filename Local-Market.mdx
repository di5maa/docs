title: "Local Market"
---

Local Market is the built-in marketplace inside the Local Pass app where you can browse and buy products directly from local farmers, producers, and small businesses — no middlemen, no markups.

Everything you see is sourced from vendors operating in your area, so what you find depends on where you are and what's in season.

<Note>
  Local Market is separate from the restaurant section of the app. Products here are things you take home — groceries, produce, artisan goods, and more.
</Note>

---

## How to Get There

1. Open the **Local Pass** app
2. Tap the **shop bag icon** on the bottom navigation bar, or tap **Local Market** from the home screen
3. You'll land on the **Market home page**, which shows you featured deals, top-rated items, and new arrivals

---

## What You'll Find

The Market home page is organized into several sections that update based on what's available in your area:

| Section | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Category Chips** | Quick links to browse by product type (e.g. Dairy, Produce, Meat) |
| **Fresh Deals** | Products currently on sale — sale price is lower than the regular price |
| **Featured Vendors** | Local stores you can browse directly |
| **Highly Rated** | Products with a customer rating of 4.0 or above |
| **New Arrivals** | Recently added products |
| **Soon to Go 🏷️** | Products close to their best-before date, discounted |
| **Explore All** | The full product feed with filtering options |

---

## Navigating the Market

### Search bar

At the top of the market you'll see a **Search products…** bar. Tapping it takes you to the search page where you can type in what you're looking for.

### Category chips

Below the top banner, you'll see a horizontal row of category chips (e.g. _Produce_, _Bakery_, _Dairy_). Tapping any of these takes you to all products in that category.

### Section headers with arrows

Each section header (like "Fresh Deals" or "Highly Rated") has a small arrow on the right. Tapping it takes you to a dedicated page for that section with more products and filter options.

---

## Tip: Pull to Refresh

If you've been browsing for a while and want to see the latest products, **swipe down** from the top of the market home page to refresh everything.
