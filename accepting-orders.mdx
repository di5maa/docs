---
title: 'Accepting Orders'
---

**Where to find it:** Tap any order in your orders list to open the panel

The Order Fulfillment Panel gives you full control over a single order from the moment it arrives to the moment it's completed. It's designed for fast, confident action with minimal taps.

---

## Order Header

At the top of the panel you'll see:
- The **order number**
- Current **status** (e.g., "Placed", "Preparing", "Ready for pickup")
- Whether it is a **Pickup** or **Delivery** order
- A subtle spinner if a **payment capture** is processing in the background

---

## Adjusting the Ready Time

The large time display in the centre of the panel shows when the order needs to be ready.

- Tap **− 5** to subtract 5 minutes from the ready time
- Tap **+ 5** to add 5 minutes

When you've changed the time, a **SAVE** button appears below. Tap it to confirm — the customer and any assigned driver will both receive a push notification with the updated time automatically.

---

## Customer & Driver Info

### Customer row
Shows the customer's first name and phone number so you can contact them if needed.

### Driver row (delivery orders only)
Shows the name of the assigned driver and their estimated arrival time at your location. If no driver has been matched yet, a live search animation shows the system is actively looking for a nearby driver.

<Note>
  For delivery orders, the driver search begins automatically when you tap **Accept Order**. You don't need to do anything to initiate it.
</Note>

---

## Customer Notes

If a customer left special instructions (e.g., "no onions", "please double-bag", "allergy to nuts"), they appear in a **blue card** below the customer info row.

Always check this before preparing the order.

---

## Items Table

A full breakdown of everything in the order, showing:
- Item name and quantity (e.g., "2 × Sourdough Loaf")
- Selected options or customisations (e.g., "...Extra seeds", "...No salt")
- Price per line item

---

## Action Buttons

The main action button at the bottom of the panel changes based on the current order status:

| Current Status | Button Shown |
|---|---|
| Placed | **Accept Order** |
| Accepted / Preparing / Packaged / Ready | **Ready for Pickup** |
| Ready for pickup | **Order Picked Up** |

### Accept Order
Tapping this confirms you've received the order and will prepare it. For delivery orders, this simultaneously:
- Creates a delivery request
- Begins searching for an available driver

### Ready for Pickup
Tapping this marks the order as prepared and ready to hand off. It also:
- Notifies the customer (for pickup orders) that their order is ready
- Notifies the assigned driver (for delivery orders) to come collect it
- Starts an early payment capture in the background so handoff is instant

### Order Picked Up
Tapping this completes the order and finalises payment. For pickup orders, it marks the order as **Completed**. For delivery orders, it marks the order as handed to the courier.

<Tip>
  The system starts capturing payment when you tap **Ready for Pickup**. By the time you tap **Order Picked Up**, the payment is already processed — so there's no delay at the handoff moment.
</Tip>

---

## Cancelling an order

Tap the **Cancel** button (to the right of the main action button) to cancel the order.

A confirmation sheet will appear asking you to confirm. Once cancelled:
- The payment hold on the customer's card is **automatically released** — no charge is made
- The customer receives a push notification
- The order disappears from your active queue

<Warning>
  The Cancel button is hidden once a driver has already picked up the food. Cancelling after this point is not supported to protect the driver and customer.
</Warning>

---

## Payment capture errors

If something goes wrong when capturing payment, a **red error banner** appears at the top of the panel explaining what failed.

Tap **Retry** to attempt the capture again. Common causes of failure include temporary network issues or card problems — retrying usually resolves it. If the problem persists, contact support.

---

## Support button

Tap the **headset icon** (bottom right of the action bar) to open a live support chat with the Local Pass team. This is pre-filled with your order details so support can help faster.

---

## Completed orders

When an order is fully completed, a **green overlay** appears on the panel showing:
- A pulsing green checkmark
- A delivery timeline animation (Kitchen → Driver → Customer)
- A note confirming the customer has been notified

Tap **Got it** to dismiss the overlay and move on to your next order.

---

## Cancelled orders

If an order is cancelled (by you or by the system), a **grey overlay** appears explaining that the payment hold has been released and no charge was made. Tap **Close** to return to your order list.
