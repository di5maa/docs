Welcome to Local Pass! After registering your account, the next step is to set up your **Store Profile**. This is how customers will find you, learn about your business, and contact you.

The setup process is divided into **5 easy steps**.

### Prerequisites 

Before you begin, please ensure you have the following handy:

- Your business contact information (Email, Phone, Website).
- A short description of your business (Bio).
- Your business logo and a cover photo (saved on your phone or computer).

## Step 1: Owner Details

The first screen focuses on you, the merchant. We use these details to verify your account and contact you for administrative purposes.

**Action Items:**

1. **First & Last Name:** Enter your legal name.
2. **Mobile Number:** Enter a valid mobile phone number.

<Note>
  **Note:** Your personal mobile number is used for account security and platform updates. It is **not** displayed publicly on your store profile unless you add it again in the "Business Contact" section.
</Note>

## Step 2: Store Details

Here is where you define your brand identity. This information will appear prominently on your shop page.

**Action Items:**

1. **Business Name:** Enter the name of your shop (e.g., _Gourmet Meats & Pastry_).
2. **About Business:** Write a description. Tell your story! (Max 1000 characters).
3. **Tagline:** A short, catchy phrase about your store (e.g., _Fresh. Local. Organic._).
4. **Business Type:** Select your primary business type from the dropdown menu (e.g., Restaurant, Retail, Service).

**Dynamic Options:**

- **If you select "Restaurant":** A new dropdown will appear asking for your **Cuisine Type** (e.g., Italian, Mexican, Fusion).
- **If you select other types:** A dropdown will appear asking for your **Business Category**.

Click **Next Step** to proceed.

## Step 3: Location & Contact

Help customers find your physical location and get in touch with you.

**Action Items:**

1. **Business Address:**
   - Tap the search bar under "Business address".
   - Start typing your street address.
   - **Important:** Select your address from the list of suggestions that appear. This ensures your store is pinned correctly on the Mintify map.
2. **Contact Info:**
   - **Email:** Enter your public business email (e.g., [_hello@localpass.ca_](mailto:hello@localpass.ca)).
   - **Phone:** Enter your public business phone number.
   - **Website:** Enter your website URL if you have one.

> **📍 Tip:** If you cannot find your exact address, try typing just the street name and selecting the closest option, then editing the text.

## Step 4: Sales Model & Attributes

Define how you sell your products and highlight what makes your business unique.

### Sales Model

Use the toggle switches to select how you operate. **Note:** Selecting specific options may automatically adjust others.

- **Retail:** You sell directly to consumers.
- **Wholesale:** You sell in bulk to other businesses.
- **Dine-in / Takeout:** (Visible for Restaurants) Select if you offer seating, takeout, or both.

### Store Attributes

Mintify highlights diverse businesses. Toggle the switches on if they apply to you:

- **Indigenous-owned**
- **Women-owned**
- **Ethnic Store / Cultural Heritage**

## Step 5: Appearance & Hours

The final step is to make your profile look professional with hours of operation and imagery.

**Action Items:**

1. **Location Hours:**
   - You will see a list of days.
   - Set your **Open** and **Close** times for each day.
   - Toggle the switch to mark specific days as **Closed**.
2. **Upload Imagery:**
   - **Upload Logo:** Tap the button to upload a square image of your logo.
   - **Upload Cover Photo:** Tap the button to upload a wider image that represents your shop (e.g., your storefront or popular products).

> **✅ Success:** Once your images are uploaded and hours are set, the **Complete** button will become active.

### What Happens Next?

Once you click **Complete**, your online store is registered on Local Pass. You will be automatically navigated to your **Merchant Dashboard**, where you can start adding products and managing orders!
