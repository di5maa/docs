# Awards

**Where to find it:** Settings → Awards

Displaying awards on your store profile helps customers see that your business is recognised for quality. Whether it's a local "Best Of" award, a food festival ribbon, or an industry recognition, every achievement is worth sharing.

---

## Adding an award

1. Enter the **award name** in the first field (e.g., "Best Local Restaurant 2024", "Farmers Market People's Choice")
2. Enter the **year** the award was received in the second field (e.g., "2024")
3. Tap **Add**
4. A confirmation message will appear when the award is saved

<Note>
  The award name field is required. The year field is optional but recommended — it adds context and credibility.
</Note>

---

## Viewing your awards

All saved awards are listed below the form. Each award displays:

- A **medal icon** to indicate it's an award
- The **award name** in bold
- The **year** shown in a green badge

---

## Removing an award

Tap the **delete icon** (trash bin) next to any award to remove it. A confirmation dialog will appear before it is permanently deleted.

---

## Tips

- Be specific with award names — "Booking.com Best Restaurant in Victoria" is more compelling than just "Best Restaurant"
- If you've won the same award multiple times, add a separate entry for each year
- Awards are displayed publicly on your store profile page
