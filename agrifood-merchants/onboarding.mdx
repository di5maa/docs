title: "Onboarding"
---

After completing your store profile setup, you'll see a short onboarding walkthrough. This introduces you to the key features available to you as a Local Pass merchant.

You can tap **Next** to move through each screen, or tap **Skip** at any point to go directly to your dashboard.

---

## Onboarding screens overview

### 1. Welcome to Local Pass

An introduction to what Local Pass Business offers — helping local businesses reach more customers through a single platform.

### 2. Get Found by More Customers

Learn how customers discover your store on the Local Pass marketplace by searching, browsing, and filtering by category or location.

### 3. Sell Your Way, Keep Your Margins

Understand your selling options — individual meals, bundles, packages, and more — while keeping control of your pricing.

### 4. Manage Orders, Inventory, and More

See how your dashboard brings orders, inventory, fulfilment, and settings into one place, so you don't need to manage multiple apps.

### 5. Bundle Products and Partner with Others

Restaurants can partner with local producers to bundle products and create unique offerings for customers.

### 6. Meet Kyla AI — Your Business Intelligence Assistant

Kyla AI is your built-in AI assistant, designed specifically for food businesses on Local Pass. Use it to get insights and answers about your store directly from your dashboard.

### 7. Start Selling More Food Today

You're ready. Tap **Explore** to go to your dashboard and start setting up your store fully.

---

## Skipping onboarding

If you'd prefer to go straight to your dashboard at any point, tap **Skip** on any screen. You can always revisit features through your dashboard navigation.

---

## Next steps

Once you reach your dashboard, follow the [setup checklist](/dashboard#setup-checklist) to complete the remaining tasks needed before you can go live.
