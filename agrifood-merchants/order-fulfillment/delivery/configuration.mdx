The Delivery tab is your central hub for controlling how, when, and where your products get to your customers.

Whether you are offering simple standard delivery, setting up complex delivery zones, managing your own fleet of drivers, or shipping nationwide, this guide will walk you through exactly how to configure your settings to suit your business needs.

# 1. The Basics: Turning Delivery On/Off and Setting Hours

At the top of the page, you control the master switches for your delivery operations.

## Toggle Delivery Status

- **What it does:** Instantly turns your delivery offering on or off.
- **How to use:** Toggle the switch next to **Delivery**. It will display **"Offering delivery"** when active, and **"Not offering delivery"** when paused.

## Set Delivery Hours

By default, your delivery hours match your normal business hours. If your kitchen or warehouse stops offering delivery before you actually close, you can set specific hours.

- **Step 1:** Look for the switch labeled **"Delivery available during normal business hours."**
- **Step 2:** Toggle this **OFF** to set specific hours.
- **Step 3:** A weekly schedule will appear. Adjust the **Open From** and **Open Until** times for each day, or mark specific days as **Closed** for delivery.

# 2. Own Delivery

If you use your own staff to deliver goods rather than relying on third-party platform drivers, you can manage them right from this page!

## Standard Delivery Configuration

Standard Delivery is your default method. It applies fixed rules for calculating your delivery costs and service area.

### To set up Standard Delivery:

1. Locate the **Default delivery** section.
2. Click the black **Edit** button. A panel will slide up from the bottom.
3. Fill in your default parameters:
   - **Min order \$:** The minimum cart subtotal a customer must reach before they are allowed to request delivery (e.g., `$15.00`).
   - **Radius (in min/km):** Your maximum delivery distance or estimated drive time (e.g., `20`).
   - **Delivery fee (\$):** The flat rate you charge for delivery (e.g., `$5.00`).
   - **Free delivery from:** An optional threshold to incentivize larger orders. If a customer spends this amount, their delivery fee is waived.

## Advanced Delivery Zones

Want to charge a \$5 fee for customers within 5 miles, but an \$8 fee for customers 10 miles away? **Delivery Zones** allow you to do exactly that.

### Enabling and Creating Zones

1. Scroll to the **Delivery Zones** section and toggle the switch to **ON**.
2. Click the **\+ Add radius** button.
3. Click the **Edit** button next to your newly created zone.
4. Define the specific rules for this tier (Name of the zone, specific radius, specific fee, and minimum order).
5. Repeat the process to create as many tiers as your business needs.

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** When Delivery Zones are enabled, they will override your Standard Delivery settings based on the customer's exact location.
>
> **To Delete a Zone:** Simply click the red **Trash Can** icon next to the zone you wish to remove.

### Enabling Your Fleet

1. Find the **Own Delivery** (or **On-demand delivery**) toggle and turn it **ON**.
2. Scroll down to **Enable delivery fleet**.
3. If this is your first time, click **Generate Code**.
4. The system will create a unique Driver Registration Code (e.g., `#YourStore1234`).

### Managing Your Drivers

- **Share the Code:** Tap directly on the generated code to instantly **Copy to Clipboard**. You can text or email this to your new drivers.
- **QR Code:** Click the **QR Code** button. Drivers can simply scan this from your screen using their driver app to automatically register under your store.
- **Manage Drivers:** Click the **Manage Drivers** button to view your active fleet, check who is currently online, and monitor their status.

> 📝 **NOTE:** By enabling your Delivery Fleet, the platform's standard driver network will be disabled for your orders. Your own drivers will be paid out according to the fees you set in your Delivery Zones.

## 5. National Shipping (Retail/Non-Restaurant Only)

_Note: This feature is only visible if your business type is set to something other than "Restaurant."_

If you sell physical goods that can be mailed, you can configure your shipping zones here.

1. Toggle **Enable national shipping** to **ON**.
2. A list of available shipping locations/regions will appear.
3. Simply check the boxes next to the regions or provinces you are willing to ship to.

## 6. Saving Your Changes

> 🛑 **CRITICAL STEP:** **Your changes are NOT saved automatically!**

Once you have configured your delivery settings perfectly, you must save them.

- Look for the **Floating Black "Save" Button** at the bottom right corner of your screen.
- Click **Save**.
- Wait for the pop-up dialogue confirming **"Changes saved: You have successfully updated delivery settings."** Click **Close**.

**Need to adjust Pickup or General settings?** Use the menu on the left side of your screen (or the top Navigation bar on mobile) to seamlessly switch between your **Delivery**, **Pickup**, and **General** Fulfillment Settings.
