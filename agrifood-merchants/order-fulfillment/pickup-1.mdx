The **Pickup** settings page allows you to control exactly how and where customers collect their orders. Whether you operate from a single storefront or manage a chain of locations, you can customize your availability and hours here.

## General Availability

At the top of the page, you will see a main status toggle:

- **Offering Pickups:** Turn this **ON** to allow customers to select "Pickup" at checkout.
- **Not Offering Pickups:** Turn this **OFF** to disable pickup orders entirely (e.g., if you are renovation-only or delivery-only for a period).

## Single vs. Multiple Locations

Local Pass supports businesses of all sizes. Use the **Enable multiple pickup locations** toggle to switch between modes.

### Single Location Mode

If you only have one physical store, keep "Enable multiple pickup locations" **OFF**. This simplifies your dashboard.

You will see your **Default Pickup Location** card, displaying:

1. **Location Name**
2. **Address**
3. **Location Hours:** The specific hours customers can pick up orders (these can differ from your general store opening hours).

**To Edit:** Click the **Menu icon** (three vertical dots) on the location card. This opens a dialogue where you can:

- Update the **Address**.
- Rename the **Location**.
- Modify the specific **Pickup Hours** for each day of the week.

### Multiple Locations Mode

If you have more than one pickup location, turn "Enable multiple pickup locations" **ON**.

- **Add New Location:** Click the **Add a new pickup location** button to register a new location. You will be asked to provide the address, name, and specific hours for that branch.
- **Manage List:** Your locations will appear in a list. You can edit or deactivate specific locations individually without affecting the others.

<Tip>
  Multiple pickup locations are effective when you have mobile pickups in various locations, e.g. Victoria on Tuesdays, Sooke on Fridays, Nanaimo on Sundays.
</Tip>

## How multiple locations work on customer side

When customer is at checkout and they select "Pickup" as an option to get their product, a slider with your pickup locations will appear sorted by distance relative to their current location. Pickup hours is another option they will be prompted to select. This allows you to see when to expect customers on pickup.
