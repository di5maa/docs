Understanding what your customers love—and where you can improve—is vital for growth on **Local Pass**. The **Reviews** section in your Merchant Dashboard allows you to monitor feedback from Local Pass customers and even display your existing reputation from Google.

## Viewing Your Feedback

On your main dashboard, you can browse through your most recent feedback to stay on top of customer sentiment.

- **History:** You can view a history of your last **100 reviews**.
- **Details:** Each review card displays the customer's written comments and their detailed score breakdown.

## The Local Pass Rating System

Unlike simple 5-star systems, Local Pass allows customers to rate specific aspects of their experience. This gives you granular data on exactly what your business is doing right.

A customer's **Overall Score** is calculated based on these four distinct categories:

1. **Atmosphere**
   - The vibe, decor, cleanliness, and comfort of your establishment.
2. **Food Quality**
   - The taste, presentation, and freshness of your products.
3. **Service**
   - The friendliness, knowledge, and speed of your staff.
4. **Value for Money**
   - Whether the price point matched the quality of the experience.

## Importing Google Reviews

If you are new to Local Pass but have an established presence on Google Maps, you don't have to start from zero! You can import your top reviews directly into your profile using your **Google Place ID**.

### How to Find Your Place ID

Your Place ID is a unique code that Google Maps uses to identify your specific store location.

1. Go to the official [**Google Place ID Finder**](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id) (or search "Google Place ID Finder" in your browser).
2. In the map search bar on that page, type your **Business Name**.
3. Click on your business when it appears in the list.
4. A pin will drop on the map with a tooltip. Look for the line that says: `Place ID: ChIJ...`
5. **Copy that code** (it is a long string of letters and numbers).

### Importing to Local Pass

Once you have your ID, follow these steps in your dashboard:

1. Navigate to the **Reviews** tab in your Local Pass Dashboard.
2. Click the **Import from Google** button.
3. A dialogue box will appear. **Paste** your Place ID into the text field.
4. Click **Fetch Reviews**.

<Warning>
  ⚠️ Important Limitation: Currently, the system will import your top 5 most relevant reviews from Google. We are actively working on expanding this feature to allow for larger imports in future updates
</Warning>
