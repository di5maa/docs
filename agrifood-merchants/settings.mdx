# Store Settings (General)

**Where to find it:** Settings → Store Settings → General

This is your main store profile page. Here you can update the core information customers see when they browse your store on Local Pass.

---

## What you can edit

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Store Name** | The public name of your business |
| **Tagline** | A short one-liner (max 140 characters) that captures what you're about |
| **About Business** | A longer description of your story, products, and values |
| **Business Category** | The type of business you run (e.g., Restaurant, Bakery, Farm) |
| **Cuisine Type** | Only shown for restaurants — select your cuisine style |
| **Email** | Your public contact email |
| **Website** | Your business website URL |
| **Phone** | Your contact phone number |

---

## Sales Model

Tell customers how they can buy from you.

**Restaurants:** Toggle **Dine-in** or **Takeout** on or off.

**All other businesses:** Toggle **Wholesale** or **Retail** on or off.

<Note>
  You can only have one sales model active at a time. Turning one on will keep the other at its current setting.
</Note>

---

## Business Attributes

These optional flags help customers find businesses that match their values:

- **Indigenous-owned** — Check this if your business is Indigenous-owned
- **Women-owned** — Check this if your business is women-owned
- **Ethnic Store** — Check this if your business represents a cultural heritage

---

## Using AI to write your Tagline or Description

Not sure what to write? Click the **Ask AI** button below the Tagline or About Business fields. A panel will open where AI can generate content based on your store profile. You can accept the suggestion and it will pre-fill the field.

---

## Saving your changes

When you're done making changes, tap the **Save** button in the top-right corner. A confirmation dialog will appear when your changes are saved successfully.
