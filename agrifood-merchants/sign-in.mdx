title: "Sign in"
---

When you visit the Local Pass Business portal, you'll land on the **Sign In** page. Here's how to access your account.

## Logging in with email

1. Click **Continue with email**
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **Login** — you'll be taken straight to your dashboard

## Forgot your password?

If you can't remember your password:

1. Click **Continue with email**
2. Enter your email address in the email field
3. Click **Forgot Password?**
4. Check your inbox for a password reset link

> Make sure you enter your email address **before** clicking Forgot Password, otherwise the reset cannot be sent.

## Don't have an account yet?

Click **Create Account** on the sign-in page to register as a new merchant. See the [Create Your Account](/account-signup) guide for the full walkthrough.
