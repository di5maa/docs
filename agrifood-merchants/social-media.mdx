---
title: "Social Media"
---

**Where to find it:** Settings → Social Media

Linking your social media accounts lets customers follow your latest updates, see behind-the-scenes content, and stay connected with your business beyond the Local Pass platform.

---

## Supported platforms

| Platform | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Facebook** | Your Facebook page URL |
| **Instagram** | Your Instagram profile URL |
| **YouTube** | Your YouTube channel URL |
| **TikTok** | Your TikTok profile URL |
| **WeChat** | Your WeChat ID or link |

---

## How to update your links

1. Enter the full URL for each platform you use
2. Leave any unused platforms blank
3. Tap **Save** in the top-right corner

---

## Tips

- Use the full URL including `https://` (e.g., `https://www.instagram.com/yourbusiness/`)
- If you update a link, the old one is replaced immediately after saving
- Social links appear on your public store profile so customers can tap directly through to your pages

<Note>
  You don't need to fill in all platforms. Only add the ones where your business is actually active — a blank field is better than a broken link.
</Note>
