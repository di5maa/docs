**Where to find it:** Settings → Support

The Support page is your central hub for account management, contract details, help resources, and platform information. Everything related to your account beyond your store profile lives here.

---

## Store Space (Quick Links)

A quick-access panel at the top links you to all the key settings pages:

| Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| Store settings | Edit store name, description, and contact info |
| Social media | Connect Facebook, Instagram, and more |
| Certifications | Upload food-safe and other certificates |
| Awards | Add business awards and recognition |
| Appearance | Upload logo and cover photo |
| Address | Update your business address |
| Store growth | Manage keywords and checkout offers |
| Dark / Light Mode | Switch the app colour theme (see below) |

---

## Dark Mode / Light Mode

Tap the **sun icon (☀)** to switch to Light Mode or the **moon icon (☾)** to switch to Dark Mode. Your preference is saved immediately.

<Note>
  This is an experimental feature. If you notice any display issues in Dark Mode, use the **Report Bug** link to let us know.
</Note>

---

## Account Information

Displays your current account details:

- **Email** — The email address linked to your Local Pass account
- **Phone** — Your registered phone number
- **Personal details** — Tap to open and edit your personal profile information

---

## Contract Details

Your contract with Local Pass is summarised here for quick reference:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Ends on** | The date your current contract expires |
| **Delivery fee %** | The platform commission rate on orders fulfilled by Local Pass drivers |
| **Pickup fee %** | The platform commission rate on pickup orders |
| **Own delivery fee %** | Your commission rate if you use your own drivers (shown as "Not offering" if not applicable) |

<Note>
  Contract terms are set during your onboarding. To discuss changes or renewals, contact the Local Pass team directly.
</Note>

## App Version

The current version of the app is displayed at the bottom of the support page (e.g., `1.0.15+106`).

Always include this version number when reporting a bug — it helps the support team identify exactly what build you are running.

## Contacting Support

If you are experiencing an issue that isn't covered in the documentation:

- **Email:** support@imlocal.ca
- **In-app:** Settings → Support → Contact Local Team
- **Bug reports:** Settings → Support → Report Bug

When reporting a bug, please include:
1. Your app version (shown on this page)
2. The device and operating system you are using
3. A description of what happened and what you expected to happen
4. Screenshots if possible
