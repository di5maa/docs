---
title: "Update address"
---

**Where to find it:** Settings → Address

Your business address is used by the platform to calculate delivery distances, show your location to customers, and configure pickup zones. Keeping this accurate is important for smooth order routing.

---

## How to update your address

1. Type your street address or business name in the search field
2. A list of matching addresses will appear below as you type
3. Tap the correct address from the list — it will be saved to your app session automatically
4. A green snackbar at the bottom of the screen will confirm **"Location saved"**
5. Tap **Update Address** to write the confirmed address to your store profile

<Tip>
  The search uses your current GPS location to prioritise nearby results. Make sure location access is enabled on your device for the best results.
</Tip>

---

## What each result shows

Each address suggestion in the list displays:

- **Name** — The place name or street address
- **Full address** — The complete formatted address
- **Category** — The type of location (e.g., food, retail)
- **Distance** — How far the location is from your current position

---

## Current address

Your currently saved address is displayed near the top of the page under **"Current address."** Check this to confirm your address is correct before making any changes.

---

## Why this matters

- Customers see your location on your store page
- The platform uses your address as the default **pickup point** for delivery orders
- Delivery zone calculations are based on drive time from this address
- Drivers are routed to this location to collect orders

<Warning>
  If your address is wrong, drivers may arrive at the incorrect location and deliveries will be delayed. Always double-check after updating.
</Warning>
