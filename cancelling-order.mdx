Cancellations should be a **last resort**. Every cancellation affects your acceptance rate score, and some carry heavier penalties than others. Only cancel if you genuinely cannot complete the delivery.

## How to Cancel

1. Tap the red **✖ (Cancel)** button on the left side of the navigation screen.
2. A cancellation panel slides up from the bottom.
3. Select a reason, optionally add a comment, then tap **Cancel Delivery**.

 

<Warning>
  The **Cancel Delivery** button stays greyed out until you've selected a valid reason. Some reasons require additional steps (like uploading a photo) before the button activates. 
</Warning>

## Cancellation Reasons

The available reasons depend on which stage of the delivery you're at.

### Stage 1 — At or En Route to Pickup

| Reason | Notes |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Excessive long wait at pickup** | The app checks how long you've waited vs. the estimated ready time. If you haven't waited long enough, the option is blocked. |
| **Restaurant is closed** | No penalty applied. |
| **Mechanical issues** | Photo proof required. |
| **Car accident** | Photo proof required. |
| **Other reasons** | Standard cancellation. |

### Stage 2 — En Route to or At Drop-off

| Reason | Notes |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Can't find customer \| Failed delivery** | Photo proof required AND the app checks your distance to the customer's address. |
| **Mechanical issues** | Photo proof required. |
| **Car accident** | Photo proof required. |
| **Cancel anyway without disclosing the reason** | Highest penalty. A warning is shown before you can confirm. |

 

<Note>
  If you're en route to drop-off, a note at the top of the cancellation screen will advise you to contact Customer Support instead. You can tap **Contact Support** to open a live chat and resolve the issue without a formal cancellation where possible. 
</Note>

## Uploading Photo Proof

Some cancellation reasons require a photo before the **Cancel Delivery** button activates:

1. After selecting a qualifying reason (e.g., _"Can't find customer"_), a **Photo Proof** section appears.
2. Tap **Attach File** and take a photo (e.g., a photo of the customer's door showing no one is home, or your vehicle if there's a mechanical issue).
3. Tap **Confirm & Upload** to upload the photo.
4. Once uploaded, the **Cancel Delivery** button activates.

 

<Tip>
  You can tap **Reupload** if the first photo wasn't clear enough. 
</Tip>

## Penalty Guide

| Cancellation Reason | Penalty |
| :-- | :-- |
| Restaurant is closed | **0 points** |
| Excessive long wait (wait time validated) | **0 points** |
| Can't find customer \+ photo proof uploaded | **0 points** |
| Standard cancellation (mechanical, other) | **–5 points** |
| Cancel without disclosing reason | **–50 points** |

## Additional Comments

You can type notes in the **Additional Comments** field for any cancellation. This is optional but helps our support team understand what happened, especially for unusual situations.

## After Cancellation

Once you confirm the cancellation, the app:

1. Updates the order status to **Cancelled**.
2. Records the cancellation reason and any uploaded photo.
3. Attempts to reassign the order to another driver (for Stage 1 cancellations).
4. Sends you to a loading screen and then back to the **Driver Mode** lobby.

If the customer is eligible for a refund, it's processed automatically — you don't need to do anything.
