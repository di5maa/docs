---
title: "Activity history"
---

The Activity History page keeps a record of all your past rides and food orders on Local Pass. You can review trip details, order summaries, and costs at any time.

---

## Getting to Activity History

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **Personal Account**, tap **Activity history**.

Alternatively, tap the **Trips** icon in the bottom navigation bar to go directly to your activity.

---

## Switching Between Trips and Eats

If your account has driver access, you will see two tabs at the top of the page:

- **Trips** — shows your ride history
- **Eats** — shows your food order history (this is the default view)

Tap the tab you want to view. The selected tab is highlighted with the Local Pass green colour.

<Note>
  If you do not see the tab switcher, your account is not registered as a driver and only your Eats (order) history will be shown.
</Note>

---

## Viewing Your Order History (Eats)

Each order in the list shows:

- **Order number** — the unique reference code for the order
- **Fulfilment type** — whether the order was for Delivery, Pickup, or Shipping
- **Status** — the current status of the order (for example, Completed or Cancelled)
- **Prepared by** — the name of the restaurant or vendor
- **Date and time** — when the order was placed
- **Cost** — the amount paid (minus any Local Coins redeemed)
- **Items in your cart** — a scrollable list of the items you ordered

Tap any order to open the full order details page.

---

## Viewing Your Ride History (Trips)

Each ride in the list shows:

- **Trip status** — for example, Completed or Cancelled
- **Trip code** — the unique reference for that trip
- **Flag icon** — appears if the ride was a street hail (flag down)
- **Pickup location** — where you were picked up, with the city shown below
- **Drop-off location** — your destination (not shown for street hails)
- **Date and time** — when the trip ended
- **Cost** — the fare for the trip
- **Distance** — how far you travelled in km

Tap any ride to open the full trip details page.

## Order Details Page

Tapping an order opens a detailed summary that includes:

### Customer Information

Your name, email address, and the date and time of the order.

### Price Breakdown

A full breakdown of what you paid, including:

- Subtotal
- Tax
- Platform fee
- Plastic bag fee (if applicable)
- Shipping cost (if applicable)
- Driver tip (for delivery orders)
- Long distance charge (if applicable)
- Delivery fee (for delivery orders)
- Promotional discount (if a promo code was used)
- Redeemed Local Coins (if any were used)
- **Total paid**

### Billing Address

The address on file for billing at the time of the order.

### Fulfilment Details

- For **Delivery** orders: the vendor's address, the delivery address, and a timeline of status updates (order placed, preparing, out for delivery, etc.)
- For **Pickup** orders: the pickup location name, address, scheduled pickup time, and pickup location business hours
- For **Shipping** orders: the shipping address and status history

### Items Ordered

A list of every item in your order, including:

- Item image
- Item name
- Price per item
- Quantity
- Any add-ons or extras selected, and their additional cost

### Cancelled Orders

If an order was cancelled, a red banner will appear at the top of the order summary confirming that the order has been refunded.

### Get Help with an Order

At the bottom of every order details page, there is a **"Have issues with your order? Contact us"** link. Tap this to open a support chat for that specific order.

## Trip Details Page

Tapping a ride opens a detailed trip summary that includes:

### Your Profile

Your name and email, confirming which account took the trip.

### Trip Summary Chips

Badges at the top of the card showing:

- The date and time the trip ended
- The fare amount
- The total duration in minutes
- The total distance in km

### Street Hail Notice

If the ride was a street hail, a yellow banner will appear explaining that the trip was flagged down on the street, so destination details may not be recorded.

### Business Trip

If the trip was marked as a business trip, the company name and billing address will be displayed.

### Driver Information

- Driver's profile photo
- Driver's first name with a verified badge
- Driver's rating (shown as a star and a number)
- A "legend" badge if the driver has an exceptionally high rating
- Vehicle make and model
- Vehicle number
- License plate number
- A vehicle icon representing the vehicle type (standard, van, or accessible van)

### Route

A visual summary showing your pickup location and drop-off location with city names.

### Cancellation Details

If the trip was cancelled, a red section will appear showing:

- Who cancelled (the user or the driver)
- The short reason for cancellation
- Any additional comments

### Get Help with a Trip

At the bottom of every trip details page, there is a **"Have issues with your trip? Contact us"** link. Tap this to open a support chat for that specific trip.

---

## Copying Your Order or Trip ID

On the trip and order details pages, your order or trip code is displayed in the top right corner of the screen header. Tap it to copy the ID to your clipboard. A brief message will confirm it has been copied.

This is useful when contacting support, as it helps the team find your order or trip quickly.
