A subscription box (farms often call it a CSA share) is a box from a local business that comes every week or every two weeks, like vegetables from a farm or bread from a bakery. Each box is packed for you and ready on your pickup or delivery day.

You subscribe on **localpass.ca**. When the Local Pass app shows a box, it sends you to localpass.ca to sign up.

## Find a box

Boxes are listed under **Subscriptions & Farm Boxes** in the shop, and on each business's store page. A box's page shows:

- The price of a box, and the season price if the business offers one.
- How often it comes, its size, and what's usually in it (or this week's list).
- The date of the next box, when changes close for it, and how many spots are left.
- Pickup spots and delivery towns.
- What you can change once you subscribe, and the business's policies.

Press **Subscribe**. If the box is full, the button reads **Join the waitlist**: you get an email as soon as a spot opens.

## Sign up

You need a Local Pass account. Press **Sign in to subscribe** if you're not signed in. Then choose:

1. **Which season**, if the box has more than one.
2. **How often**: **Every week** or **Every two weeks**, if the business offers both.
3. **How do you want your box?** **Pick it up** at one of the business's pickup spots, or **Home delivery** if it delivers. For delivery, your address must be in one of its delivery towns and include your house number. The business delivers it itself, on your delivery day, in its delivery window.
4. **How you'll pay**: see below.
5. Read the **Rules** and the business's **Subscription terms**. If it has terms, tick "I've read [business]'s subscription terms and I agree to them."
6. Add your card and press **Save card and subscribe** (paying per box) or **Pay [amount] and subscribe** (paying for the season).

You'll see your first box date and where to get it. A welcome email follows, with any message from the business.

## Paying per box or for the season

**Pay per box**: your card is saved and charged automatically for each box when changes close, a few days before your pickup or delivery day. You're charged only for the boxes you get. Skip any week before changes close and you're not charged for it.

**Pay for the season**: one payment today covers every box left in the season. The business may give a discount (shown as **Save X%**). You can't skip a week, but you can take any box as Local Coins instead. Your card is saved for extras you add later.

Each box costs:

| Line | |
| --- | --- |
| **Box** | The business's price for one box (less the season discount, if you pay for the season). |
| **Delivery** | The business's delivery fee, if you chose home delivery. |
| **Service fee** | 5% of the box (never more than $6), plus $0.30. It goes to Local Pass. |
| **Taxes** | GST and PST on the service fee, and GST on the box if the business charges it. |

The signup page adds these up for you before you pay. Your card is saved securely with Stripe.

<Note>
You never pay more than the price you signed up at. If the business raises its price later, you keep yours. If it lowers it, you pay the lower price.
</Note>

## Invited by a business?

If a farm or shop you already get boxes from moves its subscriptions to Local Pass, it may email you an invitation. The link in it opens signup with your box, how often and pickup already chosen. If it's holding your spot, the page says "your spot is held until" a date. Sign in or create a free account and finish signing up.

Don't want these emails? Press **Stop these emails** in the invitation. The business won't invite you again, and you can still join on your own.
