title: "Referrals"
---

The Referrals page lets you share Local Pass with friends and family. When someone signs up using your unique referral link, both you and your friend benefit.

---

## Getting to the Referrals Page

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **Personal Account**, tap **Share with a friend**.

---

## How Referrals Work

Every Local Pass account has a unique referral code. When you share your referral link and a friend creates a new account through it:

- Your friend receives **3 free deliveries** as a welcome bonus.
- You receive \$10 reward to your Local Pass Wallet as a thank-you for the referral.

<Tip>
  The more friends you refer, the more rewards you can earn. Share your link as widely as you like.
</Tip>

---

## Sharing Your Referral Link

On the Referrals page, you will see several options for sharing your unique link.

### Share via Message or Social Media

1. Tap the **share button** or a social sharing option shown on the page.
2. Choose how you want to share: via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or any other app on your device.
3. Your referral link will be included automatically in the message.

### Share via Instagram Stories

The Referrals page includes pre-designed Instagram Story templates you can use to share your referral on Instagram. Tap the template you like to open it and share it to your story.

### Copy Your Referral Link

If you prefer to copy the link manually:

1. Tap the **copy** option on the Referrals page.
2. Your referral link is copied to your clipboard.
3. Paste it into any message, post, or email.

---

## Your Referral Code

Your unique referral code is displayed on the Referrals page. The base link for your referral is:

```text
https://app.localpass.ca/signin/[your-referral-code]
```

When a friend opens this link and creates a new account, the referral is tracked automatically.

---

## Checking Your Referral Rewards

Referral rewards are credited to your account automatically. To see your current balance and rewards, visit the Wallet or Rewards section of the app.

---

## Terms for Referrals

- The friend you refer must be a **new user** who has never had a Local Pass account before.
- The referral reward is granted once the new user successfully creates an account through your link.
- Referral rewards are subject to Local Pass terms and conditions.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My friend signed up but I did not get my reward — what should I do?** Make sure your friend used your referral link when creating their account. If you believe there is an error, contact Local Pass support through **Settings → Contact Local Pass team**.

**Can I use someone else's referral link when creating my account?** Yes. If you were referred by a friend, open their referral link before creating your account to claim your 3 free deliveries.

**Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?** No. You can share your referral link with as many people as you like.
