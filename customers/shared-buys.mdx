title: "Shared buys"
description: "Buy a share of a whole animal from a local farm, or food by the amount, on localpass.ca. Split it with friends in a private group or join a community share, choose your cuts, follow your group and pay for just your part."
---

A shared buy lets you buy straight from a farm or a fisher in bigger amounts, and share it. You can buy:

- **A share of an animal**: a half, a quarter or an eighth of beef, half a pig, a whole lamb, a bird or a box of birds, or any amount in pounds, depending on what the farm offers.
- **Food by the amount**: vegetables, fruit, fish, eggs, cheese, flour and more, by the lb, kg, dozen, bunch or piece. You choose how much you want.

Don't need that much? Split it. Start a **Private group** with friends and family, or join a **Community share** someone else started. Everyone pays for their own part.

Shared buys are on **localpass.ca**. You'll find them on a farm's store page, under its pre-orders.

## How a shared buy works

1. The farm opens the shared buy and says when orders close.
2. You buy your share, or your part of a split, and pay for it.
3. Orders close. For an animal, it goes to the processor on the processing day.
4. If your share has a cut sheet, you choose how it's cut before choices close.
5. The farm sets the pickup or delivery day and lets you know.
6. If your share is priced by weight, your bill is settled on the real weight.
7. You pick up your share, or it's delivered.

The page shows what's left right now, for example "75 lb left to join in 2 community shares", when orders close, and the farm's updates.

## Choose your share

The shared buy's page has up to three tabs:

| Animal | Food by the amount | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy a share** | **Order for yourself** | It's all yours, in your own box. |
| **Start a split** | **Start a split** | Buy a share (or an amount), keep your part, and others pay for the rest. |
| **Join a split** | **Join a split** | Take part of a share someone else started. |

**Buy a share:** pick a size. Each one says about how much meat it holds and its price. "Need more than one?" lets you take more. If the farm sells any amount, choose how many pounds. Some farms offer extras, such as liver, heart or tongue, which you add before you pay.

**Order for yourself:** choose how much with **−** and **+**, or type it. The amount goes up in the farm's steps. The price is on the button, for example **Order 10 lb · $28.00**.

On a phone, the button stays at the bottom of the screen: **Continue**, then pay.

## Start a split

On **Start a split**:

1. Choose the share (or how much in all).
2. Under **Your part**, choose how much is yours. You see what you pay now.
3. Under **Who can join?**, choose:
   - **Private group**: for friends and family. Only people you send your link to can join.
   - **Community share**: your split is public. Anyone in the community can join and take part of your share. You're the organizer, and your badge says **Community champion**.
4. For a community share, choose your town. Others see your first name and town, never your address.
5. Pay for your part.

Then share your link with the people you want in your group.

**Private group:** your friends have until a set day to pay for their part. "Then you pay for what's left." You collect the whole share at pickup and share it out.

**Community share:** you pay your part now. Before you pay, the page tells you what happens if nobody takes the rest by the deadline. Depending on the farm, either you still get your part, or you get every cent back. You can't end a community share early, but you can take it off the list so nobody new can join.

Extras can't go in a community share.

### Group price

Some farms offer a group price: "In a split, everyone pays 10% less once a second person joins." If you start a split, you pay the regular price at first. When someone joins, you get the difference back: "You pay $45.00 now. When someone joins, you get $4.50 back."

## Join a split

**From a friend's link:** the link opens their private group. Choose how much you want (from 1 lb, in half-pound steps, or one piece for birds and boxes), or **Take the rest**. Enter your name, email and phone, and pay for your part. You don't need a Local Pass account. The group sees only your first name and your amount.

**A community share:** open **Join a split** on the shared buy's page. **Community shares you can join** lists the open ones, with the organizer's first name, town and how much is left. Filter by town. Pick one, then choose:

- how much you want, from the farm's smallest part;
- **How you get your bag**: **I'll pick it up myself**, or the organizer collects it for you (then you get it from them; sort out when in the group posts). When the farm delivers, every bag goes to the organizer.
- whether to show your profile photo in the group (if you're signed in). If you say no, others see your initials.

Everyone in a community share gets their own bag with their name on it.

## Your group

Your group has its own page. Open it from your order page (**Open group**), from **Your group** on the shared buy's page, or from **Coming up** on your **Activity** page, where the row shows the group, for example "Kyla's group · 2 people".

The group page has sections for **Your part**, **Group**, **Posts**, **Pickup** and **Farm**:

- **Your part**: what you have, what you've paid, and anything to do. Once you've paid and there's nothing left to do, you see **You're all set**, with your pickup or delivery day.
- **Group**: the share, how full it is, and who's in it. Members show by first name only.
- **Posts**: short notes to the group, each with a kind: **Pickup plan**, **I can collect**, **Cut preferences** (animals only), **Question for the group** or **Paid / sorted**. From 5 posts, the page shows the latest 3, and **See all** opens every post.
- **Pickup**: who collects, the dates and the place.
- **Farm**: the farm's updates and the story of the animal (or the boat, for fish).

On a phone, a bar at the bottom shows the one thing to do now, such as paying, sharing the split, choosing your cuts or getting directions.

### If you're the organizer

Press **Manage** at the top of the group page:

- **Post an update** and **Pickup plan**.
- **Who collects** (private groups).
- **Rename the group**.
- **New link**: the old link stops working.
- **People**: remove someone who hasn't paid.
- **I'll pay for what's left now** (private groups): you're billed for the rest, and nobody else can join.
- **Take off the community list** (community shares): nobody new finds it on the farm's page. People with your link can still join until it closes. It can't go back on the list.

### New posts and the bell

When you're signed in and have a shared-buy order, a bell in the site header shows what's new in your groups: other members' posts and news from the farm. Press it and pick a group to open it. "New" badges also show on your order page, on **Your group** and on your **Activity** rows. Opening the group page marks it read.

### Emails about your group

- **Posts:** when someone else posts a **Pickup plan**, a **Question for the group** or **Cut preferences**, you get an email, at most one a day per group, with the new posts since your last email.
- **The farm's updates:** once you've paid, you get the farm's notes and big news (the processing day, and when it's going ahead) by email, at most 3 a day.

To stop these, press **Stop these updates** in any of these emails. Your receipts, bills and pickup dates still come.

## Choosing your cuts

If the farm asks for a cut sheet, your order page shows **Choose your cuts**. You get an email when you pay, and a reminder before choices close.

Your sheet starts with the farm's usual cuts. Change only what you care about. What you're asked depends on your share:

| Your share | You choose |
| --- | --- |
| **Half or whole** (and every bird) | How each part is cut. |
| **Quarter** | How your packs are made. A quarter shares one side with another buyer, so it's cut the farm's usual way. |
| **Less than a quarter** | Nothing. You get the farm's usual cuts. |

- Every section starts closed and shows its current picks. Tap a part on the drawing, or open a section, to change it. Each part has its own choice.
- **About** beside a part or a choice opens three short lines: what it is, how to cook it, and why pick it.
- At the end, some farms ask for a note for the butcher.
- Press **Save my cuts** (or **Save my choices** for packing). Or press **Keep the usual cuts** (**Keep the usual packing** for a quarter).

Your choices don't change the price. You can change them until choices close. If you don't choose by then, you get the farm's usual cuts.

**On a split,** the share is cut once, and everyone gets their part of every cut: "What the half holds: about 75 lb, cut once your way. Your part: about 37.5 lb, half of every cut." The organizer chooses the cuts. Everyone else in the split sees them but can't change them. Want something? Post it in the group under **Cut preferences**.

If the farm fills in or changes your sheet for you, you get an email saying what changed.

## Paying

You pay by card on a secure checkout page, straight to the farm. Local Pass never moves money between buyers: everyone pays for their own part.

- **In full:** you pay for your share when you buy it.
- **A deposit:** on some shares, you pay a deposit now and get a bill for the rest later. The page says when.
- **Splits:** everyone pays for their own part in full when they join.
- **Food by the amount:** always paid in full when you order.

### Final weight

Some shares are priced by weight. You pay for the expected weight now. After processing, the farm enters the real weight:

- if it weighs more, you get a bill for the difference;
- if it weighs less, you get the difference back.

In a split, everyone gets their own bill or refund, by their part.

If the farm cancels a split or a shared buy, everyone gets a full refund, to the card they paid with.

## Changing or cancelling

Each shared buy shows the farm's policies, including cancelling. To change your order, contact the farm: reply to any email about your order.

## Find your order again

- **Your order page:** the link in your receipt email opens it. No account needed.
- **Signed in:** your shared buy shows under **Coming up** on your **Activity** page.
