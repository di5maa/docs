---
title: "Deleting your account"
---

If you want to permanently remove your Local Pass account, you can do so from the Settings page. This action is irreversible, so please read this guide carefully before proceeding.

---

## Before You Delete

Please be aware of the following before deleting your account:

- **All your data will be permanently deleted.** This includes your order history, ride history, saved addresses, favourite vendors, Local Coins balance, and profile information.
- **Deletion cannot be undone.** Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered.
- **Outstanding issues should be resolved first.** If you have pending orders, active trips, or unresolved support cases, please resolve them before deleting your account.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. There is no way to recover your account, history, or Local Coins balance after deletion.
</Warning>

---

## How to Delete Your Account

### Step 1 — Open Settings

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.

### Step 2 — Find the Danger Zone

1. Scroll down to the **Support** section.
2. Look for the **Danger Zone** block (shown with a red label).
3. Tap **Delete account**.

### Step 3 — Confirm Your Identity

A confirmation dialog will appear with the following message:

> _"Delete Account? All your data, orders, and history will be permanently removed."_

To confirm you want to proceed, you must type the following phrase exactly into the text field:

```text
DELETE MY ACCOUNT
```

The text must be typed in **capital letters**. The delete button will remain grey and cannot be tapped until the exact phrase is entered.

### Step 4 — Tap DELETE

Once you have typed `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` correctly:

1. The red **D E L E T E** button will become active.
2. Tap it to confirm the deletion.

### Step 5 — Account Removed

Your account will be deleted and you will be signed out of the app automatically.

---

## Changing Your Mind

If you decide not to delete your account:

- Tap **CANCEL** at the bottom of the confirmation dialog at any time before tapping DELETE.
- The dialog will close and your account will remain unchanged.

---

## After Deletion

Once your account is deleted:

- You will no longer be able to sign in with your previous email or Google account.
- Any Local Coins or rewards credits in your account will be lost.
- Your payment methods stored in the Wallet will be removed.
- Your order and ride history will no longer be accessible.

---

## Alternatives to Deleting

If you are thinking about deleting your account because of a specific issue, there may be a better solution:

| Issue | Alternative |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy concern | Review your data settings or contact support |
| Too many notifications | Adjust notification settings on your device |
| Issue with an order or ride | Contact support through **Settings → Contact Local Pass team** |
| Switching devices | Simply sign in on your new device — you do not need to delete your account |
| Taking a break from the app | You can simply stop using the app — your account will remain inactive until you return |

---

## Contact Support Before Deleting

If you are unsure or want to discuss your situation with the Local Pass team before deleting your account, reach out via:

- **Email:** [support@imlocal.ca](mailto:support@imlocal.ca)
- **In-app:** Settings → Contact Local Pass team
