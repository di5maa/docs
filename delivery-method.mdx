---
title: "Delivery method"
---

Your delivery method tells Local Pass what type of transport you're using. This affects which orders you're matched with, so make sure it's set correctly before going online.

## How to Update Your Delivery Method

1. Open your **Driver Profile**.
2. Tap **Delivery Method** or the relevant settings section.
3. A panel slides up with three options — toggle on the one that applies to you.
4. Tap **Save** to confirm your choice.

## Available Methods

| Method | When to Use |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Vehicle Delivery** | You're delivering by car, truck, or SUV |
| **Bike / Scooter Delivery** | You're delivering by bicycle, e-bike, or scooter |
| **Foot Delivery** | You're completing deliveries on foot |

<Note>
  Only one method can be active at a time. Switching to a new method resets the others back to your previously saved settings, so there's no risk of accidentally having two active at once. 
</Note>

Your delivery method determines the distance radius and order types you'll be matched with, so update it any time you change how you're working.
