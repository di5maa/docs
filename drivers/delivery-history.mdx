title: "Delivery history"
---

The **Activity History** screen shows a record of every delivery and ride you've completed or cancelled on Local Pass.

## How to Access

Go to **Driver Profile → Activity History** (or tap the **Trips** icon in the bottom navigation bar).

## Switching Between Deliveries and Rides

If your account is set up for both delivery and rideshare, a toggle at the top lets you switch between:

- **Deliveries** — food and marketplace delivery history
- **Rides** — passenger trip history

If your account is delivery-only, the list shows deliveries automatically with no toggle needed.

## What Each Entry Shows

Each item in the list shows:

- **Status badge** — Completed (dark) or Cancelled (red)
- **Order ID** — the unique identifier for the order
- **Restaurant / Pickup location** — where the order came from
- **Date and time** — when the delivery was completed or cancelled
- **Delivery Fee** — your base pay for that order
- **Driver Tip** — the tip the customer added

Tap any entry to open the **Activity Details** screen.

## Activity Details

The details screen gives you a complete breakdown of a single delivery or ride.

### For Deliveries

- **Delivery fee** and **tips** earned
- **Pickup location** — restaurant name and address
- **Timeline** — when the order was accepted and when it was delivered (or cancelled)
- **Customer rating** for that delivery (if the customer left one), including:
  - Overall star rating
  - What you did well (quick delivery, friendly, followed instructions, communication)
  - Things to improve

### For Rides

- **Fare** earned
- **Trip duration** and **distance** in km
- **Date and time**
- **Pickup and drop-off locations**
- **Customer rating** including sub-scores for punctuality, friendliness, clean car, and safety
- **Cancellation reason** if the trip was cancelled

### Copying the Order ID

Tap the **Order ID pill** in the top right of the details screen to copy it to your clipboard. A snackbar confirms the copy. This is useful when contacting support about a specific order.

## Filtering Rides by Date

On the Rides tab, you can filter your history by a date range using the **Start Date** and **End Date** date pickers. Only rides that fall within the selected range will appear.

## Reporting an Issue

At the bottom of every Activity Details screen, there is a **support link**:

> _"Have issues with your delivery? Contact support."_

Tap it to open a live chat with Local Pass support, pre-filled with the order ID and status so the team can help you quickly.
