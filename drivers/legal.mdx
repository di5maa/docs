Before you can complete registration, you must read and accept the Local Pass Driver Terms & Conditions. This agreement sets out the rules of operating on the platform, what Local Pass provides, and what's expected from you as a driver.

## How to Accept

The Terms & Conditions appear as a sheet that slides up from the bottom of the registration screen. You can open them by tapping the **Terms & Conditions** link on the sign-up screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agreement">
    Tap the **Terms & Conditions** link during registration. The agreement sheet opens automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the agreement">
    Scroll through the full text inside the box. Take your time — this is a legal agreement. You can scroll up and down freely within the text area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or Decline">
    Two buttons sit at the bottom of the sheet:

    - **Accept** — saves your agreement, closes the sheet, and enables the **Confirm** button on the registration screen. A confirmation message appears at the bottom of the screen briefly.
    - **Decline** — closes the sheet without saving. You cannot register without accepting the agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The agreement is available in **English, Spanish, Ukrainian, Russian, Chinese, and Hindi**. Your phone's language setting determines which version is shown automatically. If you switch languages using the language selector on the registration screen, the agreement will display in your chosen language.
</Note>

## Key Points in Plain Language

Below is a summary of the most important sections of the agreement. This is not a substitute for reading the full document — it's a quick reference for drivers.

### Your Status as a Driver

You work as an **independent contractor**, not as an employee of Local Pass. This means:

- You choose your own hours and can log off at any time
- Local Pass does not provide employment insurance, health benefits, or pension contributions
- You are responsible for filing your own taxes, including reporting income from deliveries
- You may be required to register for GST/HST depending on your annual earnings

### Insurance

- You must hold a **valid driver's licence** and current vehicle registration for any vehicle you use on the platform
- Standard personal auto insurance policies typically **do not cover commercial use** — you are responsible for obtaining the appropriate endorsement or policy
- Local Pass does **not** provide primary vehicle insurance during trips
- Foot and bicycle couriers are responsible for any liability coverage applicable in their area

### Your Responsibilities on the Platform

- Maintain an **acceptance rate of at least 80%** to remain in good standing
- Do not share your account credentials or let anyone else use your profile to accept orders
- Treat customers, restaurant staff, and other drivers respectfully at all times
- Provide accurate information during registration and keep your details up to date

### Location Data

By accepting the agreement, you confirm that you understand and consent to the following:

- Local Pass tracks your location **continuously while you are on shift**, including when the app is running in the background
- Your location is used to match you with nearby orders and to show customers a live map of your progress
- Location history may be retained for dispute resolution and earnings verification
- You can stop location tracking at any time by toggling your shift off

### Earnings and Wallet

- A **\$50 welcome credit** is added to your in-app wallet when your account is created
- Earnings from completed orders are credited to your wallet after each delivery
- Tips shown at the time of the offer reflect what the customer set before ordering. Additional tips added after delivery are processed separately and credited within 48 hours
- Local Pass may deduct amounts from your wallet for confirmed fraud, chargebacks, or damages following a dispute process

### Account Suspension and Termination

- Local Pass can suspend or permanently terminate your account for serious violations including fraud, harassment, repeated cancellations, or uploading false documents
- You can appeal a suspension within **14 days** of receiving notice by contacting support
- Any remaining wallet balance is paid out after the standard dispute hold period on termination

### Disputes

- Disputes between you and Local Pass are resolved through **binding arbitration**, not court action
- You waive the right to participate in class-action lawsuits
- Small claims court remains an option for claims within the applicable monetary limit in your province
- The agreement is governed by the laws of the **province you registered in**

## If the Agreement Is Updated

Local Pass occasionally updates the Terms & Conditions. When a significant change is made, you may be asked to review and re-accept the updated agreement when you next sign in. You cannot continue using the platform until you accept the new version.

<Warning>
  Declining the Terms & Conditions means you cannot create or use a Local Pass driver account. There is no way to bypass this step.
</Warning>
