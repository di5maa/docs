---
title: "Reviews"
---

After every completed delivery or ride, customers can leave a rating and written feedback. Your ratings directly reflect the quality of your service and are visible to you in the app.

## How to View Your Ratings

Go to **Driver Profile → Reviews** (or navigate to the Reviews screen from your profile).

The screen shows your last **100 ratings**, split by:

- **Passenger Trips** — ratings from rideshare customers
- **Deliveries** — ratings from delivery customers

Each section displays your **average rating** calculated across all entries in that category.

## Delivery Ratings

For deliveries, customers rate you with a star score and indicate specific things they liked or didn't like:

### What You Did Well

Customers can highlight positive attributes:

- **Quick delivery** — you arrived fast
- **Friendly** — you were kind and professional
- **Good communication** — you kept the customer informed
- **Followed instructions** — you respected their delivery notes

### Things to Improve

Customers can also flag areas where you fell short, using the same categories in reverse.

### Comment

Customers may also leave a written comment. This appears below the thumbs-up / thumbs-down breakdown.

## Ride Ratings

For rideshare trips, customers rate you on four specific categories:

| Category | What It Measures |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Punctuality** | Did you arrive on time? |
| **Friendliness** | Were you professional and welcoming? |
| **Clean Car** | Was your vehicle clean and tidy? |
| **Safety** | Did you drive safely? |

An **Overall** score combines all four into a single number.

Customers can also leave an additional written comment.

## How Ratings Affect You

Your average rating is visible on your driver profile and is a key factor in your standing on the platform. Consistently low ratings may result in reduced order matching or a review of your account.

<Tip>
  To improve your ratings: follow delivery instructions carefully, keep your vehicle clean, communicate proactively if there's a delay, and be friendly at every handoff. 
</Tip>

## What Each Review Entry Shows

In the reviews list, each entry displays:

- **Order or Trip ID** — links to the specific job
- **Type** — Delivery or Taxi Ride
- **Date** — when the review was posted
- **Overall star rating**
- Specific feedback (thumbs-up attributes, thumbs-down attributes, written comment)
