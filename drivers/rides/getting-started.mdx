title: "Getting Started with Rides"
description: "Local Pass drivers can do delivery, rides, or both — here's how the two modes work and how to switch between them."
---

Local Pass has two separate driving modes: **Deliveries** and **Rides**. You can do either one, or both — they're controlled by two completely independent switches, and nothing links them together.

## Choosing a mode

From your driver home screen, you'll see **"What would you like to do today?"** with two options:

- **Deliveries** — food and package delivery. See the [Delivery](/drivers/getting-started-1) section.
- **Rides** — passenger trips, covered by this section.

Tap **Select** on either one to go to that mode's own online/offline screen.

<Warning>
  **These are two independent on-duty states, not one shared "online" status.** Going online for Rides does not put you on a delivery shift, and starting a delivery shift does not make you available for ride requests. If you want to do both, you need to turn each one on separately — check both screens if you're not seeing the requests you expect.
</Warning>

## Going online for Rides

On the Rides screen, tap **START SHIFT**. The button relabels itself to **COMPLETE SHIFT** once you're online, so the same button takes you offline again when you tap it a second time.

<Warning>
  **You can't go online for Rides without completing a pre-trip vehicle check first.** If you haven't completed one, tapping Start Shift shows: _"Please complete mandatory pre-trip vehicle check."_ This gate is specific to Rides — starting a delivery shift doesn't require it. See [Vehicle](/vehicle) for what the inspection covers.
</Warning>

## Going online for Deliveries

On the Deliveries screen, the same **START SHIFT** / **COMPLETE SHIFT** button applies — but instead of a vehicle check, you'll be shown a location-sharing disclosure to accept. Unlike the vehicle check (which stays completed until your next inspection is due), the location disclosure is shown **every time** you start a shift.

## What's actually different between the two modes

Beyond the toggle itself, Rides and Deliveries are genuinely different experiences once you're online — not just a different queue of jobs:

|  | Deliveries | Rides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you get matched | Accept or decline a specific job | Bid on a fare — see [Fare Bidding](/drivers/rides/fare-bidding) |
| Vehicle requirement | None beyond your delivery method | Mandatory pre-trip inspection each time you go online |
| Passenger/customer info | Full details on the order | First name only — last name is intentionally withheld |
| Safety tools | Standard support chat | A dedicated in-trip Safety toolkit (911 shortcut, incident reporting) |

The rest of this section covers what's specific to Rides. Everything about payouts, cash-out minimums, and your account applies the same way to both modes unless a page says otherwise.
