Before you can take your first order, your account needs to be verified. You'll need to upload three documents through the **Documents Verification** section of the app.

## What You Need to Upload

| Document | What It Is |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Driver's License / Government ID** | Your valid government-issued photo ID |
| **RCC (Registrar of Companies Certificate)** | Company or sole proprietor registration document |
| **Vehicle Insurance** | Proof of valid vehicle insurance |

## How to Upload

1. Open the app and navigate to **Driver Profile → Documents**.
2. You'll see three separate upload sections — one for each document.
3. Tap **Attach File** in each section and follow the on-screen prompts.
4. Once all three are uploaded, tap **Save & Finish**.

<Note>
  Your documents are reviewed within **24–48 hours**. You'll get a notification once your account is verified and ready to take orders.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Not ready to finish? Tap Finish Later at any time — your progress is saved automatically and you can return whenever you're ready.
</Tip>

## Document Upload Tips

- Make sure photos are **clear and well-lit** — blurry or dark images will delay your review.
- All edges of the document should be **fully visible** in the photo.
- Insurance documents must be **current and not expired**.
- If a document is rejected, you'll be notified and can re-upload from the same screen.

## After Verification

Once approved, your driver profile is unlocked and you can:

- Set up your vehicle profile
- Schedule shifts
- Go online and start accepting delivery requests
