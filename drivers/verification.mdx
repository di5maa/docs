title: "Account verification"
description: "Upload your required documents to get verified and start taking deliveries on Local Pass."
---

Before you can take your first order, your account needs to be verified. You'll upload your documents through the **Documents Verification** section of the app.

## What You Need to Upload

| Document | What It Is |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Driver's License** | Your valid government-issued driver's licence |
| **Vehicle Insurance** | Proof of valid vehicle insurance |
| **Criminal Record Check (RCC)** | A police-issued record check certificate |
| **Identity Document** | Only needed if you don't have a driver's licence — see below |

<Note>
  **The Identity Document is the alternate path for a courier without a driver's licence** — for example, someone delivering by bike or on foot. It accepts one of several kinds: a work permit, SIN document, PR card, passport, study permit, provincial photo ID, or another government-issued ID. You only need to submit **one**, whichever applies to you — you don't need both a licence and an identity document.
</Note>

## How to Upload

1. Open the app and navigate to **Driver Profile → Documents**.
2. You'll see a separate upload section for each document that applies to you.
3. Tap **Attach File** in each section and follow the on-screen prompts.
4. Once everything required is uploaded, tap **Save & Finish**.

<Note>
  Your documents are reviewed within **24–48 hours**. You'll get a notification once your account is verified and ready to take orders.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Not ready to finish? Tap Finish Later at any time — your progress is saved automatically and you can return whenever you're ready.
</Tip>

## Document Upload Tips

- Make sure photos are **clear and well-lit** — blurry or dark images will delay your review.
- All edges of the document should be **fully visible** in the photo.
- Insurance documents must be **current and not expired**.
- If a document is rejected, you'll be notified and can re-upload from the same screen.

## How "Verified" Is Decided

Your account only counts as fully verified once **every document you've submitted has been approved** — a single declined document keeps your account unverified, even if the rest passed. You don't need to submit a document you don't have (like the licence, if you're on foot or by bike and submitted an Identity Document instead) — only what you've actually uploaded is counted.

## After Verification

Once approved, your driver profile is unlocked and you can:

- Set up your vehicle profile
- Schedule shifts
- Go online and start accepting delivery requests
