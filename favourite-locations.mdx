---
title: "Favourite locations"
---

Favourite Locations lets you save addresses you visit often — like your workplace, a family member's home, or a regular pickup spot — so you can select them quickly when booking a ride or placing a delivery order.

---

## Getting to Favourite Locations

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under the **Favourite Locations** section, tap **View all**.

A panel will slide up showing all your saved locations.

---

## What You Will See

The Favourite Locations panel shows a list of all the addresses you have saved. For each location you will see:

- **Location name** — the label you gave it (for example, "Work Office")
- **Address name** — the name of the specific place
- **Street address** — the full street address

If you have not saved any locations yet, you will see a message: **"Add favourite locations to display here."**

---

## Adding a New Favourite Location

1. In the Favourite Locations panel, tap the **green plus button** ( \+ ) in the top right corner.
2. The address search panel will open.
3. Follow these steps:

### Step 1 — Select Province and City

1. Tap the **Select your province...** dropdown and choose your province.
2. Once a province is selected, the **Select your city...** dropdown will appear.
3. Tap it and choose the city where this location is.
4. Tap **Next**.

### Step 2 — Name Your Location

1. In the **Name** field at the top, type a label for this address (for example, _Work Office_, _Mum's House_, _Gym_).

### Step 3 — Search for the Address

1. Tap the **address field** (the one with the location pin icon).
2. Start typing the street address.
3. Suggested addresses will appear below as you type.
4. Tap the correct address from the list.
5. A green confirmation bar will appear at the bottom of the screen saying **"Location saved"**.

### Step 4 — Save the Location

1. Tap **Add Location**.
2. A confirmation message will appear: **"Address is updated."**
3. Tap **OK**.

The new location will now appear in your Favourite Locations list.

<Tip>
  Give each location a clear, memorable name. This makes it much faster to select the right address when booking a ride or placing a delivery order.
</Tip>

---

## Removing a Favourite Location

If you no longer need a saved location:

1. Find the location in the list.
2. Tap the **Remove** button (shown with a red rubbish bin icon) on the right side of that location.
3. A confirmation dialog will appear: **"Favourite location is deleted — Favourite location has been removed from the list."**
4. Tap **OK** to confirm.

The location will be removed from your list immediately.

<Warning>
  Removing a favourite location cannot be undone. If you remove one by mistake, you will need to add it again from scratch.
</Warning>

---

## Closing the Panel

To close the Favourite Locations panel without making any changes, tap the **X button** in the top right corner of the panel.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="How many favourite locations can I save?">
   There is no stated limit. You can add as many locations as you need.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit a saved location? ">
   At this time, you cannot edit a saved location directly. To update an address, remove the old one and add the new one in its place.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are favourite locations different from my default address? ">
  Yes. Your default address is your primary home address and is set separately under **Settings → Home**. Favourite Locations are additional addresses you want to save for quick access.
</Accordion>
