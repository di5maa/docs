---
title: "Favourite vendors"
---

The Favourite Vendors page shows all the restaurants and merchants you have saved to your favourites. It gives you quick access to the places you order from most often.

## Getting to Favourite Vendors

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under the **Favourite Locations** section, tap **Favourite merchants**.

---

## What You Will See

Your saved vendors are displayed as cards. Each card shows:

- **Cover photo** — the vendor's banner image
- **Logo** — the vendor's logo displayed as a circular photo
- **Vendor name** — shown below the logo
- **Verified badge** — a green tick appears on the logo if the vendor holds certifications on Local Pass
- **Arrow button** — tap to open the vendor's store page

If you have not saved any vendors yet, the page will be empty.

---

## Visiting a Saved Vendor

To go to a vendor's store page:

1. Tap anywhere on the vendor card, or tap the arrow button on the right side.
2. You will be taken directly to that vendor's menu and store profile.

---

## How to Save a Vendor as a Favourite

Vendors are saved from their individual store pages, not from the Favourite Vendors page itself.

To save a vendor:

1. Open any restaurant or store on Local Pass.
2. Look for the **heart icon** or **favourite button** on the store profile page.
3. Tap it to add the vendor to your favourites.

The vendor will then appear on your Favourite Vendors page.

---

## Removing a Vendor from Favourites

To remove a vendor:

1. Open the vendor's store page from Local Pass.
2. Tap the **heart icon** again to un-save it.

The vendor will be removed from your Favourite Vendors list.

<Note>
  You cannot remove a vendor directly from the Favourite Vendors page. You need to visit the vendor's store page to un-save them.
</Note>

---

## Going Back

Tap the **back arrow** (←) in the top left corner to return to Settings.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is my favourite vendor not showing on this page?** If you recently saved a vendor, try closing and reopening the Favourite Vendors page. If it still does not appear, check that you are signed in to the correct account.

**Can I reorder my favourite vendors?** At this time, vendors are listed in the order they were saved. Custom ordering is not available.

**What does the verified badge mean?** The verified badge means the vendor holds one or more certifications on the Local Pass platform, such as quality or health-related credentials.
