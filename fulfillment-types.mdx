When you check out on Local Pass, you'll be asked how you'd like to receive your order. The options available to you depend on the vendor and the type of products you're ordering.

<Info>
  Vendors set their own fulfillment options. Not every option will be available at every store.
</Info>

---

## Delivery

**Who handles it:** Depends on the vendor.

Standard delivery is available at most restaurants and many marketplace vendors. Your order is delivered to the address saved on your account.

There are two kinds of standard delivery:

### Platform Delivery (Local Pass Drivers)

Your order is picked up and delivered by a Local Pass driver. This is the most common option for restaurant orders. Some food marketplace vendors can opt in for "Express" delivery which will is identical and fulfilled by Local Pass. Delivery time is shown at checkout and is estimated based on the vendor's prep time and your distance.

### Vendor's Own Delivery

Some vendors — particularly restaurants — have their own delivery drivers. When a vendor uses their own drivers, the delivery is managed entirely by them. Local Pass coordinates the order, but the vendor is responsible for the delivery itself.

<Note>
  Whether a vendor uses their own drivers or our platform drivers affects how fees are calculated. We'll always show you the breakdown clearly at checkout. See [Understanding Your Fees](/checkout/understanding-fees).
</Note>

**When to choose it:** You want your order delivered to your door and you're within the vendor's delivery range.

---

## Express Delivery

**Who handles it:** Local Pass drivers — always.

Express is an on-demand delivery option available exclusively through the **Agrifood Marketplace**. It's faster than standard delivery and uses our dedicated driver network to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Express delivery uses **pre-authorization** — your card is held at checkout but only charged once the driver picks up your order. If the order is cancelled before pickup, you won't be charged.

**When to choose it:** You want your order fast and you're willing to pay a slightly higher delivery fee for on-demand service.

<Warning>
  Express delivery is a premium service. Delivery times are estimates — actual delivery may vary based on driver availability, traffic, and weather.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Express delivery is available in select locations. 
</Note>

---

## Pickup

**Who handles it:** You pick it up yourself.

Select a pickup location from the available options, then choose a pickup time that works for you. The time options are calculated based on the vendor's prep time and their business hours.

For marketplace vendors with multiple locations (e.g. farmers market stalls, urban pickup hubs), you'll see a list of active pickup spots to choose from.

**When to choose it:** You'd prefer to pick up your order and avoid delivery fees entirely.

<Tip>
  Pickup orders do not include a delivery fee or regulatory fee, which makes them the most affordable fulfillment option.
</Tip>

---

## Shipping

**Who handles it:** Third-party shipping carriers (Canada Post, Purolator, etc.)

Shipping is available only in the **Agrifood Marketplace**, and only for products that are eligible to be shipped (e.g. packaged goods, dried products, non-perishables). Merchants specify which products require shipping. 

Each vendor decides whether they want to use flat **rate shipping** or carrier's calculated rates. Our system automatically picks up the most efficient and cost-effective solution for you.  

When you place a shipping order, you'll be shown available shipping rates from real carriers with estimated delivery dates. You choose the speed that works for you:

- Standard (slower, more affordable)
- Expedited (faster, higher cost)

Your shipping address can be updated at checkout. If you change your address, Local Pass will automatically recalculate shipping rates for you.

<Note>
  Shipping is not available for fresh or perishable items. These products will show Delivery or Pickup as the only options.
</Note>

**When to choose it:** You're ordering packaged goods and want them sent directly to your home, especially if you're outside a vendor's local delivery zone.

## A Note on Fairness

Local Pass does not mark up delivery fees or take a hidden cut from the vendor's earnings. Every fee you see at checkout goes to one of three places:

1. **The driver** — who picks up and delivers your order
2. **The platform** — to cover payment processing and operations
3. **The vendor** — who prepares your order

We believe everyone in the chain deserves fair compensation, and we're committed to showing you exactly where your money goes. See [Understanding Your Fees](/checkout/understanding-fees) for the full breakdown.
