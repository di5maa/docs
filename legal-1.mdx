Welcome to Local Pass — your all-in-one app for ordering food, shopping local, and more. This page is your quick orientation before you dive in.

---

## New Here? Start With These

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account or sign in">
    Sign up with Google or email, or sign back in if you already have an account.

    [Sign in →](/sign-in)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    A short walkthrough sets your default delivery address and introduces Takeout Delivery.

    [User Onboarding →](/onboarding)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your personal details">
    Add your name, photo, and contact details so vendors and drivers can recognize you.

    [Personal Information →](/personal-details)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Local Pass">
    Browse restaurants near you or shop the Local Market — see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>
