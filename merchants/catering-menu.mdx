title: "Catering menu"
description: "Add trays, per-guest meals and buffets to your catering menu, with their own prices, sizes, options, allergens and notice."
---

Your catering menu is what customers can order from your catering page. Open it from **Catering → Catering menu**. Restaurants also reach it from **Menu**, on the **Catering** tab (next to **Menu**). Stores reach it from **Inventory**, on the **Catering** tab (next to **Products**). It's the same menu in all three places.

At the top you see your group discount, if you have one, and **Preview page** to open your catering page as customers see it.

## Two kinds of catering item

Every catering item is sold one of two ways. You choose under **Sell as** in the item editor.

- **Catering only**: "Only on your catering page. It won't show on your menu, in your shop or in the Local Pass app." New items you make here start this way.
- **Also on my menu**: "The same item customers order from your menu. Catering gets its own price, sizes and options." Use this for a dish or product you already sell.

To bring in things you already sell, press **Add from your menu** (restaurants) or **Add from your products** (stores). Tick the items, choose a section under **Add to**, and press **Add items**. Each one stays on your menu or in your shop as usual. Subscription boxes can't be catering items.

## Add a catering item

Press **Add catering item**. You're asked "What are you adding?":

- **Tray or platter**: customers choose how many. For example, a tray at $90.00 that serves 10 to 12: 4 trays come to $360.00.
- **Per-guest meal**: one portion for each guest. At $18.00 a guest, 40 guests come to $720.00.
- **Buffet**: one price per guest, and guests help themselves. Choose:
  - **Set menu: you choose the dishes**. Every guest gets every dish.
  - **Guests choose from courses**. Guests pick from each course, for example 2 mains of 6. You can **Start blank** or start from a template, such as a wedding, office lunch, birthday, celebration of life, holiday open house or brunch buffet.

You can change the type later from the item's menu (**Change type**). **Duplicate as a new buffet** makes a copy to turn into, say, a Premium version.

## The item editor

The editor opens beside your menu, in four parts.

### What it is

The **Name**, a **Photo**, and **Show on my catering menu**. An item goes on your catering page only once it has a price and you've confirmed its allergens. Until then the switch says customers can't see it yet.

### Price (Dishes and price, for a buffet)

- A tray or per-guest meal has one price, or sizes: **Offer sizes**, like a half tray and a full tray, each with its own price. For each size you can say how many it **Serves**.
- A buffet always has a **Price per guest**. **Offer menu levels** lets customers pick a level, like Classic and Premium, each with its own price per guest.
- Options add to the price:
  - **Add a choice**: customers pick, for example a protein or 2 sides. Set **Fewest choices** and **Most choices**.
  - **Add extras**: optional add-ons, for example extra sauce, each with its added price.
  - On a buffet: **Add a course**, **Add an optional course** (guests can add these, for example desserts) and **Guests get all of these** (included in the price per guest). A dish can **Charge extra for this dish**.
- You can pick dishes for a buffet from your menu. They show as **From your menu**.

**Customers see** shows the price as it appears on your page. If customers cover card processing, that's your price plus 3.5%.

### Allergens

- **Dietary**: tap the labels that apply, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal or Kosher.
- **Allergens**: tick each priority allergen the item contains. On a buffet, **Copy allergens and labels from these dishes** fills them in from the linked dishes. Check them against your recipes.
- Tick **I've checked the allergens for this item**. Nothing ticked means the item contains none of the priority allergens. If you change the allergens later, you confirm them again.

<Warning>
Local Pass is a platform, not a regulator. You're responsible for your own permits, licences, labels and allergen information. If a rule is unclear, ask Island Health.
</Warning>

### More details (Guests and more, for a buffet)

- **Minimum order**, **Sold in multiples of** and **Maximum order**. A live sentence shows the result, for example "Minimum 2 trays". For a per-guest item, "The quantity starts at the customer's guest count."
- **Notice needed**: how far ahead this item must be ordered, in hours, days, weeks or months, up to 3 months. Leave it empty to use your store's notice.
- **Describe it**, **What comes with each tray** (with quick picks like "Serving utensils" or "Sauce on the side"), how it's packed, how it's **Served** (for example hot), and **Serving & reheating**. Serving and reheating notes are shown to the customer and printed on your kitchen ticket.

Press **Save item**. If you leave with unsaved changes, you're asked first.

## Organize your menu

- **New section** adds a section. Drag sections and items by their handles to reorder them.
- **Search items** and the **Show** filter (**All**, **On menu**, **Hidden**, **Needs attention**, **Catering only**, **Also on menu**) help you find things. Clear the search and filters to drag again.
- **Needs attention** appears over the list when something is stopping items from reaching customers, such as a missing price or allergens not confirmed. Each line opens the item, or filters the list to the items it's about. If a catering-only item was published to your shop from the business app, **Fix all** puts it back on your catering page only.
- The switch on each row puts the item on or off your catering page.

## What customers see

Customers see your sections, each item's photo, name, price, sizes and options, dietary labels and allergens. They choose options and a quantity within your minimum, multiples and maximum. If an item needs more notice than your store does, the earliest date they can pick moves out to match.
