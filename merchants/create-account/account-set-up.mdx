title: "Account set up"
---

After creating your account, you'll be guided through a **5-step store setup**. You can move forward and backward between steps at any time using the **Next Step** and **Previous Step** buttons.

The steps are:

1. Owner & Store Details
2. Business Address & Hours
3. Store Attributes
4. Social Media
5. Appearance

---

## Step 1 — Owner & Store Details

This step collects essential information about you and your business.

### Owner details

These details are used internally to manage your account and are not displayed publicly to customers.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **First name** | e.g. Peter |
| **Last name** | e.g. Johnson |
| **Mobile number** | Used for account purposes only |

### Store details

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Business name** | The name customers will see, e.g. Gourmet Meats & Pastry |
| **About business** | The story of your business — up to 1,000 characters |
| **Tagline** | A short catchy phrase highlighting your store — up to 140 characters |
| **Business type** | Select your category from the dropdown (e.g. Restaurant, Farm, Bakery) |
| **Cuisine type** | Only appears if you select **Restaurant** — choose your cuisine style |

### Business contact details

These details are **visible to customers** on your public store page.

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | [hello@yourbusiness.ca](mailto:hello@yourbusiness.ca) |
| **Phone** | (604) 555-1234 |
| **Website** | [https://yourbusiness.ca](https://yourbusiness.ca) |

Click **Next Step** when done.

---

## Step 2 — Business Address & Hours

Set the physical location of your business and your opening hours.

### Business address

1. Start typing your address in the search field
2. A list of suggested addresses will appear below
3. Tap the correct address to confirm it
4. Your saved address will display above the search field

> **Important:** You must select a business address before you can move to the next step. The **Next Step** button stays disabled until an address is confirmed.

### Location hours

Set your opening and closing times for each day of the week.

- Use the time selectors to set **Open from** and **Open until** for each day
- If you are closed on a particular day, toggle the **Closed** switch for that day

Click **Next Step** when done.

---

## Step 3 — Store Attributes

Tell customers how you sell and who you are.

### Sales model

The options shown depend on your business type.

**For non-restaurant businesses** (farms, bakeries, food producers, etc.):

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Retail** | You sell directly to individual customers |
| **Wholesale** | You sell in bulk to other businesses |
| **Both** | You offer retail and wholesale |

**For restaurants:**

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Dine-in** | Customers eat at your location |
| **Takeout** | Customers pick up their order |
| **Both** | You offer dine-in and takeout |

> Only one sales model option can be active at a time. Selecting **Both** will automatically deactivate the individual options.

### Business attributes

These optional tags help customers discover your store when searching or filtering on Local Pass.

| Attribute | What it signals |
| --- | --- |
| **Indigenous-owned** | Your business is Indigenous-owned |
| **Women-owned** | Your business is women-owned |
| **Ethnic Store** | Your business specialises in products from a specific cultural heritage |

Click **Next Step** when done.

## Step 4 — Social Media

Link your social media accounts so customers can follow your business. All fields are optional.

| Platform | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Facebook** | Your full profile link or page URL |
| **Instagram** | Your full profile link |
| **TikTok** | Your full profile link |
| **YouTube** | Your channel link |
| **WeChat** | Your WeChat handle or link |

Click **Next Step** when done.

---

## Step 5 — Appearance

Upload your store's logo and cover photo. A live preview shows how your store will look to customers on both desktop and mobile before you submit.

### Uploading your logo

1. Click **Upload logo**
2. Select an image from your device
3. Click **Upload** to save it to your profile

### Uploading your cover photo

1. Click **Upload cover photo**
2. Select an image from your device
3. Click **Upload** to save it to your profile

> Images are compressed automatically to help your store page load quickly for customers.

### Resetting your images

If you want to start over, click **Reset** to clear your selected images and begin again.

### Live preview

Below the upload buttons you'll see a real-time preview showing how your store card will appear to customers in both **desktop** and **mobile** views. The preview updates automatically as you upload images and fill in your store name and description.

### Completing setup

Once you're happy with your store's appearance, click **Complete** to create your store. 🎉
