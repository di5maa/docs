**Customers** lists everyone you sell to: the people and businesses who have bought from you, and the ones you've saved yourself. It's in the business portal and in the Local Pass Business app.

- **Portal:** in the sidebar, **Customers → Customers**.
- **App:** in the menu, under **CUSTOMERS**, tap **Customers**.

<Note>
  Customers is for every business except restaurants. A business with no type set counts as a store. The wholesale part is for shops and farms only.
</Note>

This page describes the portal. The app's list still shows order counts, spend and a status for each customer; see [In the app](#in-the-app).

## Step by step: work with your customers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a customer">
    Open **Customers** and type in the search box: a name, business, email, phone or tag. The list narrows as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Filter by **Type**, **Buys through** or **Tags**. On a wide screen, use the funnel in a column's header. On a phone or tablet, press **Filters**. Your filters show above the list, each with an **×** to remove it, and **Clear all** removes them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Click or tap a customer. You see their orders and spend for the past year, how often they order, what they buy most, and each order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save them">
    Tags and notes are for saved customers. If they bought from you but aren't saved yet, press **Save customer**, check their details and press **Save customer** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag them">
    On the **Tags** card, press **Edit** and add a tag, such as "Farmers market" or "Allergy: nuts". Each change saves right away, and you can then search and filter by it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Type in the **Note** card: delivery instructions, who to call, what they usually need. It saves as you type. Customers never see tags or notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message them">
    Press **Message** to chat with them, the same chat as in your [Inbox](/merchants/inbox). They need a Local Pass account. You can also press **Call** or **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a wholesale account">
    For a business you supply, press **Add customer** and choose **Business**. Enter the **Business** name and an **Email** (wholesale customers need one: order emails and invoices go there). Turn on **Sells to them wholesale**, choose **Their prices** and **Invoices due**, and press **Save customer**. Their profile now has a **Wholesale** card, with **Send availability**, and a **New order** button. See [Wholesale customers](/merchants/wholesale-customers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The list

The list is for finding a customer or a group of customers: who they are, how they buy, your tags and when they last ordered. Order counts and money are on each customer's profile.

On a wider screen, the list is a table:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Their name, their business under it, and their email and phone. |
| **Type** | **Person** or **Business**, with the kind of business if you set one, for example **Business · Restaurant**. |
| **Buys through** | How they buy from you: **Shop**, **Wholesale**, **Subscriptions**, **Catering** or **Pre-orders**. |
| **Tags** | Your tags. If there are more than two, the rest show as **+1**, **+2** and so on. |
| **Last order** | The date of their last order. **Reserved** means they reserved on a pre-order and haven't paid yet. **No orders yet** is a saved customer who hasn't ordered. |

**Type**, **Buys through** and **Tags** show on a large screen. On a smaller one, their filters are under **Filters**.

On a phone, you get a compact list: each customer's name, their business, how they buy, and their last order.

The list shows how many customers you have, or how many match your search and filters, for example **12 of 85**.

### No customers yet

Until someone orders, the page says **No customers yet**. You can **Add customer**, or **Import** the businesses you already sell to: **download the template** from the same message.

## Search and filter

Search by name, business, email, phone or tag. For a phone number, the digits are enough.

Three filters narrow the list:

- **Type**: **Person**, **Business**, or a kind of business (Restaurant, Café or bakery, Grocer or market, Caterer or food truck, School, care home or hospital, Non-profit or food bank, Office or workplace, Other).
- **Buys through**: **Shop**, **Wholesale**, **Subscriptions**, **Catering** or **Pre-orders**.
- **Tags**: your own tags. With many tags, type in **Find a tag**.

Each filter lists only what your customers have. The number beside each choice is how many customers it would show.

Ticking several choices in one filter shows customers who match any of them. Filters on different columns must all match. If nobody matches, press **Clear filters**.

**Sort:** click the **Customer** or **Last order** header to sort by it, and click again to reverse. On a phone, choose **Sort by** in **Filters**: **Last order, newest first**, **Last order, oldest first**, **Name, A–Z** or **Name, Z–A**.

Your search, filters and sort stay in the page's address, so you can bookmark a view.

## A customer's profile

Open a customer to see their profile. **Customers** at the top takes you back to the list.

At the top: their name (the business name for a business) and one line about them, such as **Wholesale customer**, **Business** or **Person**, the kind of business, **Subscriber**, **Catering** or **Pre-orders**, when they became a customer, **New customer** or **Regular**, and **Due for an order** or **Quiet**. Beside it are **Call**, **Email**, **Message**, **Edit** (or **Save customer**) and, for a wholesale customer, **New order**.

The numbers cover their orders from the past year.

- **Overview:**
  - **Orders**, with how they buy.
  - **Total spent**, with what they spend an order.
  - **Last order**, with its date.
  - **Usually orders**, for example **About every 7 days**, or **Not enough orders yet**. It also says when it's been longer than usual.
  - **Last 12 months**: what they spent each month.
- **What they buy most:** their top products by spend, for the last **90 days** or **12 months**. Each one shows how many times they bought it, how much, what they spent, when they last ordered it and the last price.
- **Orders:** every order, newest first, 25 to a page. If they buy more than one way, choose a type, such as **Shop**, **Order sheet** or **Pre-order**. A wholesale customer's orders also have tabs such as **To invoice**, **Unpaid**, **Overdue** and **Paid**, with **Invoice** and **Mark paid** where they apply.
- **Reserved, not paid:** pre-order reservations with nothing paid yet. These aren't counted in orders or spend.

On the side, or below on a phone:

- **Contact:** who you deal with, their email and phone, and **Message**.
- **Address:** where they are, when they take deliveries, and whether they usually pick up or get delivery.
- **Wholesale:** for a business you supply. See [Wholesale customers](#wholesale-customers).
- **Tags** and **Note**.

### Contact them

- **Call** and **Email** open your phone or email.
- **Message** opens a chat with them, the same chat as in your [Inbox](/merchants/inbox). The customer needs a Local Pass account.

There's no group email. Shoppers haven't signed up for newsletters from you, so you contact them one at a time.

### Tags and notes

For a saved customer, add up to 10 tags on the **Tags** card. Press **Edit**, type a tag and press Enter, or pick one you've **Used before**. Each change saves right away.

The **Note** card is for delivery instructions, who to call or what they usually need. It saves as you type.

Only you see tags and notes. Customers never do.

## Add or save a customer

Press **Add customer** to add someone who isn't in the list yet. For someone who bought from you, press **Save customer** on their profile to keep them with your own notes and tags.

1. Choose **Person** (their **Name**) or **Business** (the **Business** name, and optionally the **Kind of business** and a **Contact**).
2. Add an **Email** or a **Phone**. One is enough.
3. Optionally add an **Address**. For a business, search its name or address and pick it. For a business, you can also add **Receiving hours**, which delivery routes plan around.
4. Optionally add **Tags** and a **Note**.
5. Press **Save customer**.

To change them later, press **Edit**.

## Import business customers

Moving from another system? Press **Import** to bring in the businesses you already sell to from a spreadsheet.

1. Press **Download template** and fill in one business per row, or use your own file with the same column names.
2. Save it as a CSV file and choose it.
3. Choose a **Price sheet for everyone**, or **None (not wholesale)**. Rows that name a sheet get that one.
4. Check the rows: **Will add**, **Already a customer** or **Problem**. Then press **Import**, for example **Import 12 customers**.

Nobody is emailed. A business whose email is already one of your customers is skipped, never changed.

## Wholesale customers

A business you supply can be a wholesale customer: turn on **Sells to them wholesale** and choose **Their prices** (one of your order sheets) and **Invoices due**. You can also **Leave them out of availability emails**.

The **Wholesale** card on their profile shows their **Price sheet**, when **Invoices due**, whether they get **Availability emails**, what's **Owed now** and overdue, and how soon they **Usually pay**. Press **Send availability** to email them your sheet, or **Repeat last order** to start from their last order. **New order** is at the top of the profile. See [Wholesale customers](/merchants/wholesale-customers).

## Download a CSV

Press **Download CSV** to get the customers you're looking at, with your search, filters and sort. It has each customer's name, business, email, phone, type, how they buy, tags, and first and last order.

The file holds customers' contact details. Keep it safe, and use it only to serve your customers.

## In the app

The app's Customers works the same way, with a few differences:

- At the top: **Customers** (who bought in the past year), **New this month**, **Repeat** (the share who ordered more than once) and **Average spend**.
- Each customer shows their orders, what they spent, their last order and a status:

  | Status | When |
  | --- | --- |
  | **New** | First order in the last 30 days. |
  | **Regular** | 3 or more orders. |
  | **Due to order** | It's been more than one and a half times their usual gap. |
  | **Gone quiet** | 2 or more orders, and none in over 60 days. |
  | **Reserved, not paid** | Reserved on a pre-order but hasn't paid. |

- **Filters** also has **Orders** (for example **No orders yet**, **2 or more** or **10 or more**), **Spent** (for example **Under \$50** or **\$500 or more**) and **Status**, and you can sort by spend or orders.
- On a tablet, the list and the customer sit side by side.
- The note is called **Your note**.
- **New order** and **Repeat last order** open the business portal.
- **Download CSV** opens your share sheet.
