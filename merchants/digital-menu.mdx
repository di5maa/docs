This is a separate tool from the plain "QR Menu" link on your regular Menu Management page. That one just shares your existing live menu URL — this one lets you design a fully branded, presentation-quality version with your own layout, colors, and fonts, exported as a PDF or shared as a public link and QR code.

Restaurants see this called **Menu Creator**; agrifood/store vendors see it called **Price List Creator** — same tool, same tabs, different label throughout.

<Info>
  The value proposition, in the app's own words: _"Your menu, always up to date — no reprinting, ever. Customers scan a QR or tap a link to see your menu; edit a price or sold-out item and it updates everywhere instantly. They tap any dish to order on Local Pass."_
</Info>

## Five tabs

### Template

Three layouts to start from:

| Template | Style |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimal** | Clean lines, airy spacing |
| **Noir** | Dark, dramatic, premium |
| **Vivid** | Bold color, modern |

### Brand

Upload a logo, set your **Business Name** and an optional **Tagline**, and toggle **Show prices**, **Show descriptions**, and **Show item photos** independently.

### Colors

Six preset palettes, each pairing a primary/secondary/accent color with a matching background and text color — pick one rather than choosing colors individually:

**Midnight Noir** (dramatic), **Forest Garden** (organic/fresh), **Warm Terracotta** (rustic), **Nordic Slate** (clean/Scandinavian), **Kyoto Cherry** (delicate), **Golden Classic** (timeless/warm).

You also get six font pairings (heading + body font), each with its own preview: Classic Elegance, Romantic Serif, Literary, Bold Impact, Friendly Modern, and Pure Minimal.

### Content

Your menu/products, organized into sections — drag to reorder, toggle any item's visibility without deleting it.

### Publish

Same publishing model as the [Order Sheet Creator](/merchants/order-sheet): a **Public** toggle (blocked until you've set a Business Name), a public URL at `localpass.ca/{your-store}/menu` with copy/open/QR-download buttons, and optional **Availability windows** to restrict when it's live to specific days and times (America/Vancouver).

## Preview and export

Toggle between **Desktop** and **Mobile** preview at the top of the page to see how it looks on each. **Export PDF** generates a printable version of your current design — useful for printing physical menus or price sheets.

## Saving

There's no autosave. The header shows **"• Unsaved changes"** next to your business name whenever you have edits pending — tap **Save** to commit them.
