---
title: "Manage your subscribers"
description: "See everyone who subscribes to your boxes, keep notes and tags, email them, follow up on payments, skip a box for a subscriber or cancel a subscription."
---

**Customers → Subscribers** lists everyone you have a relationship with through your boxes: current and past subscribers, people on a waitlist, and people you've invited. Each box's own **Subscribers** tab shows the same people for that box.

## The subscriber list

Filter the list with **Current**, **Not paid**, **Paused**, **Waitlist**, **Invited**, **Past** or **Everyone**. Narrow it with the box and tag menus, or search by name, email, phone or tag.

Each row shows the person's boxes, their plan, their payment, their next box, and buttons to email, call or text them.

### Payment

The payment pill says where each subscriber stands:

| Pill | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Paid** | Their next box is paid. A subscriber who paid for the season shows how many of their boxes are left. |
| **Pending** | They pay per box, and their card is charged when changes close. |
| **Not paid** | Their card was declined, or their bank is waiting for them to confirm. The card is tried again every 12 hours until your pickup window starts. |
| **Skipping** | They're skipping their next box, and won't be charged for it. |
| **Paused** | Their boxes are paused, until a date or until they resume. |
| **No box scheduled** | No box is coming up for them. |

## Open a subscriber

Press **Open** on a row to see the person's details:

- **Contact**: email and phone. **Email** writes to them through Local Pass. **Email app**, **Call** and **Text** use your own email app and phone.
- **Subscription**: the box, their plan, pickup location or delivery address, payment, next box, subscriber since and boxes received.
- **Weeks**: each past box and what happened to it.
- **Notes**: notes for your team, like "Called Oct 3: picks up for her neighbour too." Subscribers never see them.
- **Tags**: your own labels, like "Volunteer", to filter the list. Subscribers never see them.
- **Messages**: every email Local Pass sent this person about your boxes. Their replies go to your email, not here.

### Skip or put back a box for a subscriber

If a subscriber who pays per box phones to skip, press **Skip the [date] box** on their subscription. They aren't charged for it, and Local Pass emails them to say so. They can undo it in their account until changes close. **Put back the [date] box** does the reverse, and the email says their card is charged when changes close.

This works only before changes close. Subscribers who paid for the season skip from their own account, where the box's value goes back to them as Local Coins.

### Send a payment reminder

When a subscriber shows **Not paid**, press **Send payment reminder**. They get an email with a link to update their card. You can send one every 12 hours.

### Cancel a subscription

Press **Cancel subscription**, give a reason, and press **Cancel subscription** again.

- No new boxes are made for them, and a subscriber who pays per box isn't charged again.
- They get an email with the reason you gave.
- If they paid for the season, the boxes they haven't received are refunded to their card.

## Email your subscribers

- From a subscriber: press **Email**.
- Several people: tick their rows and press **Email them**.
- Everyone in a box, or its waitlist: press **Email everyone in a box** and choose.

Write a subject (up to 120 characters) and a message in plain text (up to 5,000 characters). A blank line starts a new paragraph.

What the subscriber sees: an email from "your store name via Local Pass". Replies go to your store's email. The email ends by saying why they're getting it, with a link to their subscription.

- Emails are for your boxes: pickups, changes, what's coming. Not for advertising.
- One send reaches up to 500 people. Your store can send up to 1,000 emails a day.
- Past subscribers can be written to one at a time. Invited people get only their invitation until they join.

## Export

Press **Export CSV** to download the people shown, or tick rows first to download only those. The spreadsheet has name, email, phone, box, status, plan, pickup or address, since, boxes received, payment and tags. Invited people are included.

<Note>
Your subscribers' details are for running your boxes. Keep them private, and don't use them for advertising.
</Note>
