---
title: "Set up a subscription box"
description: "Make a subscription box: what's in it, its price, its dates and box days, pickup and delivery, subscriber rules and policies."
---

Go to **Sell → Subscriptions** and press **New subscription box**. To change a box later, open it and press **Box settings**.

The settings have six parts: **Box**, **Price**, **Dates**, **Pickup & delivery**, **Subscribers** and **Policies**. On a wide screen, **What subscribers see** beside them shows the box as subscribers will see it, as you type.

<Tip>
Selling two sizes? Make a box for each, like "Veggie Box — Small" and "Veggie Box — Large".
</Tip>

## 1. The box

- **Name**, for example "Summer Veggie Box" or "Weekly Bread Box".
- **What kind of box?** Veggie box, Fruit box, Fruit & veggie box, Meat box, Eggs, Dairy box, Flowers, Bakery box, Mixed box, or **Something else**. With **Something else**, type your own name under **What do you call it?** (up to 40 characters), for example "Mushroom box". Every box is listed under **Subscriptions & Farm Boxes** in the shop. Search goes by the box's name and description, so use the same words there.
- **Photo**. You need one to publish.
- **Description**: who grows or makes it, how the box changes through the season, and why people subscribe. It opens the box's page.
- **Size** (all optional): **Size**, **Feeds**, **Items** and **Weight**, for example "2–3 people" or "About 10 lb".
- **Typical items**: up to 30 things that usually go in. Type to pick from your products, or paste a list.
- **What do subscribers see each week?**
  - **I'll list what's in the box**: you post the week's items before changes close, and subscribers can swap items if you allow it.
  - **Surprise box (no weekly list)**: nothing to post each week. Subscribers see your typical items, and swaps are off.
- **Product labels**: how it's raised or made, and dietary labels. Shown next to the price.

## 2. Price and payment

- **Box price**: what one box costs.
- **Charge 5% GST on this box**: off for most boxes, because fresh produce is zero-rated. Check with your accountant.
- **Subscribers can pay for the season**: one payment covers every box left in the season they sign up for. You can set a **Season discount** of up to 25%, taken off each box. This needs season dates, so a box that runs all year can't offer it. Each new season is paid for on its own.

**What a subscriber pays** shows each line for one box: the box, delivery (if you deliver), the service fee, GST on the box (if you charge it) and the tax on the service fee. With season payment on, it also shows what a season costs for someone joining today.

<Note>
Subscribers keep the price they signed up at. Each box is charged at that price or your current price, whichever is lower. A lower price reaches everyone. A higher price, or switching on GST, applies to new subscribers only.
</Note>

## 3. Dates and schedule

- **Runs all year**: on for a box with no seasons. Off to set seasons, each with a **Season name** (optional), a **First day** and a **Last day**. Press **Add a season** to add another, up to 6. Subscribers who pay per box carry on into the next season. Subscribers who paid for a season finish with it. If you have another season planned, they get an email inviting them to sign up for it.
- **How often**: **Every week**, **Every two weeks**, or both. Subscribers choose one when they sign up.
- **Box day**: pickup and delivery both happen on this day.
- **Changes close**: 1 to 6 days before the box day, at a time you choose. That's when skips, swaps and extras lock, the week's boxes become orders, and subscribers who pay per box are charged.

### The box calendar

The **Box calendar** shows every box day in your seasons (or the next 12 months for a box that runs all year).

- Tap a box day to take that week off. Tap it again to put the box back.
- A week can come off until its changes close. After that it's shown but can't be changed.
- Nobody is charged for a week off. Subscribers who paid for the season get that box back as Local Coins, with 5% extra.

Under the calendar, **Holiday weeks** lists the BC stat holidays (and Boxing Day) that fall in a week with one of your box days. Press **Take the week off** beside one to take it off.

<Warning>
If a change takes boxes away from subscribers (new season dates, a removed season or a new box day), you're asked first, with how many subscribers it affects. A subscription with no box days left ends, and subscribers who paid for the season get their unused boxes back as Local Coins.
</Warning>

## 4. Pickup and delivery

Offer one or both. Subscribers choose when they sign up.

**Subscribers pick up**
- Set the pickup window with **Pickup from** and **Until**.
- Tick your **Pickup locations**: your main location and any others you've added. To add one, press **Add a pickup location in Settings**.

**We deliver**
- You, or your driver, bring boxes to subscribers' doors on box day. Local Pass couriers aren't involved, and you keep the delivery fee.
- **Towns you deliver to**: subscribers can choose delivery only with an address in one of these towns. Many addresses in Saanich, Oak Bay and Esquimalt say "Victoria", so if you deliver there, tick Victoria too.
- **Delivery fee for each box**: \$0 for free delivery, up to \$50. Like the box price, subscribers keep the fee they signed up at, or your current fee if it's lower.
- **Delivered from** and **Until**: your delivery window.
- **Delivery notes** (optional): shown to subscribers who choose delivery.

Current subscribers keep what they chose. Turning off delivery, a town or a pickup location doesn't move anyone already on it.

## 5. Subscribers

- **Subscriber limit** (optional): when the box is full, people can join a waitlist. The first person on it is emailed when a spot opens.
- **Boxes before a subscriber can cancel**: for subscribers who pay per box. 0 lets them cancel any time. Subscribers who paid for the season can always cancel and get back what's left.
- **Limit skips**: off is recommended. Subscribers who pay per box aren't charged for a week they skip. Turn it on to allow a set number of skips each season (each calendar year for a box that runs all year).
- **Subscribers can pause**: for subscribers who pay per box. Set a **Longest pause** in weeks, or leave it blank for no limit.
- **Subscribers can swap items**: for alternatives you offer each week, of about the same value. Set how many items a week. The price doesn't change. Not shown for a surprise box.
- **Subscribers can add extras**: your other products, like eggs, bread or honey, charged with that week's box.
- **Subscribers can donate a week's box**: you still pack it and you're still paid. Enter **Who gets donated boxes (a food bank or charity)**.

## 6. Policies

Write these in your own words. Subscribers read them before they sign up.

- **Subscription terms**: subscribers tick a box agreeing to them when they sign up. Leave it empty and they aren't asked. A change applies to people who sign up after you save.
- **Missed pickup**: what happens to a box nobody picks up.
- **Cancelling** (optional): anything to add, like asking for a week's notice.
- **Crates and bags** (optional): if you'd like crates or bags back.
- **Welcome message** (optional): added to the welcome email subscribers get when they sign up.

For empty terms or an empty missed-pickup policy, press **Use our starting text** and edit it. Farms get starting text written for a farm season; other businesses get a shorter general one. **Rules subscribers will see** lists the rules written from your settings, so subscribers see the same rules Local Pass holds them to.

## Save and publish

- **Publish** puts the box on sale. People can find it on localpass.ca and subscribe.
- **Save draft** saves it without putting it on sale.
- Once published, use **Save changes**. **Unpublish** moves the box to drafts: nobody can subscribe, no boxes are made, and subscribers who paid for the season get Local Coins for each week missed.

If something needs fixing, the bar at the top says how many things, and takes you to each one.
