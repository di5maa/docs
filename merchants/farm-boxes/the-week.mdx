title: "Run the week"
description: "Post what's in the box, see the week at a glance, pack, tick boxes off as they go out, and write to subscribers."
---

Open a box from **Sell → Subscriptions**. Each card on that page shows the next box day, when changes close, and whether this week's list is posted (**No list yet**, **Not posted yet**, **Posted** or **No box this week**). The buttons **This week**, **Pack list** and **Subscribers** go straight to those tabs.

## This week

Pick the **Box day** at the top. The tab says when changes close for it.

### The week at a glance

The top of the tab sums up the day in plain words, for example "18 boxes · 2 skipping · 1 donated · your payout ≈ $612", then:

- **Paid · Pending · Not paid**: how the boxes stand on payment.
- How many are for pickup and for delivery, and how many extra items subscribers added.
- New subscribers, and subscriptions that were cancelled or ended, since the last box day. People on the waitlist.

Open **How your payout adds up** for the breakdown: boxes and extras, your delivery fees, GST you collect, the Local Pass fee and the GST and PST on it, refunds, and your payout. Before changes close it's an estimate (marked ≈), because skips, pauses and new subscribers can still change it. After the boxes are made, it's what goes to your wallet.

### What's in the box

For a box where you list the items:

1. Type each item and how much goes in one box, for example "Kale, 2 bunch". Suggestions come from your products and common item names.
2. Or press **Start from your typical items**.
3. If subscribers can swap, press **Add a swap** under an item to offer an alternative.
4. Add a **Note to subscribers** if you like: news from your business, how to store things, a recipe idea.
5. Press **Post to subscribers**. Subscribers see the list on their subscription and can make their swaps. **Save draft** saves it without showing subscribers.

After you post, change the list and press **Save changes**. Subscribers see the changes straight away.

No list this week? The box still goes out, and subscribers see your typical items.

For a surprise box there's no list to post. To tell subscribers what's coming, press **Write to your subscribers**.

### No box this week

Turn on **No box this week** and press **Save — no box this week**. Add a note to say why, and when boxes are back. Subscribers see it on their page, and nobody pays for that week. Subscribers who paid for the season get that box back as Local Coins.

You can also take weeks off ahead of time on the [box calendar](/merchants/farm-boxes/set-up-a-box#the-box-calendar).

### After changes close

The list is locked, because the boxes are made from it. You can still edit your note and press **Save note**. If you never posted the list, you can still press **Post to subscribers** so subscribers see what's coming.

## Pack & pickup

Pick the **Box day**. Until changes close, the list says "Not final: subscribers can still make changes". After, it says **Final**: these are the week's orders.

- **Pick list**: every item to pack for that day's boxes, swaps included, with the total and the amount for each pickup location.
- **Extras added**: what to pack beside the boxes.
- A list for each pickup location, with each subscriber's name, phone and box contents. Donated boxes say who they go to.
- **Home delivery**, if you deliver: stops numbered town by town, so the list reads as a route. Use **Print route** and **Copy addresses**. You deliver these yourself; Local Pass doesn't send a courier.

Press **Print or PDF** to print the pack list.

<Warning>
Only hand over a box marked **To pack** or **Paid — order being made**. A subscriber whose card was declined shows **Not paid — don't hand over**. Their card is tried again every 12 hours until your pickup window starts. If it still hasn't gone through, no box is made for them that week.
</Warning>

### Mark boxes picked up

Each box becomes an order when changes close. As subscribers pick them up, tick each name and press **Mark picked up**, or press **Mark all at this location**. For deliveries, use **Mark all delivered**. Subscribers aren't emailed when you do this.

Boxes are also in **Order fulfillment** with your other orders. Use the **Subscriptions** filter to see only boxes. A box paid for with the season reads **Paid for the season**.

## Subscribers and Waitlist

The **Subscribers** tab lists this box's subscribers: how often, pickup or delivery, payment, since when, boxes received and donated, and status. Filter by **Current**, **Not paid**, **Home delivery** or **Cancelled or ended**, and press **Copy emails** to copy their addresses. Open a subscriber to see more. See [Manage your subscribers](/merchants/farm-boxes/members).

The **Waitlist** tab shows who is waiting for a spot, if the box has a subscriber limit. When a subscriber leaves, the first person who hasn't been told yet gets an email that a spot opened.

## Updates

**Updates** is a timeline of what happened to the box, newest first: lists posted, weeks off, cards charged, people who subscribed or left, invitations sent and settings changed.

To write to every current subscriber of the box, use **Write to your subscribers** at the top. The subject starts as "News from" your store name. Write your message and press the send button, which says how many subscribers it goes to.

- It comes from "your store name via Local Pass". Replies go to your store's email.
- Subscribers get it by email, and see it with their subscription on localpass.ca, under your store's name.
- It's for your boxes: pickups, changes, what's coming. Not for advertising.
- Your store can send up to 1,000 emails a day.
