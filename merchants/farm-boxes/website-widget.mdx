title: "Box widget for your website"
description: "Show a farm box on your own website with its price, next box and a Join button."
---

The box widget puts a card for one of your boxes on your own website. Visitors see the box there and join on Local Pass.

## Add it

Two ways to get the code:

- Open the box and press **Add to my website** at the top. This button shows while the box is live.
- Or go to **Grow → Website widgets** and choose the **CSA box** type, then pick the box. It's offered once your store has a box.

Choose how it looks: the colour, the button shape, the font and the button wording. The preview shows the card as it will look. Press **Copy code**, then paste the code into your website where you want the card.

## What visitors see

A card with the box's photo, kind of share, name, price a box, how often it comes, the next box or the season, and spots left. The button reads **Join** by default, or **Join the waitlist** when the box is full.

The button opens the box's signup page on localpass.ca in a new tab. Signing up needs an account and a card, so it never happens inside your site. When someone joins this way, your box's **Updates** tab says they joined from your website.

<Note>
While your store or the box isn't live, your website shows only a link to the box. Publish the box in **Box settings** to show the full card.
</Note>

The other website widgets (your store, products and buttons) never show boxes, because a box isn't sold one at a time.
