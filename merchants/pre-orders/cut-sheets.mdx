title: "Cut sheets"
description: "Let buyers of an animal shared buy choose how their meat is cut. Set up your cut sheet once per animal, see what each buyer chose, fill one in for a buyer, and print the sheets for your processor."
---

A cut sheet asks each buyer of an animal [shared buy](/merchants/pre-orders/shared-buys) how they want their meat cut and packed. You keep one cut sheet per animal: your beef sheet, your pork sheet, and so on. You set it up the first time you sell that animal, and every shared buy of it after that uses it.

Cut sheets are on by default for every new shared buy of an animal still to be processed. Meat that's already cut and frozen has no cut sheet, and neither does food sold by the amount.

## Set up your sheet for an animal

The first time you make a shared buy of an animal, its **Cut sheet** step says **Set up your beef cut sheet** (with your animal's name). "You do this once. Every beef shared buy after this one uses it. We filled it in with the Local Pass starter sheet."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have your processor's sheet beside you">
    Match each part to how your processor usually cuts it, and untick anything they don't do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each part">
    Press **Change** on a part. Choose its **Standard cut**, and under **Let buyers pick instead**, tick the other choices buyers can make. Press **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the processor details">
    **Processor**, **Sheet due**, **Hang time**, **Packaging** and **Notes for your processor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own questions (optional)">
    Up to 4, each with 2 to 6 choices, your standard marked, and who is asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Press **Use this sheet**. You can change it later in **Settings → Cut sheets**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Not letting buyers choose cuts on this shared buy? Press **Turn cut sheets off**. Every share then gets your standard cuts. A shared buy you open without setting up a sheet uses the Local Pass starter sheet, and the setup page shows again next time.

### The sheet editor

The editor looks like the sheet your processor prints: your farm's name and the animal at the top, the processor details, then each part in print order, starting with how it's packed. On a wide screen it's a table: **Part**, **Standard**, **Buyers can also pick**.

- **Costs extra at most processors**: some choices sit in their own group. Show one only if your price already covers it. Buyers are told their choices don't change the price. To charge for something, add it to the shared buy as a paid extra instead (for example "Smoke my bacon"). A part sold as a paid extra isn't asked on the sheet.
- **Older cattle?** On beef and bison, a note on the short loin and rib.
- **Your own questions**: choices never change the price, so a choice can't contain "$".
- **Note for buyers**: the question buyers see above a note box at the end of their sheet. Leave it blank for no note box.
- **Start again from the Local Pass starter sheet** (in the **⋯** menu) clears your standard cuts, what buyers can pick and your own questions. Shared buys that already have orders don't change.

**Processor**, **Hang time**, **Packaging** and **Notes for your processor** are printed for your processor. Buyers never see the last three.

## What each share size is asked

How much a buyer is asked depends on the size of their share:

| Share | Asked |
| --- | --- |
| **Half or whole** | Every question. |
| **Quarter** | Only the questions marked **Quarters too**: how the packs are made. A quarter shares one side with another buyer, so it's cut your standard way. |
| **Less than a quarter** | Nothing. They get your standard cuts. |

For birds, every bird gets the full sheet.

## On each shared buy

On the **Cut sheet** step (and on **Animal & dates** once it's open):

- **Ask buyers how they want it cut**: the switch. Off, every share gets your standard cuts.
- **Choices close**: 1 to 30 days before processing (7 to start). The date shows beside it. Buyers can change their choices until then.
- **Change your beef sheet** opens the editor in place.
- **Leave out a question on this shared buy only**: for example when another processor doesn't do a cut. Everyone gets the standard for a question you leave out.

A shared buy keeps a copy of your sheet from when its first share sells, because buyers may have filled it in. Until then, a change to your sheet reaches this shared buy too. After that, changes apply to your next shared buy of that animal.

## The Cut sheets tab

While cut sheets are on, an open shared buy has a **Cut sheets** tab.

At the top: which sheet the shared buy uses and since when, when choices close, how many questions each size is asked, and when the reminder email goes out. **See this shared buy's sheet** shows it.

**Buyers' cut sheets** has one row per order with a share:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Order** and **Buyer** | Who it's for. |
| **Share** | Their share size. |
| **What they chose** | **Not yet**, **Standard cuts**, or the number of changes and the first few. |
| **Filled in by** | Who saved it and when, for example "Kyla · Nov 24" or "You · Nov 25". |

Filter by **All**, **Not yet**, **Changed** or **Standard**. A share under a quarter has nothing to fill in, so its row doesn't open.

### Reminders

Buyers are asked to choose their cuts when they pay. From 3 days before choices close, everyone who hasn't chosen yet gets one reminder by email and app notification. A buyer who doesn't choose by the deadline gets your standard cuts.

## See or fill in a buyer's sheet

Click a row. The buyer's sheet opens read-only, laid out like the print: each part and their choice, with changes marked. A quarter shows only how its packs are made. Their note for the butcher and any paid extras are at the bottom. **Print this sheet** prints it.

To fill it in for them (for example, a buyer who phoned you), press **Fill in for Kyla** (with their name), or **Change Kyla's choices** once they've saved one.

1. Pick a choice for each part you want to change. You can also edit their **note for the butcher**.
2. Add an optional **Note to Kyla**. It goes in the email.
3. Press **Save 3 changes and email Kyla** (or **Save standard cuts and email Kyla** on a sheet nobody has filled in). A confirm lists what changes and what stays as it is.
4. If it replaces choices the buyer made, a second confirm, **Replace 2 of Kyla's choices?**, shows what they chose beside what you're saving.
5. Press **Save and email Kyla** (or **Replace and email Kyla**).

You can fill in or change a sheet after choices close too.

If the buyer (or someone else at your store) saved the sheet while you had it open, nothing is saved, and you see the sheet as it is now. Closing with unsaved changes asks "Leave without saving?"

### What the buyer gets

The person who placed the order gets an email and an app notification saying you changed their cut sheet (or how their share is packed, for a quarter), with what changed and your note. It says until when they can still change it, or that choices are closed and to reply if something's wrong. If you change the same order again within 10 minutes, no new email goes. The buyer sees the change on their order.

## Print for your processor

On the **Cut sheets** tab, press **Print for the processor**, or choose **Cut sheets (PDF)** in the **⋯** menu. The PDF has:

1. Your farm's standard: the shared buy, processor, processing day, when the sheet is due, hang time, packaging, your notes for the processor, every part with its standard and what buyers could pick, and what isn't offered.
2. For each animal: a page listing every order on it, with a space for its tag, kill date, cutting day and hanging weight.
3. One page per order: the order number big, with "Label every pack with" that number, the buyer's choices in bold beside your standard, what wasn't asked, paid extras, their note, and a line for the packed weight. A split shows how its cuts are shared out.

**Export** downloads the same choices as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV**.

## Splits

On a split (a private group or a community share), the whole share is cut once. The person who started the split chooses the cuts, and everyone in it gets their part of every cut: "What the half holds: about 75 lb, cut once your way. Your part: about 37.5 lb, half of every cut." Others in the split see the choices but can't change them. They can post their wishes in the group.

## Settings → Cut sheets

**Settings → Pre-orders → Cut sheets** lists your sheets: **Animal**, **Questions**, **Your changes** and **Last edited**, with **Edit** on each. **Set up another animal** starts a sheet for an animal you haven't sold yet. Saving a sheet here applies to new shared buys of that animal, and to any that nobody has bought a share of yet.

## What buyers see

Buyers choose their cuts on their order page on localpass.ca. Their sheet starts at your standard cuts, and they change only what they care about. Each part they can choose has an **About** button with what the cut is, how to cook it and why pick it. Your own questions don't have one. See [Choosing your cuts](/customers/shared-buys#choosing-your-cuts) in the customer guide.
