On a [shared buy](/merchants/pre-orders/shared-buys), buyers can split a share with friends in a **Private group**, or open it to other customers as a **Community share**. This page covers following those groups, posting updates, and the **Updates & promote** tab.

## Private groups and community shares

You choose which ways to buy you allow under **Ways to buy**. See [Ways to buy](/merchants/pre-orders/shared-buys#ways-to-buy).

| | Private group | Community share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who can join** | Only people the organizer sends their private link to. | Anyone. It's listed on your shared buy's page with the organizer's first name and town. |
| **Paying** | Each person pays for their own part when they join. Anything still free when the friends' time is up is billed to the organizer. | Each person pays for their own part when they join. Nobody is billed for what isn't taken. |
| **If it doesn't fill** | The organizer pays for the rest. | For an animal, your choice: everyone gets every cent back, or everyone keeps their part and the rest is yours to sell. For food by the amount, everyone keeps their part. |
| **Packing** | One box, which one person collects and shares out. | One bag per person, with their name on it. Each person picks theirs up, or asks the organizer to collect it. On delivery, every bag goes to the organizer. |

The organizer is the customer who started the split. On your pages they show as "Organizer". On localpass.ca, the organizer of a community share is called a "Community champion".

Local Pass never moves money between buyers. Everyone pays you for their own part.

## Groups

The **Groups** card on the shared buy's **Overview** lists every private group and community share. Its header says how many are open and how many are full, and when community shares close.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Group** | The organizer's name (and which animal, on a batch), with a **Private group** or **Community share** tag and the town. |
| **Taken** | How much of the share is taken, with a bar, and what's still free. |
| **Status** | **Open** or **Open to friends** (with the closing day), **Full**, **Closed**, **Didn't fill** or **Cancelled**. |
| **Buyers** | How many people, and who has paid, who is paying now, or who owes. |

Click a row to open that order, or to see that group's orders on **Buyers**. With more than 6 groups, full groups fold into one line ("4 full groups"). Press **Show** to see them.

### What's left to join

The Overview's **Open to join** tile says how much is left to join in community shares. What's held for private groups is said separately, for example "+ 37.5 lb held for Jen C.'s friends". The bar draws the same parts: taken, left to join, held for a private group, and not sold.

Buyers see the same thing on your page, for example "75 lb left to join in 2 community shares".

### On Buyers

Filter **Buyers** by group to see one group's orders. Open an order to see each person in the split, their part and what they paid. From there you can remind someone to pay, refund one person's part, take a community share off your page (**Take off the community list**), or cancel the whole split. See [Buyers](/merchants/pre-orders/shared-buys#buyers).

The **To do** card on the Overview tells you when a private group's hold ends within 3 days and isn't full.

## Updates

Buyers see your updates under **Updates** on your page, newest first. Some are posted for you: when you open it, change a date, add a photo, change the place or a price, enter the weight, close orders, or when it fills.

To post your own, open **Updates & promote** and use **Post an update**:

- Write a short note. One-tap starters fit the kind of food, such as "Picking started." from a farm or "Back at the dock. Your fish is landed." from a boat.
- Add a photo if you like.
- On a batch of animals, choose **All animals** or one animal. Only buyers with a share of that animal get it.

You can post up to 10 updates a day.

**Who gets it:** buyers who've paid get updates that change their plans (your own notes, the processing day, and when it's going ahead) by email, a couple of minutes later. At most 3 update emails go out a day per shared buy. After that, a new update shows on the page only. Buyers with a Local Pass account also get it as an app notification. Each buyer can stop these updates with the link in the email. Their receipts, bills and pickup dates still come.

## Updates & promote

The top of the **Updates & promote** tab is everything that helps you fill your shared buy.

### Closing soon

In the last 3 days before community shares close, while one still has room, an amber banner says so. The Overview shows it too, with **Promote them**.

### Share the link

Your shared buy's link, with **Copy**, **Share…** (on devices that can share) and **QR code**. Print the QR code for your market table or farm gate. People who scan it land on your shared buy.

### Pictures and text for social media

- **Story picture**: a tall picture for Instagram and Facebook stories, in your farm's name, with what's left right now. Press **Download**. Download it again before each post, so it shows what's left.
- **Link preview**: shows on its own when you paste the link on Facebook, WhatsApp or in a text.
- **Text for your post**: a ready-to-paste post you can edit, then **Copy text**. The link in it tells you which orders came from your posts.

### Email

- **Your waiting list**: people who asked to hear about your pre-orders. Press **Email _n_ people about it** to tell them it's open. When you add more to a shared buy later, you can **Email _n_ people that you added more**, each time you add more.
- **Send a last call**: once per shared buy, in the last 3 days before the community shares close (or before orders close), or once less than a quarter is left. It emails the people on your waiting list who haven't ordered, "Last call: _your shared buy_ from _your store_", with what's left and when it closes. It's also posted as an update for your buyers, so they can pass it on. If today's 3 update emails have gone out, your buyers see it on the page.
- **Your buyers**: press **Write an update** to ask them to pass it on. It posts as an update and emails everyone who's paid.

Nothing here emails your past customers.

### Where it already shows

Without you doing anything, your shared buy shows on your store page on localpass.ca under pre-orders while it's open, and in link previews on Facebook, WhatsApp and iMessage.
