---
title: "Shared buys"
description: "Sell a whole animal in shares, or food by the amount that buyers can split. Set the sizes and prices, take payment as people buy, follow how it fills, weigh in, and pack one box or bag per buyer."
---

A shared buy is a kind of pre-order where buyers split one big thing between them. You can sell:

- **An animal** (beef, bison, pork, lamb, goat, chicken, turkey, duck or another animal) in shares: a half, a quarter, an eighth, a bird, a box, or any amount in pounds.
- **Food by the amount**: vegetables, fruit, fish and seafood, eggs, dairy and cheese, grains, flour and honey, or other food. One product, sold by the lb, kg, dozen, bunch or piece. Customers choose how much they want.

Each buyer pays when they buy their part. Buyers can also split with friends in a **Private group**, or open their order to other customers as a **Community share**. When orders close, you set the pickup and delivery days, weigh in if you sell by weight, and hand everyone their part.

Shared buys are part of [Pre-orders](/merchants/pre-orders/overview), so most of what you know about pre-orders applies. This page covers what's different.

<Note>
  Set up and run shared buys in the business portal (business.localpass.ca). Shared buys are for every store that isn't a restaurant.
</Note>

## Where to find it

In the sidebar, **Sell → Pre-orders**, then **New pre-order**. It asks **What are you selling?**:

- **Pre-order**: sell stock before it arrives. See [Pre-orders](/merchants/pre-orders/overview).
- **Shared buying**: buyers split a whole animal, or order food by the amount and split it.

Farms, farmers markets and butchers see **Shared buying** first. If you turned off Pre-orders and kept Shared buying on in **Settings → Features**, **New pre-order** goes straight to **What are you splitting?**

In the Pre-orders list, shared buys sit with your other pre-orders.

## Before you start

- **Your payment account** must be set up, as for any pre-order. You can build a shared buy while you wait, but you can't open it.
- **Pre-order settings** give every new shared buy its pickup locations, delivery zones and policies. Open them from **Settings** on the Pre-orders page, or **Settings → Pre-orders → Pre-order settings**. See [Pre-order settings](/merchants/pre-orders/settings).

## Step by step: run a shared buy of an animal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the animal">
    Press **New pre-order**, choose **Shared buying**, then **Meat**. Under **Which animal?**, pick one. We fill in the usual sizes, price and dates. You can change any of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shares">
    Choose **How you price it**, the **Sizes you sell** and their prices, and how many animals. Under **Ways to buy**, choose whether buyers can split with friends or start community splits. See [Set up the shares](#set-up-the-shares).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pickup & delivery">
    Check your pickup locations and delivery zones. They come from your Pre-order settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dates">
    Set when orders close, the **Processing day** and where it's processed, and the day it's ready at each place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut sheet">
    The first time you sell this animal, set up your cut sheet. After that, every shared buy of this animal uses it. See [Cut sheets](/merchants/pre-orders/cut-sheets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page & payment">
    Add the title, the story and photos, how buyers pay, and the animal's profile if you like. Then press **Open shared buy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it and follow it">
    Share the link from **Updates & promote**. Follow how it fills on **Overview**, and who bought on **Buyers**. See [Groups, updates and promoting](/merchants/pre-orders/groups-and-promote).
  </Step>
  <Step title="After processing">
    If you sell by weight, enter the weight on **Weigh-in** and send the final bills. Print the cut sheets for your processor. Then pack from the **Pack list** and hand everyone their share.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Next** saves each step and moves on to the next one. The tabs appear once it's open.

## Start a shared buy

**What are you splitting?** has one tile for each kind. One product per shared buy, and customers choose how much they want.

| Tile | Sold |
| --- | --- |
| **Meat** | A whole or part animal, a flock of birds, or boxes of cut meat. Opens **Which animal?** |
| **Vegetables** | By the lb, the bunch or the piece. |
| **Fruit** | By the lb or the piece. |
| **Fish & seafood** | By the lb or the dozen. |
| **Eggs** | By the dozen. |
| **Dairy & cheese** | By the lb or the piece. |
| **Grains, flour & honey** | By the lb, kg or piece. |
| **Other food** | Anything else, by the amount. |

A shared buy of an animal is set up in five steps: **Shares**, **Pickup & delivery**, **Dates**, **Cut sheet** and **Page & payment**. Food by the amount has four: **What you're selling**, **Pickup & delivery**, **Dates** and **Page & payment**.

## Set up the shares

The **Shares** step (and tab) starts with the animal on one line, for example "Beef · To be processed". **Change** lets you pick another animal or, while it's a draft, choose **The meat**:

- **To be processed**: sell shares before the processing day. You can use a weigh-in and cut sheets.
- **Already cut and frozen**: the meat is cut, wrapped and in the freezer. A set price, ready now, until it sells out or a date. No weigh-in and no cut sheet.

### How you price it

- **Set price**: one price per share. Nothing to weigh.
- **By weight**: a price per lb. Buyers pay an estimate now. After processing you enter the weight and each bill is settled.

### Sizes

- **Sizes you sell**: tick the sizes, such as half, quarter and eighth. The smallest one you tick is one piece, so "The animal is 8 eighths. A half uses 4."
- Each size shows **About** how much meat it holds and its price. **Price per lb (optional)** works out each size's price from its meat. You can type your own price instead.
- **Sold out** stops new orders for a size.
- **Birds**: choose **By the bird**, **Bird or a box**, or **Boxes only**, and **Birds in a box**.
- **Other ways to sell: any amount in pounds, or boxes**:
  - **Let buyers choose any amount**: buyers pick how many pounds they want, from your smallest order up, in your steps. Set **About the whole animal**, **In steps of**, **Smallest order**, **Most per order** and **Price per lb**. Your sizes become **Quick picks**.
  - **Sell boxes instead** ("20 lb box"): name the box and how many boxes there are per animal.
- At the bottom, how many animals are in this shared buy. A share never spans two animals, and the shares can never add up to more than the animal.

**Extras** are paid add-ons a buyer can take with their share, such as liver, heart or tongue. Each has its own price and limit.

### Once someone has bought a share

What was sold stays fixed: the animal, how you price it, and each size's pieces. **What's fixed?** lists it. You can still change:

- prices, for new buyers (everyone keeps the price they paid);
- the smallest and largest order, the quick picks and **Sold out**;
- new sizes, added at the end;
- everything on **Animal & dates** and **Settings**.

## Set up food sold by the amount

The **What you're selling** step (and tab) is one card:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **What are you selling?** | Search your products or type any name. Picking a product copies its name, photo and price. |
| **Price** | The price per lb, kg, dozen, bunch or piece. |
| **Smallest order** | "Customers order at least 10 lb, adding 10 lb at a time." |
| **How much do you have?** (optional) | Leave it empty to take every order until orders close. |
| **It's a mix: list what's in it** (optional) | Up to 12 things, separated by commas. You see the line customers see. |
| **Limit how much one customer can order** (optional) | **Most per order**. |

Food sold by the amount is always a set price and paid in full when ordered. There's no deposit, weigh-in or cut sheet.

Once people have ordered, what it's sold by, the smallest order and how much at a time stay as they are. You can still change the name, change the price for new orders (people who already ordered keep theirs), and add more. How much you have can only go up.

**Dates** asks whether it's ready now or still to come, and the harvest, ready or dock day that fits the kind of food. For fish and seafood you can add the boat and where you fish.

## Ways to buy

Every shared buy has a **Ways to buy** section with three rows. Each one says what it is and why it helps you sell.

| Animal | Food by the amount | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy a share** (always on) | **Order for yourself** (always on) | One customer buys any size you offer, or any amount from your smallest order. |
| **Split with friends** | **Split with friends** | A customer buys a share or an amount, pays for their part, and sends friends a private link to pay for the rest. You pack it as one order. |
| **Community split** | **Split with other customers** | A customer opens their share or order on your page so other customers can take part. You pack a bag with each person's name on it. |

To customers, these are **Private group** and **Community share**.

### Split with friends

- **Friends have** 2 to 14 days to pay for their part, counted from when the split starts and never past when orders close. Food by the amount uses 7 days.
- After that, the customer who started is billed for what's left.
- Friends share their part out themselves, so you never weigh their parts.

### Community splits

- **Smallest part per person** (food by the lb: **Smallest bag**): 1 to 50 lb, in steps of 0.5 lb.
- **Open until**: by default, until orders close. Or choose **On a date**, on or before the day orders close. It always ends by the start of the day before pickup, so you have a day to pack the bags.
- **If it isn't full by then** (animals only):
  - **Refund everyone**: everyone gets every cent back, and the share goes back on sale.
  - **Keep what's left**: everyone gets the part they paid for, and the rest of the share is yours to sell.

  Customers are told which one before they pay. On food by the amount, everyone still gets their bag and you don't pack the rest.

A community split is listed on your shared buy's page with the starter's first name and town. Extras can't go in a community split.

Splits with friends can start until a day before orders close. Community splits can start until a day before they end.

### Group price

While a split is on, **Group price** sets how much less everyone in a split pays: 1% to 50% off, or leave it empty for none. "In a split, everyone pays 10% less once a second person joins."

Whoever starts a split pays the regular price. When someone joins, they get the difference back. Buying alone stays at the regular price.

## Payment

On **Page & payment**:

- **Buyers pay** **In full** or a **Deposit** (10–90% of the order). By weight, a buyer paying in full pays the estimate now. After the weigh-in, anyone who owes more gets a bill and anyone who paid more gets the difference back.
- **When the rest is due**, with a deposit:
  - **After the weigh-in** (by weight): we bill each buyer for the rest at the real weight once you enter the weigh-in. They have 3 days to pay.
  - **After they reserve** (set price): we bill the rest as soon as the deposit is paid.
  - **Before pickup or delivery** or **By a date**: we email the bill for the rest once the order has a date.

  If someone doesn't pay it by then, they get reminders. After that you can cancel their order from **Buyers** and keep the deposit.
- **Only some sizes** lets you choose which sizes take a deposit. The others pay in full.
- **Card processing**: the same choice as for any pre-order. See [Pre-order settings](/merchants/pre-orders/settings#card-processing).

A **What buyers see** line sums it up. Everyone in a split pays their own part in full. Food by the amount is always paid in full when ordered.

## Open it

Press **Open shared buy**. The **Open this shared buy?** box says when buyers can lock in a share and until when. It shows on the Local Pass Market as a shared buy, and "Orders are open" is posted under Updates. If something is missing, you see **Fix these to open it**.

After it opens you can still change prices and settings for new orders. If you close it by mistake, press **Reopen** and choose a new order-by time.

## While it's open

An open shared buy has these tabs: **Overview**, **Buyers**, **Cut sheets** (while cut sheets are on), **Shares** (or **What you're selling**), **Animal & dates** (or **Dates**), **Updates & promote**, **Weigh-in** (by weight, once orders close), **Delivery**, **Pack list** and **Settings**.

### Overview

- **How it's going**:
  - **Taken**: how much is sold, of how much, and by how many people.
  - **Open to join** (with community splits on): what's left to join in community shares, plus what's held for private groups.
  - **Collected**: the money paid in, less refunds.
  - **Not sold yet**: only while something is unsold.

  A bar shows taken, left to join, held for a private group and not sold. With several animals, there's one bar per animal.
- **To do**: only what needs you, such as **Enter the weight**, **Set the ready day**, unpaid orders (**See them**) and private groups whose hold ends within 3 days.
- **Closing soon**: in the last 3 days before community shares close with room left, an amber banner says how much is left to join and offers **Promote them**.
- **Groups**: every private group and community share. See [Groups](/merchants/pre-orders/groups-and-promote#groups).
- On the side: **Dates**, **Shares** (or **What you're selling**), **Extras** and the latest of your **Updates**, each with **Edit**.

### When orders close with some left

If orders close with shares (or an amount) still left, the Overview says "Orders closed with … left." Choose:

- **Go ahead with** what's sold;
- **More time**, to reopen with a new order-by time;
- **Cancel and refund**, which refunds everyone.

### Buyers

One row per order: the customer, their part, their group, the total and the payment. In a group, the organizer's row says "Organizer · 3 more in the group", and a friend's row says which group they're in. Filter by group to see one group's orders. When everyone picks up the same day at the same place, that's said once above the table.

Open an order to see everything about it. For a split, it shows each person's part and what they paid. Depending on the order, you can:

- **Remind** someone to pay, or **Release** an unpaid reservation (not once a friend in the split has paid).
- Refund one person's part.
- **Take off the community list**: nobody new finds the community share on your page. Anyone with the organizer's link can still join until it closes.
- **Lower the amount** on food by the amount, when you have less to give. We refund the difference to their card. This isn't offered on a community share.
- **Cancel this order**, or **Cancel this community share** (everyone who paid gets their part back in full).

Refunds come from your payment account balance. Local Pass's fee and the card fee aren't returned.

### Weigh-in

For an animal sold by weight, open **Weigh-in** once orders close. Enter the meat weight of the whole animal from the butcher's bill. We split it by the share each person bought and show the bill for each order before anything is sent. Change an order's weight if the butcher weighed it on its own.

Press **Send final bills**. A deposit order gets its bill for the rest at the real weight. An order paid in full that weighed less is refunded the difference. Orders not paid yet are skipped. Pressing again sends corrected bills. Nobody who has paid is charged more by a correction.

With several animals, weigh each one. A buyer with shares of two animals gets one bill, once both are weighed.

### Delivery and pack list

Set the pickup and delivery dates on **Delivery**, as for any pre-order. See [Dates, pack list and packing](/merchants/pre-orders/dates-and-packing).

On the **Pack list**, each shared-buy order is one box: who it's for, their share, and who picks it up. A private group stays one box, collected by one person in the group. A community share is packed as one bag per person, each with its own label. On delivery, every bag goes to the customer who started the split. A split's cuts are shared out by each person's part.

## Cancel a shared buy

In the **⋯** menu, **Cancel shared buy** cancels every order, and everyone gets back what they paid. They're emailed. This can't be undone.

## What buyers see

Your shared buy has its own page on localpass.ca, with your story, the animal or the food, what's left, the dates, and the ways to buy. See [Shared buys](/customers/shared-buys) in the customer guide.
