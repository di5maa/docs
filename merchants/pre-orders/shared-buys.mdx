title: "Shared buys"
description: "Sell one animal in shares before it goes to the butcher. Set the sizes and prices, take payment as people lock in, weigh in, take cut sheets, and pack one box or bag per buyer."
---

A shared buy is a kind of pre-order for meat. You sell one animal (or a batch, or a flock of birds) in shares: a half, a quarter, an eighth, a bird, a box, or any amount in pounds. Each buyer pays when they lock in their share. Once it's spoken for, you take the animal to be processed, set the pickup and delivery days, and hand each buyer their meat.

You can also sell meat that's already cut and frozen the same way, at a set price per share.

Shared buys are part of [Pre-orders](/merchants/pre-orders/overview), so most of what you already know about pre-orders applies. This page covers what's different. Shared buys work the same in the business portal (business.localpass.ca) and in the Local Pass Business app. Where the two differ, this guide says so.

<Note>
  The app version of shared buys described here is not in the app stores yet. Until it is, use the portal.
</Note>

## Where to find it

- **Portal:** in the sidebar, **Sell → Pre-orders**, then **New pre-order**.
- **App:** in the menu, under **SELL**, tap **Pre-orders**, then **New pre-order**.

**New pre-order** asks **What are you selling?** Choose **Shared buy**. Farms, farmers markets and butchers see **Shared buy** first. Every other store (except restaurants) sees **Products for a drop** first, which is an ordinary pre-order.

In the Pre-orders list, a shared buy shows "Shared buy" and how full it is, for example "Shared buy · 3 of 8 eighths taken" or "About 110 of 440 lb taken". With no photo, its picture is a drawing of the animal.

## Before you start

- **Your payment account.** Payments go straight into your own payment account, so it must be set up, and Local Pass must turn on direct payments for your store. Until then, **Open pre-order** stays greyed out. If you see "Local Pass needs to turn on direct payments for your store before this can open", email support@imlocal.ca. You can build the shared buy while you wait.
- **Pre-order settings.** A new shared buy copies your pickup locations, delivery zones and card processing choice from [Pre-order settings](/merchants/pre-orders/settings). Set those up first.

## Step by step: run a shared buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Press **New pre-order**, choose **Shared buy**, then choose whether the meat is **To be processed** or **Already cut and frozen**, and pick the animal. A draft opens on its **Shares** tab with the usual sizes ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the shares and prices">
    On **Shares**, check the sizes you sell, the meat in the whole animal and the price of each size. Choose **Fixed price per share** or **By weight**. Add any **Extras**, such as liver or a tongue. Decide whether buyers can **Buy with friends** and use **Community splits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    On **Animal & dates**, set when **Orders close**, the **Processing day** and, if you know them, the **Ready on** day for each pickup place and delivery zone. Add a name, a few words and photos of the animal if you like. Turn on cut sheets here if you want buyers to choose their cuts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check payment">
    On **Settings**, check **Payment**. Buyers pay in full when they lock in, or you can take a deposit on the bigger sizes. Check your pickup places, delivery zones and policies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it and share the link">
    Press **Save**, then **Open pre-order**, and confirm with **Open it**. Press **Copy link** and share the page. If something is missing, you see **Fix these to open it** with what to fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it fill">
    The **Overview** tab shows how much is taken and the money collected. Each order appears on the **Buyers** tab. Post news about the animal from **Updates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close orders">
    Orders close at the time you set, or at midnight before the processing day at the latest. If shares are left, choose to go ahead with what's sold, give people more time, or cancel and refund everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect cut sheets">
    If you turned on cut sheets, check the **Cut sheets** tab before the deadline. Fill one in for anyone who phones you. Download the PDF for your processor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weigh in">
    If you sell by weight, enter the meat weight from the butcher's bill on the **Weigh-in** tab and press **Send final bills**. Each buyer is billed for the rest, or refunded the difference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the ready days">
    On the **Delivery** tab, press **Set date** for each place and **Save and email them**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack and hand over">
    Pack from the **Pack list**: one box per order, or one bag per person on a community split, with a label for each bag. When a buyer collects, press **Picked up** on their order. It appears once the order is paid in full. An order that still owes shows "Owes \$… — collect it before the box goes" on the pack list. A by-weight order is fully paid only after the weigh-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start a shared buy

After **Shared buy**, the dialog asks **Which animal?** "We fill in the usual sizes and timing. You can change every one of them."

First choose **The meat**:

- **To be processed** (the usual choice): you sell shares before the processing day. Weigh-in and cut sheets are available.
- **Already cut and frozen**: the meat is cut, wrapped and in the freezer. It sells at a set price per share, ready now, until it sells out or a date. There's no processing day, weigh-in or cut sheets.

Then pick the animal. Each one starts with these settings, and you can change all of them:

| Animal | Sizes ticked to start | Meat (about) | Any amount (steps, smallest) | Orders close | Ready |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beef | Half, quarter, eighth | 440 lb | 5 lb, from 20 lb | 7 days before processing | 14 days after |
| Bison | Half, quarter, eighth | 350 lb | 5 lb, from 20 lb | 7 days before | 14 days after |
| Pork | Whole, half, quarter | 150 lb | 5 lb, from 10 lb | 7 days before | 7 days after |
| Lamb | Whole, half | 40 lb | 1 lb, from 5 lb | 7 days before | 7 days after |
| Goat | Whole, half | 35 lb | 1 lb, from 5 lb | 7 days before | 7 days after |
| Chicken | One bird, box of 10 | 4.5 lb each, 20 birds | Not offered | 3 days before | 1 day after |
| Turkey | One bird | 16 lb each, 10 birds | Not offered | 3 days before | 1 day after |
| Duck | One bird, box of 6 | 5 lb each, 10 birds | Not offered | 3 days before | 1 day after |
| Other | Whole, half | You set it | 1 lb steps | 7 days before | 7 days after |

The new draft is named after the animal ("Beef shares"; "Meat shares" for Other) and opens on **Shares**. It starts with:

- your pickup places and delivery zones from Pre-order settings, with no dates yet
- payment in full by card when buyers lock in
- **Buy with friends** and **Community splits** on
- one animal
- a product for each size, with no price yet

A shared buy has its own tabs: **Overview**, **Buyers**, **Cut sheets** (while cut sheets are on), **Shares**, **Animal & dates**, **Updates**, **Weigh-in** (when you sell by weight), **Delivery**, **Pack list** and **Settings**. A draft always opens on **Shares**; once it's open, it opens on **Overview**.

## Shares

This is where you decide what one buyer can lock in and what it costs. Beside the settings, **What buyers see** previews your page, with the hand-drawn animal that fills in as shares go.

### The animal

- **Animal**: changing it re-cuts the sizes from that animal's defaults. For **Other**, type the animal's name (for example "Rabbit"). You can't open the buy until it's named.
- **The meat**: **To be processed** or **Already cut and frozen**. You can change it only while it's a draft and nobody holds a share.
- **How you price it**:
  - **Fixed price per share**: "One price per share. No weighing, one payment. Buyers see about how much meat it is."
  - **By weight**: you set a price per lb. Buyers pay the estimate when they lock in (or a deposit, on the sizes that take one). After processing, you enter the weight from the butcher's bill and each buyer is settled on it.
  - Frozen meat is always a set price per share.
- **Let buyers choose any amount** (not for birds): buyers pick how many pounds they want, from your smallest amount up. Your sizes become quick picks.

Once anyone has locked in a share, the animal, how you price it and the any-amount switch are fixed.

### Share sizes

For beef, bison, pork, lamb, goat and other animals, tick the **Sizes you sell**: **Whole**, **Half**, **Quarter**, **Eighth**. The smallest size you tick is one piece. With half, quarter and eighth ticked, the animal is 8 eighths, a half uses 4 and a quarter uses 2.

For birds, choose **By the bird**, **Bird or a box** or **Boxes only**, and set **Birds in a box**.

The calculator row works out prices for you:

- **Meat in the whole animal** (birds: **Meat per bird**) sets each size's "About" pounds.
- **Price per lb** is optional. With a fixed price, each size's price is the price per lb times its meat. By weight, each size starts at that price per lb.
- Type a different price on a size to change just that one. It shows "your own price", with a link to put the worked-out price back.

Buyers are billed on each size's price. The price per lb is only there to help you set it.

To sell boxes instead of halves and quarters, open **Sell boxes instead (“20 lb box”)**, give the box a **Name**, set **Boxes per animal** and press **Use it**. **Back to halves and quarters** undoes it. You can **Add a size** for boxes and birds, up to 6 sizes.

Each size has a **Size** name, **About** (pounds of meat), **Price a share** (by weight: **Price / lb**) and **Sold out**. The line under each size shows what buyers see, for example "About 110 lb of meat · \$1,366". When customers cover card processing, the price buyers see includes it.

### Any amount

With **Let buyers choose any amount** on, the **Any amount** panel replaces the sizes:

- **About the whole animal**: the meat you expect from it.
- **In steps of**: whole pounds, 1 to 50.
- **Smallest order**: required, and a multiple of your step.
- **Most per order**: optional. Leave it empty for up to what's left.
- **Price per lb** and **Sold out**.
- **Quick picks**: buttons such as "a quarter" so buyers can pick one in a tap. A pick outside your smallest and largest order is hidden.

Buyers see something like "From 20 lb · up to 100 lb". If fewer pounds are left than your smallest order, one last buyer can take exactly what's left. The whole animal can't be oversold.

### The whole animal and how many animals

The **The whole animal** panel shows the cut in words, for example "The animal is 8 eighths. A half uses 4, a quarter 2.", and how much is left. The shares can't add up to more than the animal. Buyers see a drawing that fills in as shares go. Nobody sees who bought what.

For birds and boxes, set **Birds in all** or **How many in all** (up to 200).

To sell more than one animal at once, set **Animals in this buy** under **How many animals?** (1 to 100; not for birds). Each share comes from one animal, never two. New shares fill the fullest animal first, so animals finish one by one.

### Extras

Extras are organs and special parts buyers can add to their share, like liver, heart, tongue, oxtail, bones or lard. Nobody can buy an extra on its own. Extras aren't offered on a community split.

- Tap a suggestion for your animal, or **+ Add your own**. Suggestions don't carry a price, so set one.
- **Priced**: **Per lb** (a price per lb and about how much it weighs; a set price, never weighed) or **Per item**.
- **One lamb gives** (the label names your animal): how many one animal has, 1 to 20. With a batch, the total is that number times the animals.
- **Most per order** and **Sold out**.

You can have up to 12 extras. Once one has sold, you can't remove it: switch on **Sold out** instead.

### The products made for each size

Each size and each extra is its own product, named after the animal and the size ("Beef · Quarter", "Beef · Liver"). These products are **Pre-order only**: they show nowhere but your pre-order pages. They're made, renamed and re-priced for you when you save the **Shares** tab, so always change them there.

- A size or extra you remove is archived when you save, as long as nobody holds one.
- Deleting a draft shared buy archives every product made for it.
- Regular products can't be added to a shared buy. It sells only its own shares and extras.

In the app, their name, price and "Sold as" are locked, with the note "Set on the shared buy’s Shares tab." and an **Open the shared buy** button. You can still change their status there. In the portal you can still open them in the product editor, but their name and price are rewritten from the **Shares** tab the next time you save it.

### Once shares are sold, the cut is fixed

As soon as anyone holds a share, a box at the top of **Shares** says the cut is fixed. Every buyer locked in their share as part of this cut, so changing it could sell more than the whole animal, or change what someone already bought.

| Stays as it is | Still yours to change |
| --- | --- |
| The animal and how you price it | Prices, for new buyers (everyone keeps the price they paid) |
| How many pieces each size uses (and its pounds, by weight) | **Sold out**, and size names |
| The sizes you have: mark one **Sold out** instead of removing it | About how much meat, on a fixed price |
| The whole animal: you can add birds or boxes, not take them away | New sizes, added at the end (a whole number of the pieces already sold) |
| Any amount, in the same steps and pounds | The smallest and largest order, quick picks and **Sold out** |
| The number of animals: you can add, not take away | Everything on **Animal & dates** and **Settings** |

If someone locks in a share while you're editing, your save keeps your other changes and puts the sizes, pieces and pricing back the way they were sold. You see "Someone locked in a share just before you saved…" with **See the shares**.

## Payment

On a shared buy, **Settings → Payment** works a little differently from other pre-orders.

- **How buyers pay**: by card when they reserve. A share is held only once it's paid for, so there's no "reserve now, pay later" on a shared buy.
- **Buyers pay**: **Pay now in full** (by weight: **Pay now (the estimate)**), or **Deposit, then the rest**.
- **Deposit**: 10 to 90% of the order. It starts at 30%.
- **Which sizes take a deposit**: the others pay in full when they reserve. It starts with the sizes of at least half the animal (wholes and halves). Splits with friends or the community always pay in full.
- **When the rest is due**:
  - **After the weigh-in** (by weight only): each buyer is billed for the rest at the real weight once you enter the weigh-in. They have 3 days to pay.
  - **Before pickup or delivery**, a number of days before: we email the bill for the rest once the order has a date.
  - **By a date**: we email the bill for the rest once the order has a date, not on its own before that.
  - **After they reserve** (set price only): 1 to 60 days after; the bill goes out as soon as the deposit is paid.

A green line, **What buyers see**, sums it up, for example "Half: 30% when they reserve, the rest 7 days before pickup or delivery. Quarter, Eighth: in full when they reserve."

If someone doesn't pay the rest by its due date, they get reminders. After that, you can cancel their order from **Buyers** and keep the deposit.

**Card processing** is the same choice as other pre-orders: **Customers cover it** or **I’ll cover it**. See [Pre-order settings](/merchants/pre-orders/settings#card-processing).

There's no dollar minimum on a shared buy: the smallest share or amount is the minimum.

## Animal & dates

### When people can order

This is the one place to set it on a shared buy.

- **Orders open**: **Now** (when you press **Open pre-order**) or **On a date**.
- **Orders close**: **On a date**, or **When every share is taken** (**When the whole animal is taken** for any amount).

**When every share is taken** still has a latest time: midnight before the processing day. For frozen meat, it's the end of your last pickup or delivery day, or 90 days after it opens if no day is set.

A full shared buy doesn't close by itself. It stays open so a cancellation can free a share, and new orders are refused while it's full. Buyers see "Every share is taken" and "A cancellation frees one up — check back."

When you pick a processing day, **Orders close** is filled in for you: 7 days before at 8 p.m. (3 days for birds). Change it if you like. Orders must close before the processing day.

### Dates

Buyers see every change here under **Updates** on your page.

- **Processing day**: the day the animal goes to the butcher. Not on frozen meat: buyers see "Ready now" and your first pickup or delivery day instead.
- **Processed at** (frozen: **Cut and wrapped at**): your processor's name, shown on your page.
- **Ready on** (frozen: **Pickup dates**): one date for each pickup place and delivery zone. Buyers see the one for where they get their share. A **Use** button with a date (for example **Use Sat, Nov 21**, or **Use Sat, Nov 21 for the rest** when you have more than one place) fills every empty place with the processing day plus the usual days for that animal. You can set these later.

Once anyone has ordered, or once orders are closed, change the ready days from the **Delivery** tab instead (**Open Delivery**), so each buyer is emailed.

After orders close, this tab has its own **Save**. You can still update the animal, the processing day, the processor and the cut sheets, but not when orders open or close.

### The animal's profile

All optional: a **Name** (the panel then reads "Meet Daisy"), **A few words** about how it was raised (tap a starter such as "Raised on pasture…"), up to 6 **Photos** (the first one leads), and **Where it lives**, with an optional pin on the map. With a batch, this is one profile for the whole batch.

### Each animal in a batch

With more than one animal, a panel lists each one. Give each its own **Name**, **Tag / ID**, **Breed**, **Sex**, **Born** (for example 2024-03), a short note and up to 2 photos. Buyers see these on your page, with the birth month only. The buyer list, weigh-in and pack list use each animal's name and tag.

To bring them in from your own records, press **Import**. Upload a spreadsheet (one animal per row) or paste rows, check which column is which, then press **Apply to** _n_ **animals** and **Save**. There are Excel and CSV templates to start from. Importing more rows than animals makes the batch bigger while it's a draft or open, never once closed. In the app, import is CSV or pasted rows only.

The number of animals itself is set on **Shares**, under **How many animals?**

## Open, close and cancel

- **Open pre-order** opens **Open this pre-order?** It tells you when buyers can lock in, that it shows on the Local Pass Market as a shared buy, and that "Orders are open" is posted under **Updates**. Press **Open it**.
- **Close now** (in the **⋯** menu) stops orders and takes it off the Market. Orders on your own and friends' splits stay as they are. A community split that isn't full yet reaches its deadline at once, and your **If one isn’t full by then** choice applies: with **Cancel it**, everyone in it gets every cent back. With **I’ll take the rest**, you keep the pounds nobody took. The same happens when you press **Go ahead with** what's sold.
- **Reopen** lets buyers order again until a new **Order by** time.
- **Duplicate** makes a new draft with the same sizes, prices, places and payment. It clears every date and the animal's name, note and photos, and opens on **Shares**.
- **Cancel pre-order** cancels every order and refunds every buyer everything they paid. They're emailed. Local Pass's fee and the card fee on those payments aren't returned. This can't be undone.
- **Delete draft** removes a draft for good and archives the products made for it.

## Overview

Once it's open, **Overview** is the board for your shared buy:

- A drawing of the animal, how much is taken (for example "3 of 4 quarters taken") and the money collected, after refunds.
- A line with what's left, when orders close and how many buyers there are.
- **Where it is**: **Taking orders**, **Orders closed**, **Processing**, **Weighed** (by weight only) and **Ready**.
- With a batch, a tile for each animal: sold, held for a group, or open. The one filling now has a ring.
- **Buying with friends**: every split and who's in it.
- Cards for the **Shares**, the animal and the **Extras**, each with an **Edit** link.
- Buttons: **Post an update**, **Enter the weight** (by weight, once closed), **Set the ready day** (until one is set) and **Buyers →**.

### When orders close with shares left

A box says how much is left, with three choices:

- **Go ahead with** what's sold: closes orders and you go ahead.
- **More time**: reopens it with a new closing time.
- **Cancel and refund**: cancels the whole shared buy and refunds everyone.

## Buyers

The **Buyers** tab works like the [Customers tab](/merchants/pre-orders/customers-and-payments) of other pre-orders, with a few additions. Some messages on it still say "Customers"; they mean the same tab.

- A **Picking up** column shows who collects each order ("Themselves" if the buyer does).
- With a batch, filter by **Every animal**.
- A split shows as one order: the organizer's. It carries an **Organizer** chip and a line for each person, with their part and a state: "paid", "paying", "owes" (with the amount) or "refunded". The Overview's **Buying with friends** list shows the same states as **Paid**, **Paying now**, **Owes** and **Refunded**. You see first names and a last initial.
- By weight, an order paid in full on the estimate shows **Paid · final weight to come** until you weigh in.

### Refunds and cancelling

Refunds on a shared buy are for when you can't provide the meat. Buyers can't cancel themselves.

- **Cancel and refund** on one order: the buyer gets back what you choose, from your payment account balance. When a deposit order's balance is overdue, the refund starts at what they paid less the deposit, so you can keep the deposit.
- On a split, **Refund their part** refunds one person in full (Local Pass's fee and the card fee aren't returned). On a friends' split, their part is billed to the organizer: when the joining time ends, or at once if it has already ended. On a community split that's still open, someone else can take their pounds. Once a community split has closed, a refunded part stays with you, in nobody's bag and billed to nobody.
- **Cancel the split and refund everyone** (on a friends' split, **Cancel and refund everyone**) gives everyone on a split their own part back in full, and the whole share goes back on sale.

When you refund or cancel, Local Pass's fee and the card fee aren't returned. The one exception is a community split that doesn't fill by its deadline when you chose **Cancel it**: Local Pass gives back its fee, and the card fee still comes out of your balance.

## Buy with friends and community splits

Both are on the **Shares** tab, each with its own switch. A split is one order: the person who starts it (the organizer) books a share, keeps their part and others take the rest. Everyone pays for their own part in full when they join. Splits never take a deposit.

### Buy with friends

Switch on **Let buyers buy with friends**. The organizer splits a share with people they know, through a private link. You pack one box for the whole group.

- **Friends can join for**: 2 to 14 days (7 to start), never past when orders close.
- When the joining time is up, anything friends haven't taken is billed to the organizer, so you're paid for the whole share. The organizer gets a reminder 48 hours before.

### Community splits

Switch on **Allow community splits**. The organizer opens their share on your shared-buy page, so neighbours can take part. Small households can take a few pounds, so shares fill that otherwise wouldn't.

- The split is listed with the organizer's first name and town only.
- **Smallest part per person**: 1 to 50 lb, in half-pound steps (5 lb to start). Whoever takes the last of a share can take less.
- **Must be full by**: a date, never after orders close. **Use the usual date** sets it 14 days before orders close. A split also closes by the day before its pickup or delivery day.
- **If one isn’t full by then**:
  - **Cancel it**: everyone in the split gets every cent back, and the share goes back on sale.
  - **I’ll take the rest**: nobody is refunded or billed. You keep the pounds nobody took.
- You pack one bag per person, each with its own label. Each person picks up their own bag, or the organizer collects it for them. On delivery, all the bags go to the organizer.
- Extras aren't offered on a community split.
- Up to 10 community splits can be listed at once, and each takes up to 11 people besides the organizer.

To stop new people finding a split, open the order on **Buyers** and press **Take off the community list**. Anyone with the organizer's link can still join until the deadline. You can't put it back on the list.

Changes to these settings apply only to splits started after them.

## Cut sheets

Cut sheets let buyers tell you how they want their share cut. They're optional and aren't offered on frozen meat.

<Note>
  In the app, you can't turn cut sheets on yet. Turn them on in the portal; after that, the app shows the settings and the **Cut sheets** tab.
</Note>

On **Animal & dates**, under **Cut sheets**, switch on **Ask buyers how they want it cut**. Then:

- **Who fills one in**: **Halves and wholes** (orders with at least half an animal) or **Every share**. For birds, every buyer fills one in.
- **Choices close**: 1 to 30 days before processing (7 to start). Buyers can choose until the end of that day. Without a processing day, there's no deadline yet.
- **Questions**: the questions for your animal, such as steak thickness, roasts or ground, and soup bones, each with your standard cut highlighted. **Hide** any you don't offer; everyone then gets the standard cut for it.
- **Note for buyers**: the question above a short note box, for example "Anything else for the butcher?" Leave it blank for no note.

Shares that don't fill one in get your standard cuts. Choices don't change the price.

The **Cut sheets** tab lists every order that needs one, marked **Done**, **Waiting** or **Standard cuts**. Press **Fill in** or **Edit** to fill one in for a buyer, even after the deadline. Press **Download PDF** for one page per order for your processor, or **Export** for Excel or CSV (CSV only in the app). The sheets show names and order numbers only, never contact details or money.

**What buyers get:** their payment receipt asks them to choose their cuts by the deadline, and a reminder goes out about 3 days before it. On a split, the organizer chooses for the whole share.

## Updates

On **Updates**, write a note about the animal (up to 500 characters) and add a photo if you like, then press **Post**. With a batch, choose whether it's about all animals or one.

"Buyers see this on the page. People who’ve paid get it by email, and as an app notification if they have a Local Pass account. Changes close together go out as one message, at most 3 a day; after that it shows on the page only." You can post up to 10 updates a day.

Some changes are posted for you, such as "Orders are open.", a new processing day or ready day, a new photo, a price change, "All 4 quarters are taken." and "Weighed: 412 lb of meat." Your own notes, a new processing day and "it's a go" (every share taken, or orders closed) are emailed to people who've paid. Buyers can stop update emails; their receipts, bills and dates still come.

## Weigh-in

The **Weigh-in** tab shows only when you sell by weight. You can enter the weight once orders have closed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the meat weight">
    Type the **Meat weight** of the whole animal from the butcher's bill. It's split between the shares by the share each person bought.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each order">
    Under **Each order**, change one order's weight only if the butcher weighed it on its own. Unpaid orders aren't billed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the bills">
    Press **Send final bills** and confirm. Each buyer is emailed their final bill, and anyone who paid more than their share is refunded the difference. "Weighed: …" is posted under **Updates**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Made a mistake? Change the weight and press **Send corrected bills**. Anyone who already paid their balance can only get money back, never a new bill.

With a batch, weigh one animal at a time: **Pick an animal**, enter its weight and press **Save Daisy’s weight** (the button names the animal). A buyer with shares of two animals gets one bill, once both are weighed.

Use the **Weigh-in** tab rather than typing final weights into one buyer's order. Weights typed in one order bill that order only. The app warns you about this; the portal doesn't.

## Delivery and Pack list

The **Delivery** tab works as for other pre-orders: press **Set date** on each place, then **Save and email them**. On a shared buy with a deposit, setting the date also sends the bill for the rest, unless the share is sold by weight and hasn't been weighed yet: that bill goes out with the weigh-in. See [Dates, pack list and packing](/merchants/pre-orders/dates-and-packing).

On the **Pack list**, each shared-buy order is one box. Its card shows the share in words ("Half", "35 lb"), who's picking it up, which animal it comes from, anything still owing ("collect it before the box goes") and the cut sheet's status.

A community split is one bag per person. Its card lists each bag with the person's name, about how much goes in it and how it leaves (they pick it up, or the organizer collects it). Pounds nobody took stay with you and go in nobody's bag. Buyers can change how they get their bag until the weigh-in (by weight) or until the day before the ready day, so print labels after that. **Print pick list, slips and bag labels** prints a label for each bag.

In the app, you can also pack with [Packing mode](/merchants/packing-mode). See [Pack list](/merchants/pack-list).

## What buyers see

On your shared-buy page on localpass.ca, buyers see the animal (with its name, photos and where it lives), a drawing that fills in as shares go, the dates and your updates. They choose how to buy:

- **Buy on your own**: any size you offer, up to the whole animal. Their own box.
- **Join the community**: take part of a share a neighbour opened, from your smallest part. They pay only for their part.
- **Buy with friends**: split one share with people they know, from a private link.

Each size shows about how much meat it is, the price and about what that works out to per lb, for example "About 55 lb of meat · \$480 (~\$8.73/lb)". The button reads **Lock in**, for example **Lock in · Quarter**. Buyers can add extras to their share.

| What you do | What the buyer gets |
| --- | --- |
| They lock in and pay | A receipt email. By weight, it says the share is settled on the final weight. |
| You set the date | An email and a text with the date. |
| You send a balance or final bill | "Your final bill from" your store, with a **Pay the rest** button, and a text. |
| They weighed less | An email saying how much is coming back to their card. |
| A friends' split ends with parts left | The organizer gets a bill for the rest of the share. |
| A community split doesn't fill | Everyone gets every cent back, or (with **I’ll take the rest**) an email saying it has closed and nobody pays more. |
| You refund or cancel | An email with the amount coming back. |

Every email carries your store's name, and replies go to you.

## Portal and app differences

| | Portal | App |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turning cut sheets on | Yes | Not yet: turn them on in the portal |
| Cut sheet downloads | PDF, Excel, CSV | PDF and CSV |
| Importing animals | Excel or CSV, or pasted rows | CSV or pasted rows |
| Share products in the product editor | Can be opened; rewritten from **Shares** on save | Name, price and "Sold as" locked; status can be changed |
| Final weights in one order | No warning | Warns to use **Weigh-in** |
| Pack list ticks | This device only | Shared across your devices |
| Packing mode | No | Yes |
