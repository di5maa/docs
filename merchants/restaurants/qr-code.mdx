---
title: "QR code menu"
---

Local Pass makes it easy to share your digital menu with customers. The platform automatically generates a unique QR code for your restaurant, linking directly to your customer-facing online store.

## Accessing Your QR Code

You can find your store's unique code within the **Menu** section of your Merchant Dashboard.

## Sharing Options

Once you access the QR code section, you have several ways to use it:

1. **Save Image**
   - Download a high-quality image of your QR code directly to your local device (phone or computer). This is perfect for adding to custom flyers or table tents.
2. **Print & Share**
   - Use the built-in system options to send the code directly to a connected printer or share it via email, messaging apps, or AirDrop.
3. **Copy Link**
   - Copy the direct URL to your online store. You can paste this link into your Instagram bio, Facebook page, or Google Maps listing so customers can order online immediately.
