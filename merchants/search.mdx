title: "Search your dashboard"
description: "Find any page in your dashboard, a help article or a page of the Food business guide from one search box."
---

The search box sits under the Local Pass logo in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the magnifier in the top bar.

You can also open it from anywhere with a keyboard shortcut:

- **⌘ K** on a Mac
- **Ctrl K** on Windows
- **/**

## What it finds

Type a word, like "payouts", "pack list" or "pre-orders". Results come in three groups:

- **Pages**: every page in your dashboard's sidebar, with a line on what each is for. You only see the pages your kind of business has. Search also finds a few pages inside them, like **Business customers**, **Delivery calendar** and **Statements & accounting export**. Pages for one order, product or pre-order aren't in search: open them from their list.
- **Help articles**: articles from this help site.
- **Food business guide**: pages of the [Food business guide](https://localpass.ca/food-business-bc) on localpass.ca, such as permits, rule changes, food labels, commercial kitchens and farmers' markets. These open in a new tab.

Before you type, it shows **Recent** (pages you opened lately) and **Popular pages**.

Use the up and down arrow keys to move, **Enter** to open, and **Esc** to close.

## Suggest a feature

Search for "suggest", or press **Suggest a feature** at the bottom of the sidebar. Tell us what it's about, what Local Pass should do and how it would help your business. It goes straight to our team, and we reply by email if we have questions. In the app, open **Store settings**: it's in the support list, after **Provide feedback**.
