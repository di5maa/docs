title: "What your customer sees"
description: "Your business customer's own page for their standing order: signing in with an emailed code, signing the agreement, changing or skipping deliveries, pausing, reporting a problem, paying invoices, and the reminder emails."
---

Your customer gets their own page for the standing order. They don't need a Local Pass account: a link and a code sent to their email are enough. From there they can see what's coming, change or skip a delivery before it locks, sign the agreement, report a problem and pay their invoices.

This page walks through it from their side, so you know what they see when you call or email them.

## The link

Once the standing order has started, send your customer their link:

- **Send link**, at the top of the standing order until you've sent one, emails it to the customer's email and any other addresses on it. The email is from your store, titled "[Your store] set up a standing order with you". It says what they'll get and when, and has a button: **Open your standing order**.
- **Copy buyer link** (in the **⋯** menu) or **Copy their link** (on the **Agreement** card) copies it, so you can send it your own way.
- **Send new link…** makes a new link and emails it. The old link stops working, and so does every device signed in with it. Use it if the link went to the wrong person.

## Signing in with a code

The link alone doesn't open the page. The first time they open it on a device, they see **Check it's you**:

1. They press **Email me a code**. If the standing order has more than one email address, they choose where the code goes.
2. They type the 6-digit code from the email and press **Continue**. The code works for 10 minutes. **Send a new code** sends another (after a short wait).
3. That device stays signed in for 30 days. **Sign out** is at the top of their page.

After 5 wrong codes, they have to wait 15 minutes before trying again.

If the standing order has no email for them, they see "We need your email first" and are asked to get you to add one. If the link has been replaced or the standing order is gone, they see "This link doesn't work" and are asked for a new one.

## Their page

At the top: your store's name and logo, **Standing order for [their business]**, and a line with the delivery days and when each delivery locks, for example "Mon, Wed, Fri · each delivery locks 2:00 pm the day before".

A yellow line under it shows anything they need to know:

- "Deliveries start once you accept the agreement. Open the Agreement tab to read it and accept." (when you've chosen to wait for them)
- "Deliveries are paused. Call [your store] to start them again."
- "Not started yet. First delivery from [date]."
- "Last delivery on or before [date]." when there's an end date, or "This standing order ended on [date]." once it has.

Beside the page (below it on a phone) are three cards: **Your supplier** with your phone and email, **Delivery** (or **Pickup**) with the address, time window and delivery charge, and **Billing** with how often you invoice and when it's due.

The page has four tabs: **Next deliveries**, **Invoices**, **Past deliveries** and **Agreement**. A number on **Invoices** shows how many are waiting to be paid, and a **!** on **Agreement** means it isn't accepted yet.

## The agreement

The **Agreement** tab shows "Your agreement with [your store]": the whole agreement, with your words, the products table, the terms and your signature. See [The agreement](/merchants/standing-orders/agreement).

To sign, they read it and fill in the box under it:

- "I agree to this on behalf of [their business]."
- **Your full name**: they type it.
- **Your signature**: they draw it with a finger, a pen or the mouse. **Clear** starts again. Drawing is optional; without it, their typed name stands in its place on the agreement.
- They press **Sign and accept**.

It then reads "Accepted by [name] on [date]." Their signature, name and date appear on the agreement, and in the portal the standing order shows the date they accepted.

If you change the agreement after they've accepted, the box comes back with "Changed since you accepted on [date]. Read it again, then accept the new version." and the button **Sign and accept again**. Their deliveries carry on meanwhile. What counts as a change is listed in [The agreement](/merchants/standing-orders/agreement#when-the-customer-is-asked-to-accept-again).

**Print or save** prints the agreement exactly as they see it, signatures included, or saves it as a PDF. Files you've shared with them (on the standing order's **Documents** tab) are listed under **Files from [your store]**, each with **Download**.

<Note>
If you ticked "Wait until they accept the agreement before the first delivery", no delivery is made until they accept. Their next deliveries read "Starts once you accept the agreement", and in the portal you see **No deliveries until they accept**.
</Note>

## Next deliveries

**Next 14 days** lists each delivery day with what's on it and the total for that delivery (with GST and the delivery charge). Each day shows where it stands:

- "You can change this until [cut-off]": still open.
- "Locked: being prepared": past the cut-off. Only you can change it now.
- "Skipped by you" or "Skipped by [your store]".
- "No delivery: paused".

If a day was changed, it says "Changed by you" or "Changed by [your store]". Public holidays in B.C. are marked.

### Changing or skipping a day

Before the cut-off, a day can have **Change** and **Skip**, depending on what you allow in [Standing order settings](/merchants/standing-orders/settings) ("Before the cut-off, buyers may"). If you've turned one off, its button isn't there.

- **Change** shows each product with "Usually …" beside it. They set the quantities for that day only ("Your usual order stays the same."), can add a **Note for the store (optional)**, and press **Save**. **Back to my usual order** undoes their change.
- **Skip** takes the day off. **Undo skip** puts it back, until the cut-off.

A day you've changed or skipped yourself is yours: they can't change it, and the buttons aren't there. They'll see it in the list, and you can choose to tell them by email.

You see their changes on the standing order in the portal, for example "Skipped by them".

### Pausing

**Skip several days** opens "No deliveries from … to …": a **First day**, a **Last day** and a **Reason (optional)**, such as "Closed for the holidays". They press **Skip these days**. The first day can't be one that has already locked, and a pause can be at most a year.

Their pauses are listed above the days, "set by you", with **Remove**. Pauses you set show "set by [your store]", and they can't remove those. **Skip several days** only appears if you allow customers to pause.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** tab lists the invoices you've sent, newest first: the period, the number of deliveries, the amount and where it stands.

- "Due [date]", or "Overdue 3 days" once the due date has passed.
- "Paid [date]", or for example "Paid by cheque" when you've recorded a payment made outside Local Pass.

An unpaid invoice has **Pay**, which opens its card or bank payment page, and **PDF**. Paid ones keep **PDF**. Invoices you're still checking don't show until you send them. The invoice is also emailed to them when you send it.

They never see the Local Pass fee, your notes or anything you keep for yourself. See [Invoices](/merchants/standing-orders/invoices).

## Past deliveries

**Past deliveries** shows what came on each day and what it cost: "Didn't go out" if you marked it so, when it was delivered, **See the photo** if your driver took one, and anything that was short.

### Reporting a problem

Under the list: "Something short or damaged? Report it up to 7 days after the delivery." Each delivery in that window has **Report a problem**:

1. **Which product?**
2. **What's wrong?**: **Short** or **Damaged**.
3. **How many** (at most what came).
4. A **Note (optional)** and up to 3 photos with **Add photo**.
5. **Send report**.

They see "Thanks. [Your store] has your report." For a delivery that's arriving today, they're asked to report only once it has arrived.

The report then shows under that delivery, with where it stands: **Reported**, **Taken off**, **Credited** or **Closed**. Problems your driver reported show too, marked "reported by the driver". They never see who settled it or why. When you settle a report, they get an email about it. How you handle reports is in [Deliveries](/merchants/standing-orders/deliveries).

## The one-day link

If you turn on **Remind the customer before each cut-off** for a standing order, the customer gets a reminder before each delivery locks, by email, by text or both (text needs their mobile number on the standing order). You choose how long before in [Standing order settings](/merchants/standing-orders/settings) (3, 12, 24 or 48 hours). The reminder only goes for a day that's actually delivering.

- **The email** is titled, for example, "[Your store]: your Fri, Nov 27 delivery locks at 2 pm Thu". It lists what's on that delivery and has a button, **Change or skip this delivery**.
- **The text** says the same in one line, with the link.

The link opens just that one delivery: **Your delivery on [day]**, what's on it and the total. No code is needed. Before the cut-off they can **Change** it (with a note), **Skip this delivery** or **Undo skip**, within what you allow. If you've changed that day yourself, they see "[Your store] changed this delivery. Call them to change it." Your phone number is on the page.

The link closes at the cut-off: "This link has closed. Changes for this day are no longer possible. Call the store if something needs to change." Each person it was sent to gets their own link, and sending a new main link ends all the day links.

At the bottom, **Stop these reminders** turns the reminders off for that standing order. It still works after the link has closed. The page then reads "Reminders are off. [Your store] can turn them back on.", and the standing order's history shows they turned them off.

## Emails your customer gets

Every email comes from your store's name, and replies go to you. Each is only about this standing order: Local Pass never sends your wholesale customers offers or anything else.

| Email | When |
|---|---|
| "[Your store] set up a standing order with you" | you press **Send link** |
| "Your new link to your standing order with [your store]" | you press **Send new link…** |
| "[code] is your code for [your store]" | they ask for a sign-in code |
| "[Your store]: your [day] delivery locks at [time]" | before each cut-off, if reminders are on |
| "Your [day] delivery from [your store] changed" or "No delivery on [day]" | you change or skip one of their days and leave "Tell the customer by email" ticked |
| "No delivery on [day]" | you close for a day, such as a holiday, and tell your customers |
| "New prices from [day]" | you set a price change |
| "Your report about [product] on [day]" | you settle a problem they reported |
| The invoice | you send it |
