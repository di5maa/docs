---
title: "Standing orders"
description: "Supply a business on a schedule: the same products on set days, invoiced once a period under an agreement your customer accepts."
---

A standing order is a regular delivery to a business you supply: a café, a gas station, a university kitchen. You agree what goes out, at what price and on which days. Then it repeats on its own until one of you changes or ends it. Your customer gets one invoice per billing period for everything delivered in it, not a bill per delivery.

Standing orders are for shops and farms. Restaurants don't have them.

## Where to find it

In the sidebar, under **Sell**, press **Standing orders**. It sits just below **Wholesale**.

Your defaults are under **Settings** on the Standing orders page (the gear button), or in **Settings → Standing orders**. See [Standing order settings](/merchants/standing-orders/settings).

## How a week works

1. **Before the cut-off**, each delivery is planned. You can change its quantities or skip it. Your customer can too, if your settings allow it.
2. **At the cut-off** (2:00 pm the day before, unless you change it), the delivery locks. It becomes an order like any other: it shows in your orders, your packing and your delivery routes.
3. **You pack and deliver it**, or the customer picks it up. If something was short, you can adjust what was delivered.
4. **When the billing period ends** (a week, two weeks or a month), the morning after, Local Pass adds up every delivery in it into one invoice. You check it and press **Send**, or it goes out for you, depending on your settings.
5. **Your customer pays** on a card or bank payment page, by the due date. You can also record a cheque, e-transfer or cash payment.

The money goes to your Local Pass balance and is paid out with your other sales.

## What it costs

Your customer pays no fee and never sees one. Local Pass keeps **2% of the products on each invoice, at most \$25 for each week the invoice covers, plus 12% tax on that fee** (5% GST and 7% PST). It comes out of your payout. Your agreement with Local Pass can set a different rate or cap.

- **GST** is added only on the products you tick **GST**. It's 5% and goes to you with the products. Fresh food is usually zero-rated, so the box starts unticked.
- A **delivery charge** goes to you in full. There's no Local Pass fee on it, and no tax.

**An example.** A café gets \$200 of bread three times a week, with a \$10 delivery charge each time, invoiced weekly. The week's invoice is \$600 of bread plus \$30 in delivery charges: **\$630**. The fee is 2% of \$600, which is \$12.00 (under the \$25 weekly cap), plus \$1.44 tax. You receive \$616.56.

On a monthly invoice the cap grows with the month: \$107.14 for a 30-day month. An \$8,000 month pays the capped \$107.14 plus \$12.86 tax. A first or last period that's only partly covered has a smaller cap, in proportion.

## The Standing orders page

At the top: **New standing order**, and **Settings**.

If a B.C. holiday is coming up in the next two weeks and deliveries are still planned for it, a blue banner says so, for example "Victoria Day, Mon May 18: 3 standing deliveries." Press **Skip all**, leave **Tell each customer by email** ticked if you want them told, then press **Skip** with the number of deliveries (for example **Skip 3 deliveries**). Or press **Keep**. Either way the banner goes away for that holiday. A delivery that's already locked isn't skipped; the message names it.

### The tiles

- **Active**: how many standing orders are running.
- **Deliveries this week**: press it to open the **Calendar**.
- **Invoices to check**: invoices waiting for you to press **Send**. Press it to open **Invoices**.
- **Owed**: what customers still owe you, with the overdue part in red. Press it to open **Invoices**.

### Agreements

**Agreements** is the first tab: one row per customer. The status chips above it show how many are in each:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Running. Deliveries are made at each cut-off. |
| **Paused** | No new deliveries until you resume it. |
| **Draft** | Not started yet. Nothing is delivered or invoiced. |
| **Ended** | No more deliveries. Its last invoice still goes out. |
| **Archived** | Ended standing orders you've put away. This chip appears once you've archived one. |

On a computer each row shows the **Customer** (and whether they've accepted: "Accepted Nov 20", "Changed since they accepted" or "Not accepted yet"), the **Schedule**, the **Next** delivery and when changes close, **This period** (deliveries so far and their value) and the **Invoice** and where it stands. On a phone, the same appears as cards. Press a row to open the standing order. The next delivery comes first.

**Search customers** finds a customer by business or contact name.

Press **Filters** to narrow the list. The button shows how many filters are on.

- **Delivers on**: **Any day**, or a day of the week.
- **Delivery or pickup**: **Both**, **Delivery** or **Pickup**.
- **Needs**: **Anything**, **Not accepted yet**, **Invoice to check**, **Owes money** or **Overdue**.

**Clear filters** turns them all off.

### Calendar

**Calendar** shows how many standing deliveries go out each day, by **Week** or **Month**. Press **Today** to come back to this week. Pick a day to see who gets what, and what to make. Below the week, **To make this week** adds up every product per day. Planned deliveries can still change; made ones are final. See [Deliveries](/merchants/standing-orders/deliveries).

To close for a day, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Skip this day for every standing order**.

### Invoices

**Invoices** lists every standing-order invoice, with the ones to check first. Filter by **All**, **To check**, **Sent**, **Overdue** or **Paid**, or use **Search customers**. See [Invoices](/merchants/standing-orders/invoices).

## Archive an ended standing order

When a standing order has ended, open it, press **⋯** and choose **Archive**. You see "Archived. It’s under Archived on Standing orders." It moves from **Ended** to **Archived**, and its page shows an **Archived** badge. Nothing else about it changes: its deliveries and invoices stay as they are.

To bring it back, press **⋯** and choose **Move back from archive**. It returns to **Ended**.

Only ended standing orders can be archived. A draft you don't need is deleted instead: **⋯ → Delete draft…**.
