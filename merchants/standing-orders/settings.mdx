Standing order settings are the defaults every new standing order starts from. You can change most of them for each customer when you [set one up](/merchants/standing-orders/set-up).

To open them, press **Settings** on the Standing orders page, or go to **Settings**, find **Standing orders** under **Selling & fulfilment** and press **Standing order settings**. **Go to Standing orders** takes you back.

When you change something, a bar appears at the bottom: **You have unsaved changes**. Press **Save**, or **Discard** to put things back. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, you're asked first.

## How invoices go out

- **Let me check each invoice first**: "We prepare the invoice when the period ends. It goes out when you press Send." You get an email the morning after the period ends, and reminders two and five days after the period ends if it’s still waiting. This is the default.
- **Send them for me**: "We tell you a day before, then send it."

A standing order set to **Use my default** follows this setting, so a change here applies to it straight away. One set to its own choice keeps that choice.

## Cut-off

"After the cut-off, only you can change a delivery."

Choose a time and the day: **the same day**, **the day before**, or **2 days before** up to **14 days before**. The line underneath says it in words, for example "Deliveries lock at 2:00 pm the day before." The default is 2:00 pm the day before.

New standing orders start with this cut-off. Changing it here doesn't change the cut-off of a standing order you've already made.

## Billing

- **One invoice**: **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Every month**. The default is every month.
- **Due**: **7 days after it’s sent**, **15 days after it’s sent** or **30 days after it’s sent**. The default is 15 days.

New standing orders start with these. A customer with their own payment terms gets theirs instead.

## Before the cut-off, buyers may

- **Skip a delivery**
- **Change quantities**
- **Pause deliveries**

All three are on by default. These apply to every standing order at once, including ones already running.

**What your customer sees:** on their page, each delivery still before its cut-off shows **Change** and **Skip** when you allow them. With pausing allowed, they see **Skip several days**, to skip a stretch of days at once. Turn one off and that button disappears from their page. You can always change or skip a delivery yourself. See [Your customer's page](/merchants/standing-orders/customer-page).

## Cut-off reminders

"For standing orders that remind the customer, the reminder goes this long before each cut-off."

Choose **3 hours before**, **12 hours before**, **24 hours before** or **48 hours before**. The default is 24 hours.

Reminders are switched on for each standing order, under **Cut-off** when you set it up: by email, by text, or both. The customer gets a link to change or skip that delivery. This setting decides when every reminder goes.

## Price changes

**Give buyers** a number of **days’ notice before a new price starts**: 0 to 90 days. The default is 14.

When you change prices on a running standing order, the new prices start on this many days from today unless you pick another date. A sooner date shows a warning, but you can still save it. See [Changing prices on a running standing order](/merchants/standing-orders/set-up#changing-prices-on-a-running-standing-order).

## Use the recommended settings

If you've changed anything from the defaults, **Use the recommended settings** puts them all back: check each invoice first, 2:00 pm the day before, every month, due in 15 days, customers may skip, change and pause, reminders 24 hours before, and 14 days' notice. Press **Save** to keep it.

Changing these settings never asks a customer to accept their agreement again.

## Agreement templates

The **Agreement templates** card holds your own wording around the terms: a welcome, your conditions and your signature. New standing orders start from your **Default** template, and your customer signs it when they accept.

- **New template** starts a template, and **Import a PDF** starts one from a document you already use.
- **Local Pass starter** is a built-in template. Press **Use** to start from it.
- Each template has **Edit**, and in its **⋯** menu **Make default**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

You can keep up to 20 templates. Each standing order keeps its own copy, so editing or deleting a template never changes an agreement already made. See [The agreement](/merchants/standing-orders/agreement).
