You will see an order cart tracker at the top of your cart whenever there are thresholds worth knowing about. It's designed to help you get the most out of your order — whether that's waiving a fee, unlocking free delivery, or earning a surprise reward.

---

## What Is the Order Tracker?

The Order Tracker is a visual progress bar that shows how close your current cart is to reaching one or more milestones set by the vendor.

Tap on it at any time to see a detailed explanation of each goal.

There are up to three types of milestones:

| Milestone | Icon | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small Order Fee threshold | Receipt | Add more to waive the small order fee |
| Free Delivery | Truck | Add more to unlock free delivery |
| Mystery Box | Gift | Add more to unlock a surprise bonus item |

As you add items to your cart, the progress bar fills in real time.

---

## The Small Order Fee Threshold

Some vendors set a minimum cart value for orders. If your subtotal is below this amount, a **small order fee** is added to your service fee at checkout.

This fee exists because processing a very small order has a baseline cost regardless of order size — payment processing, packaging, and handling all apply equally 5 order as they do to 50 orders. Rather than building this into product prices, vendors use a small order threshold so that customers with larger orders aren't penalized.

**The good news:** You can always avoid it by adding a bit more to your cart.

The Goal Tracker will show you exactly how much more you need:

> _"\$4.50 to avoid small order fee"_

Once you pass the threshold, the milestone turns green and the label changes to:

> _"Small order fee waived"_

<Info>
  The small order fee threshold only applies to marketplace and restaurant orders where the vendor uses the Local Pass platform for delivery. It is not applied to pickup orders or shipping orders.
</Info>

## Free Delivery Threshold

Some vendors offer free delivery when your order reaches a certain value. The Goal Tracker shows you how close you are:

> _"\$8.00 for free delivery"_

Once reached, your delivery fee at checkout will show as **Free**.

<Tip>
  If a vendor offers free delivery and you're close to the threshold, it's often worth adding one more item — the delivery savings will likely exceed the item's cost.
</Tip>

## Mystery Box

The Mystery Box is a special reward offered by select vendors on Local Pass. When your cart reaches the Mystery Box threshold, a surprise bonus item is automatically added to your order — at no extra charge.

What's inside? That's up to the vendor. It could be:

- A complimentary product sample
- A bonus portion of something you already ordered
- A seasonal or promotional item
- A vendor-exclusive surprise

The Goal Tracker will show the milestone as:

> _"\$6.00 for Mystery Box"_

Once you hit the threshold, the gift icon turns purple and the tracker confirms you've unlocked it.

<Note>
  The Mystery Box is vendor-specific and may not be available at every store. If a vendor has enabled it, you'll see it in the Goal Tracker automatically.
</Note>

## How All Three Work Together

If a vendor has set all three goals, the tracker shows all three milestones on a single progress bar. As your cart value increases, each milestone lights up in order.

Here's an example:

| Cart Value | What's unlocked |
| --- | --- |
| $0 – $ 14.99 | Small order fee applies, nothing unlocked |
| \$15.00 | Small order fee waived ✅ |
| $15.00 – $ 34.99 | Working toward free delivery and Mystery Box |
| \$35.00 | Free delivery unlocked ✅ |
| $35.00 – $ 49.99 | Working toward Mystery Box |
| \$50.00 | Mystery Box unlocked 🎁 |

When all goals are met, the tracker shows:

> _"All rewards unlocked!"_

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I see what's in the Mystery Box before ordering?">
    No — it's a surprise by design. The vendor chooses what to include, and part of the fun is the reveal. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, contact the vendor directly before placing your order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I remove items from my cart after unlocking a goal?">
    If your cart value drops below a threshold, the milestone becomes unmet again. At checkout, the small order fee will reapply if you're below the threshold. The Mystery Box item will be removed from your order if your cart drops below that threshold.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the small order fee the same at every vendor?">
    No. Each vendor sets their own threshold. The Goal Tracker always shows the specific threshold for the store you're ordering from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to do anything to apply free delivery?">
    No. When your cart reaches the free delivery threshold, it's applied automatically at checkout. You don't need a code or any action on your part.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
