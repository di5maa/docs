## Stage-by-Stage Instructions

The large button at the bottom of the screen changes at each stage. Here's exactly what to do.

### Stage 1 — Getting to the Restaurant

**Active button:** `ARRIVED AT PICKUP`

- Drive to the pickup address shown on the map.
- When you physically arrive at the restaurant, tap **ARRIVED AT PICKUP**.
- If the order is still being prepared, you'll see a red banner: _"Order is still being prepared."_ Wait until it's ready.
- A green **"pickup ready"** badge appears on the restaurant icon once the food is ready to collect.

### Stage 2 — Collecting the Order

**Active button:** `GO TO DROP-OFF`

- Show the **Order ID** (visible in the order info panel) to restaurant staff when collecting.
- Pick up the order and confirm everything is there.
- Tap **GO TO DROP-OFF** to confirm pickup and begin navigating to the customer.
- The customer automatically receives a notification that you're on your way.

### Stage 3 — Getting to the Customer

**Active button:** `ARRIVED AT DROP-OFF`

- Drive to the customer's address.
- When you arrive, tap **ARRIVED AT DROP-OFF**.
- The customer receives a notification that you're there.
- You may be prompted to upload delivery proof — see [Completing a Delivery](https://claude.ai/completing-a-delivery) for details.

### Stage 4 — Handing Off the Order

**Active button:** `COMPLETE ORDER`

- Tap **COMPLETE ORDER** to open the completion screen.
- The app checks your GPS location — you must be at or near the customer's address for the **DONE** button to activate.
- See [Completing a Delivery](https://claude.ai/completing-a-delivery) for the full completion flow.

## Side Action Buttons

On the left side of the navigation screen, you'll find quick-action buttons available throughout the delivery:

| Button | What It Does |
| :-- | :-- |
| ⏱ **Countdown Timer** | Shows time remaining for the current stage. Turns **red** with a "late" badge if you're running behind. |
| 💬 **Chat** | Opens a live chat with the customer. A **red badge** appears when you have unread messages. |
| 📞 **Call** | Calls the customer through the app's secure proxy — your personal number stays private. |
| 👤 **Order Info** | Opens the full order details panel (addresses, customer name, instructions, payout). |
| ✖ **Cancel** | Opens the cancellation flow. Only use if absolutely necessary — see [Cancelling a Delivery](https://claude.ai/cancelling-a-delivery). |
| 🎧 **Support** | Opens a live support chat with the Local Pass team. |

## Running Late

If you're past the estimated pickup or delivery time, a red **"late"** badge appears next to the countdown timer. This is visible on your screen only — keep moving and complete the delivery as quickly and safely as possible.
