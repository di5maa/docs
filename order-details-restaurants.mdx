---
title: "Order Details - Restaurants"
---

The Order Details page gives your team everything needed to manage a single order from the moment it arrives to the moment it's closed — items ordered, customer info, delivery or pickup details, your payout breakdown, and a running timeline of every update. This guide explains what you're looking at and what you can do.

---

## Getting to an Order

Open the **Orders** page and click any order number to open its detail view. On desktop the panel opens to the right. On mobile it opens full screen.

Use the **Back** arrow at the top left to return to the orders list.

---

## Order Header

At the top of the page:

- **Order number** with the date and time it was placed
- **Status badge** — the current order status (see the status reference at the bottom of this page)
- **Flagged badge** — shown in red if a staff member has flagged this order for attention
- **Dispute badge** — shown in amber if a dispute update has been logged by the Local Pass team

**Header action buttons** (desktop) or a second row (mobile):

| Button | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Refund** | Opens the refund dialog — only shown when a refund is applicable |
| **Print Invoice** | Generates and opens a printable PDF invoice for this order |

## Quick Facts

A row of stat cards gives you an at-a-glance summary before you scroll into the details:

| Card | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Gross Sales** | The product subtotal for this order |
| **Net Payout** | Your take-home after platform deductions |
| **Items** | Number of line items in the order |
| **Type** | Delivery, Pickup, Own Delivery, or Dine-in |
| **Order Age** | How long ago the order was placed |

## Notes

If notes are present they appear as coloured banners directly below the Quick Facts row:

- **Customer Note** — a yellow banner showing anything the customer wrote in the order notes field at checkout. Always read this before preparing the order.
- **Internal Note** — a blue banner showing any note logged by your staff on this order.

---

## Ordered Items

The items section lists everything the customer ordered:

- Product photo, name, and quantity
- All modifiers the customer selected — for example, _Sauce on the side_, _Extra cheese (\+\$1.00)_, _No onion_. Modifiers are shown in an indented box under the item name.
- Unit price and line total
- A sale badge if the item was purchased at a discounted price, with the original price shown crossed out

This is your kitchen reference. The modifiers section is designed to be clear enough that staff can read it at a glance without needing to open a separate ticket.

---

## Payment Summary

Shows exactly what the customer paid:

| Line | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subtotal | Combined item prices |
| Discount | Any promo code reduction |
| GST Collected (5%) | Tax collected from the customer |
| Delivery Fee | Shown for Own Delivery and On-demand Delivery orders |
| Driver Tip | Shown for Own Delivery and On-demand Delivery orders |
| Commission | Platform fee at your contracted rate |
| GST on Commission (5%) | Tax on the platform's service fee |
| PST on Commission (7% BC) | BC provincial tax on the platform's service fee |
| **Net Payout** | What you receive after all deductions |

### Understanding Your Settlement

The settlement calculation follows standard Canadian marketplace accounting:

Your **gross sales** are the food subtotal. The platform **commission** is calculated from your contracted rate (which varies by order type — pickup, self-delivery, or platform delivery). GST and BC PST are applied to the commission itself because marketplace facilitation fees are taxable services.

The **GST Collected** line is flagged with a "Remit to CRA" badge. This is tax you've collected from the customer as a trustee — it must be remitted to the Canada Revenue Agency on your regular GST/HST filing. It is not part of your payout.

Your **Net Payout** is what remains after the commission, GST on commission, and PST on commission are deducted.

## Customer

This card shows the customer's contact details and delivery address, with direct action buttons:

- **Call** — opens your device's phone app with the customer's number pre-dialled
- **Email** — opens your email client addressed to the customer

### Returning Customer Badge

A badge in the top right of the customer card shows whether this is a new or returning customer:

- **New Customer** — this is their first order with you
- **⭐ Nx Returning** — they've ordered from you N times before. The star and count update automatically.

This is useful context for your front-of-house team — a five-time returning customer who calls about an order deserves a different conversation than a first-time guest.

## Delivery Driver

If a driver has been assigned to the order, a Driver card appears showing:

- The driver's name
- An _Assigned_ status indicator

This card only appears when a driver name is on record for the order.

## Delivery Proof

If a driver submitted a proof-of-delivery photo through the app, it appears here along with:

- The photo itself
- Any comment the driver added (e.g., "Left at front door as requested")
- The timestamp of delivery

This section only appears when a proof photo is available.

## Actions

The Actions card gives you three tools beyond the header buttons:

### Add Staff Note

Opens a text dialog. Type your note and click **Save Note**. The note is added to the order timeline immediately, prefixed with 📝 to distinguish it from automatic status updates. Notes are internal — customers never see them.

Use staff notes to log:

- Customer calls and what was discussed
- Special prep instructions not captured in modifiers
- Reasons for delays or exceptions
- Any manual steps taken outside the normal workflow

### Flag Order

Opens a dialog where you select a reason for flagging. Options include: Wrong items delivered, Missing items, Customer complaint, Driver issue, Payment dispute, Fraud concern, Other.

Flagging an order:

1. Adds a 🚩 entry to the order timeline visible to your team
2. Creates a record in the Local Pass platform operations queue so the support team is notified

A **Flagged** badge appears in the order header once an order is flagged.

### Chat with Support

Opens a support conversation pre-filled with context from this specific order — order number, status, customer address, and fulfillment type. Use this if you need help from the Local Pass team on something that can't be resolved from the dashboard.

---

## Order Timeline

The timeline shows every status change, staff note, flag, and dispute update for this order in chronological order.

### Reading the Timeline

Each entry has a coloured dot that signals its type:

| Dot Colour | Entry Type |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 Green | Standard status updates (most recent entry) |
| 🔵 Blue/Purple | Historical status updates |
| ⚫ Grey | Staff notes (📝) |
| 🔴 Red | Flagged entries (🚩) |
| 🟡 Amber | Dispute updates |

### Dispute Updates

If the Local Pass team logs a dispute update on an order, it appears as an amber card in the timeline with a **DISPUTE** label. A two-line preview is shown inline. Tap the card to open a bottom sheet with the full dispute text, including context from the platform operations team.

A **Dispute** badge also appears in the order header whenever a dispute entry is present.

### Adding a Comment

Type in the text area at the bottom of the timeline and click **Post**. Comments appear immediately in the timeline with a 📝 prefix and timestamp. They are visible to your team only.

---

## Issuing a Refund

Click **Refund** in the header (or the Refund button in the Actions area if you have scrolled past the header) to open the refund dialog.

**Full Refund** — refunds the complete order total.

**Partial Refund** — shows each item with \+/− controls so you can select exactly which items and how many units to refund. The refund amount calculates automatically, inclusive of tax.

Select a reason from the dropdown before confirming. The refund is processed through Stripe and:

- A refund record is added to the order timeline
- The customer receives a push notification and email confirmation
- The order status updates to _Refunded_ if it was a full refund
- The refund amount is reflected in your payout balance

| **Accepted** | Kitchen has acknowledged the order |
| **Preparing** | Order is being made |

| **Delivered** | Delivery confirmed |
| **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled before fulfillment |

| **Refunded** | Full refund issued |
