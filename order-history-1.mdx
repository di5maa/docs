### Viewing Order Details description: "How to access full order information, addresses, delivery notes, and your payout during an active delivery.

The **Order Info** panel gives you a complete view of everything you need for the current delivery — addresses, customer name, delivery notes, and your payout.

## How to Open It

Tap the 👤 **Info** button on the left side of the navigation screen.

## What's Inside

### Order Status

At the top of the panel, you'll see the four-step progress tracker and the current status label (e.g., _"En route to drop-off"_). A countdown timer shows the estimated time remaining for the current phase.

### Order ID

A dark pill at the top shows the **Order ID** (e.g., _"Order ID: LP-1234"_). Show this to restaurant staff when collecting the order — they use it to match the order to you.

### Pickup Location

- **Restaurant Name** — displayed prominently
- **Street Address** — the restaurant's address

**Long-press** the pickup address section to copy the full address to your clipboard. A green **"Copied"** label confirms it worked.

### Customer

- **Customer's First Name** — shown so you can address them by name

### Drop-off Address

- **Customer's Name or Address Label**
- **Street Address** — where you're delivering to

**Tap** the drop-off address section to copy it to your clipboard.

### Delivery Instructions

If the customer left special delivery notes (e.g., _"Buzz apartment 4B"_ or _"Leave at the side door"_), they appear here in a highlighted box. Always read this section before attempting delivery.

### Ready for Pickup Message

When the restaurant marks the order as ready, a special message appears at the top of the panel:

> _"Hi \[Your Name\], the order is ready for pickup. Please show the order number \[Order ID\] at the counter. Thank you!"_

### Your Payout

At the bottom of the panel:

| Field | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Delivery Fee** | Your base pay for this delivery |
| **Tips** | The customer tip (you keep 100%) |

<Note>
  The payout shown here is the estimated amount. Final payout confirmation appears on the completion screen after you tap DONE. 
</Note>
