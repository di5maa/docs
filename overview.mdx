Placing an order on Local Pass is designed to be simple, transparent, and fair. This guide walks you through everything that happens between tapping **Checkout** and getting your order.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your Cart

Before you reach checkout, you'll land on the **Cart** screen. Here you can:

- Increase or decrease the quantity of any item
- Remove items entirely using the trash icon
- Add more items by tapping **Add more items**
- Leave order instructions for the vendor (allergies, special requests, etc.)

You'll also see an **Order Goal Tracker** at the top of your cart — more on that below.

---

## Step 2 — The Loading Screen

After tapping the checkout button in your cart, Local Pass runs a quick calculation in the background. This is where we:

- Confirm your delivery address and distance to the vendor
- Calculate your delivery fee, service fee, and estimated delivery or pickup time
- Check whether you qualify for free delivery
- Check if any promotions apply to your order

This only takes a few seconds. You'll see a loading animation while it runs.

---

## Step 3 — The Checkout Summary

Once the loading screen finishes, you'll arrive at the **Checkout Summary**. This is where you:

- Choose your **fulfillment type** (how you want your order delivered or picked up)
- Review your delivery address or pickup location
- See your estimated delivery or pickup time
- Add delivery notes (e.g. "Leave at door", "Buzzer code 42")
- Optionally apply **Local Coins** to reduce your total
- Review a full breakdown of all fees
- Add a tip (for delivery orders)
- Confirm your **total** and tap **Checkout**

---

## Step 4 — Payment

Local Pass uses **Stripe** to process all payments securely. When you tap **Checkout**:

- A secure payment sheet opens — you can pay with a saved card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay
- Your card is authorized (a hold is placed)
- For most order types, the charge is finalized when your order is confirmed
- For Express (on-demand) deliveries, the charge is only captured once your driver picks up your order

You'll never be charged a markup. Every fee is itemized and explained. See [Understanding Your Fees](/checkout/understanding-fees) for a full breakdown.

---

## Step 5 — Order Processing

After payment, you're taken to the **Order Processing** screen. Please stay on this screen while we:

- Confirm your payment with Stripe
- Create your order record
- Notify the vendor
- (For delivery orders) Begin matching you with a driver

This usually takes 10–20 seconds. Do not close the app.

---

## Step 6 — Order Tracking

Once processing is complete, you'll be taken to the **Order Tracking** screen where you can follow your order in real time.

<Note>
  Your order confirmation and receipt will be available in the **Orders** section of the app. You'll also receive push notifications at key status changes.
</Note>

---

## Agrifood vs. Restaurant Orders

Local Pass serves two types of vendors, and the checkout experience is slightly different for each.

|  | **Restaurant Orders** | **Agrifood / Marketplace Orders** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fulfillment options | Delivery, Pickup | Delivery, Express, Pickup, Shipping |
| Who delivers | Local Pass drivers (or vendor's own drivers) | Local Pass drivers, vendor's own drivers, or shipping carriers |
| Payment capture | Pre-authorized, captured on confirmation | Immediate (Delivery/Pickup) or pre-authorized (Express) |
| Shipping available | No | Yes (for products that require it) |

See [Fulfillment Types](/checkout/fulfillment-types) for a full explanation of each option.
