# Personal Information

Your Personal Information page is where you manage your name, contact details, profile photo, and default address. Keeping this information up to date ensures your account works smoothly across rides, deliveries, and support.

---

## Getting There

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under the **Personal Account** section, tap **Personal details**.

---

## Updating Your Profile Photo

1. On the Personal Information page, tap the circular photo at the top of the screen.
2. Tap **Upload Photo**.
3. Choose a photo from your device's photo library, or take a new one with your camera.
4. The photo will upload automatically and update your profile.

<Tip>
A clear, well-lit photo helps drivers and vendors recognise you. It also appears on your Local Pass card.
</Tip>

---

## Updating Your Name

### First Name
1. Tap the **First Name** field.
2. Clear the existing text and type your updated first name.

### Last Name
1. Tap the **Last Name** field.
2. Clear the existing text and type your updated last name.

<Note>
If you are a registered driver on Local Pass, you will also see a **Display Name** field. This is the name shown to passengers when you are on a trip.
</Note>

---

## Updating Your Phone Number

1. Tap the **Phone Number** field.
2. Clear the existing number and enter your new phone number.
3. Numbers are formatted automatically as **(###) ###-####**.

---

## Updating Your Email Address

1. Tap the **Email Address** field.
2. Clear the current email and type your new email address.

<Warning>
Changing your email address also updates the email used to sign in to your account. Make sure you have access to the new email before saving changes.
</Warning>

---

## Setting Your Gender

1. Tap the **Gender** dropdown.
2. Choose from: **Female**, **Male**, or **Prefer not to say**.

---

## Setting Your Birthday

1. Tap the **Pick Birthday** button (it shows your current birthday or "Your Birthday" if not set).
2. A date picker will appear.
3. Navigate to your birth month and year, then tap your birth date.
4. Tap **OK** or **Confirm** to save the selection.

---

## Updating Your Default Address

Your default address is shown on this page below your birthday. It is used as your home address for deliveries and ride pickups.

To change it:

1. Tap **Update Default Address**.
2. A search panel will open. See [Updating Your Default Address](./updating-your-address) for a full walkthrough.

---

## Resetting Your Password

If you want to change your password:

1. Tap **Reset Password**.
2. A reset link will be sent to your current email address.
3. Open that email and follow the link to create a new password.

<Note>
You must be able to receive email at your registered email address to complete a password reset. If your email has changed, update it first and save your changes before resetting the password.
</Note>

---

## Saving Your Changes

Once you have made all the updates you need:

1. Tap **Save Changes** in the bottom right of the screen.
2. A confirmation message will briefly appear letting you know the update was successful.

<Warning>
Changes are not saved automatically. Always tap **Save Changes** before leaving the page, or your updates will be lost.
</Warning>

---

## Changing the App Language

From the Personal Information page you can also switch the app language:

1. Tap the language button in the top right corner of the screen (it shows the current language code, for example **EN**).
2. Select your preferred language from the list.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|---|---|
| Photo is not uploading | Check your internet connection and try again. Make sure the app has permission to access your photos |
| Changes are not saving | Tap Save Changes before leaving the page |
| Email change not working | Ensure the new email address is correctly formatted |
| Password reset email not arriving | Check your spam folder, or confirm the email address on file is correct |
