---
title: "Personalizing Local Pass"
---

Your Local Pass card is your digital identity on the platform. It displays your name, location, and rewards balance, and it can be customised with different artwork and colour themes to make it your own.

---

## Getting to the Personalization Page

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **Personal Account**, tap **Personalize Local Pass**.

---

## What You Can Customize

On the Personalization page, you can choose the **artwork** and visual design displayed on your Local Pass card. The card also automatically shows:

- Your first and last name
- Your province or region
- Your Local Coins balance
- Your unique Local Pass ID (your phone number on file)

---

## Changing the Card Design

1. On the Personalization page, you will see a preview of your current card design.
2. Browse the available artwork options shown below or beside the card preview.
3. Tap an artwork option to apply it to your card instantly.
4. The card preview updates in real time so you can see exactly how it will look.

<Tip>
  Try a few different designs to find one that feels right for you. Changes are previewed immediately before you confirm.
</Tip>

---

## Saving Your Design

Once you are happy with your card design, your selection is saved automatically. There is no separate save button required.

---

## Where Your Local Pass Card Appears

Your personalised Local Pass card is shown:

- On the **Home screen** of the app as your main profile card
- When sharing your referral with friends
- As a visual representation of your account and rewards status

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own image as the card background?">
     At this time, card designs are chosen from the available artwork options provided in the app. Custom photo uploads for the card background are not supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My card is not showing my correct balance — what should I do?">
     Your Local Coins balance updates in real time based on your activity. If you notice a discrepancy, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, contact Local Pass support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does changing the card design affect how my account works?">
     No. The card design is purely visual. It does not affect your rewards balance, your orders, or any other account functionality.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
