Every product feed in the Local Market — whether you're browsing a category, a vendor's catalogue, or a section like Fresh Deals — has the same filter bar at the top. These chips let you quickly narrow results down to what matters to you.

---

## The Filter Bar

At the top of the product grid you'll see a row of pill-shaped chips. Tap any chip to open its options (or, for the toggle-style ones, to switch it straight on or off). When a filter is **active**, its chip turns green.

| Chip | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Price** | Opens a price range picker |
| **Dietary** | Opens a searchable list of dietary labels |
| **Allergies** | Lets you exclude products listing a specific allergen |
| **Top Rated** | A toggle — shows only products rated 4.0 and above. Only appears on feeds where it's relevant |
| **Soon To Go** | A toggle — shows only products marked with the Soon to Go label. Only appears where relevant |
| **Deals** | A toggle — shows only products with an active promotion. Only appears where relevant |

You can combine multiple filters at once. When any filter is active, a **Reset** chip appears at the start of the row — tap it to clear everything.

---

## Price

Tap the **Price** chip to open a price range sheet. You can either:

- Tap a **preset range** — \$0 – \$10, \$10 – \$25, \$25 – \$50, \$50 – \$100, or \$100+ — which applies immediately, or
- Drag the **range slider** below the presets to set a custom minimum and maximum (up to \$1,000+)

Either way, results update as soon as you finish adjusting. If a price filter is active, a **Clear** button appears next to the "Price Range" title to reset it.

---

## Dietary

Tap the **Dietary** chip to open a searchable, multi-select list of labels, including:

Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten Free, Paleo, Grass-Fed Beef, Lactose Free, Keto Friendly, No Artificial Preservatives, Low Sodium, Ethically Raised, Dairy Free, Allergen Free, Non-GMO, Fair Trade, Sugar Free, Whole Grain, Certified Organic, Cage Free Eggs, Low Fat, and Contains Allergens.

Check as many as apply, then tap **Apply Filters** to confirm your selection.

---

## Allergies

Tap the **Allergies** chip to open **Exclude Allergens**. Type an allergen you want to avoid (for example, _Peanuts_) — products that list it will be hidden from your results.

<Warning>
  The allergen filter is based on information vendors have entered into the system. Always check the product details and contact the vendor directly if you have a severe allergy.
</Warning>

---

## Top Rated, Soon To Go, and Deals

These three are simple on/off toggles rather than menus — tap the chip and the filter applies immediately:

- **Top Rated** shows only products with an overall rating of 4.0 or higher.
- **Soon To Go** shows only products marked close to their best-before date and discounted.
- **Deals** shows only products with an active promotion.

Not every feed shows all three — a chip only appears when it's relevant to that particular feed.

---

## Resetting Filters

When any filter is active, a **Reset** chip appears at the start of the filter row. Tap it to clear every active filter — price, dietary, allergies, and any active toggles — and return to the full unfiltered product list.

---

## Subcategory Quick Links

When browsing a main category (e.g. _Produce_), you'll also see a horizontal row of **subcategory chips** — for example, _Root Vegetables_, _Leafy Greens_. Tapping one takes you directly to that subcategory's product page.
