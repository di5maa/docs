Every product feed in the Local Market — whether you're browsing a category, viewing deals, or exploring all products — comes with a set of **filter chips** at the top. These help you quickly narrow results down to what matters to you.

---

## Quick Filters (Pill Chips)

At the top of most product pages you'll see a row of small pill-shaped chips. You can tap any chip to toggle that filter on or off. When a filter is **active**, the chip turns green.

| Filter Chip | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Shows only products that are currently in stock |
| **Under \$20** | Shows only products with an effective price below \$20 |
| **Soon to Go** | Shows only products marked with the Soon to Go label |
| **Dietary tags** | Shows products matching a specific dietary label (e.g. Vegan, Gluten Free) |

You can combine multiple filters at the same time. For example: \*Available \+ Under $20* will show you in-stock products priced below $20.

---

## Advanced Filters (Explore All Section)

In the **Explore All** section at the bottom of the market home page, you'll find more detailed filter options:

### Price Range

Tap the **Price** chip to open a price filter. You can:

- Choose a **preset range** (e.g. $0–$10, $10–$25, $25–$50, $50–$100, \$100\+)
- Or enter a **custom minimum and maximum** price in the text fields
- Tap **Apply** to see updated results

### Rating

Tap the **Rating** chip to filter by minimum customer rating:

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Any | No rating filter applied |
| 3.0\+ | Good |
| 3.5\+ | Very good |
| 4.0\+ | Excellent |
| 4.5\+ | Outstanding |

Tap your chosen rating to apply it immediately.

### Pickup Only

Tap the **Pickup** chip to only show products from vendors who offer pickup. If you have location access enabled, results will also be sorted by distance.

### Dietary Preferences

Tap the **Dietary** chip to open a list of dietary labels. Check the ones that apply to your needs:

- Vegan
- Vegetarian
- Gluten Free
- Paleo
- Keto Friendly
- Dairy Free
- Organic
- Halal
- Kosher
- And many more

Tap **Apply Filters** at the bottom to confirm your selection. You can select more than one.

### Allergen Exclusion

Tap the **Allergies** chip to type in an allergen you want to avoid (for example, _Peanuts_ or _Shellfish_). Products that list that allergen will be hidden from your results.

<Warning>
  The allergen filter is based on information vendors have entered into the system. Always check the product details and contact the vendor directly if you have a severe allergy.
</Warning>

---

## Resetting Filters

When any filter is active, a **Reset** chip appears at the start of the filter row. Tap it to clear all filters and return to the full unfiltered product list.

---

## Filters Inside a Vendor's Store

When you're viewing a specific vendor's product list, the filter chips at the top work the same way but only apply to that vendor's products. You can filter by:

- **Price Range**
- **Dietary labels**
- **Allergens**
- **Top Rated** (products with a rating of 4.0 or above)
- **Soon To Go** (discounted near-expiry products)
- **Deals** (products with an active sale price)

---

## Subcategory Quick Links

When browsing a main category (e.g. _Produce_), you'll also see a horizontal row of **subcategory chips** — for example, _Root Vegetables_, _Leafy Greens_. Tapping one takes you directly to that subcategory's product page.
