---
title: "Understanding a Product Page"
---

When you tap any product in the Local Market, you're taken to that product's **detail page**. This page gives you all the information you need before adding it to your cart.

---

## What's on the Product Page

### Product Image

At the top of the page you'll see the main product photo. If the product has special labels, they appear overlaid on the image:

- A **discount badge** (e.g. _-20%_) if the product is on sale
- A **rating badge** (e.g. ⭐ 4.3) if the product has been reviewed
- A green **Soon-to-go** tag if the product is close to its best-before date

Tapping the **Soon-to-go** tag opens a pop-up explaining what the label means.

---

### Price

Below the image you'll see the price displayed clearly:

- If the product has a **sale price**, you'll see the discounted price prominently, with the original price shown crossed out beside it, and a red badge showing the percentage off (e.g. _20% off_)
- If there's no sale, you'll see the **regular price** with a CA\$ prefix

---

### Delivery & Availability

A small note below the price tells you when the product will be available:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| _Available today!_ | You can receive this product in today's delivery or pickup window |
| _Available next day_ | This product will be ready the following day |
| _Takes X days to prepare_ | The vendor needs a few days to prepare this item (e.g. custom orders) |

---

### Product Options

Some products have **options** you can choose before adding to your basket — for example, selecting a size, weight, or variety.

Options come in two types:

- **Variants**: Choosing a variant changes the product's price. For example, selecting _500g_ vs _1kg_ will set a different price.
- **Add-ons**: These are extras added on top of the base product, each with their own price.

Options marked **Required** must be selected before you can add the product to your basket. The **Add to basket** button will remain greyed out until all required options are chosen.

To select an option, tap the **checkbox** next to it. Some option groups are _choose only one_, meaning selecting a new option will deselect the previous one.

---

### Custom Orders (Wholesale)

Some products support **custom quantity orders**. If you see a **Custom Order** section, you can:

1. Type in how many units you want (e.g. _12 kg_)
2. Tap **Confirm** to set that quantity
3. The estimated cost will update automatically

If you enter more than the vendor's maximum, an error message will appear telling you the maximum they can supply. Tap the refresh icon to reset and enter a different amount.

A bulk discount may apply automatically if you order above a certain quantity — this will be shown in the Custom Order section.

---

### Description & Details Tabs

Below the options you'll find two tabs:

**Description tab** The vendor's own description of the product — what it is, how it's made, how to use it, etc.

**Details tab** More technical information, including:

- **Allergens**: Specific allergens present in the product (e.g. _Gluten_, _Tree Nuts_)
- **Certifications**: Dietary labels the product carries (e.g. _Certified Organic_, _Halal_)
- **Stock status**: Whether the product is in stock, low stock, or out of stock
- **Shipping**: Whether the vendor ships this product

<Warning>
  Always check the allergen list on the Details tab if you have a food allergy. Contact the vendor directly if you need more information.
</Warning>

---

### About the Seller

Further down the page you'll find a section about the vendor selling the product, including:

- Their **name**, **rating**, and **tagline**
- Buttons to view **All Products** from that store or **Visit Store** to see their full profile
- Whether **delivery** and **pickup** are available

---

### You May Also Enjoy

At the bottom of the product page, the app shows a horizontal list of **other featured products** from the same vendor. Tap any of them to open that product's page.

---

### Report This Product

If you believe a product listing is inaccurate, misleading, or inappropriate, tap **Report this product** at the very bottom of the page. This opens a pre-filled email to the Local Pass support team.

---

## Adding to Your Basket

At the bottom of the screen, you'll find the **Add to basket** bar:

1. Use the **– / \+** buttons on the left to set how many you want
2. The button on the right shows the quantity and total price (e.g. _Add 2 to basket • \$14.00_)
3. Tap it to add the product to your cart

<Note>
  You must be logged in to add items to your cart. If you're not logged in, tapping the button will prompt you to create an account.
</Note>

If any **required options** haven't been selected yet, the button will be greyed out. Select all required options first.
