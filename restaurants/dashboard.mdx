Your dashboard is your home base on Local Pass Business. From here you can publish your store, monitor orders, review customer feedback, and track what still needs to be set up.

## Dashboard overview

When you arrive at your dashboard, you'll see:

- Your **store cover photo** and **logo** at the top
- Your **store name** and a welcome message
- Quick links to **Copy your Store ID** and **View your public store page**
- A **store status toggle** to publish or unpublish your store
- An **analytics panel** showing store performance
- A list of **recent orders**
- A summary of **recent reviews and ratings**
- A **setup checklist** if there are remaining steps to complete

## Publishing your store

At the top of your dashboard you'll find the **Status** toggle showing either **Published** or **Inactive**.

### Before you can publish

Your store must meet the following requirements:

| Requirement | Where to set it up |
| --- | --- |
| **Stripe payment account connected** | Go to **Payments** in your dashboard menu |
| **Business address confirmed** | Set during [store setup](/account-setup) or in your store settings |

If either of these is missing, the toggle will be disabled and a message will explain what still needs to be done.

### Publishing your store

Once both requirements are met:

1. Toggle the **Status** switch to **Published**
2. Your store and products will become visible to customers on Local Pass

### Unpublishing your store

If you need to take your store offline temporarily:

1. Toggle the **Status** switch to **Inactive**
2. A confirmation dialog will appear — click **Unpublish** to confirm
3. Your store and products will no longer appear to customers

## Viewing your public store page

Click the **View** button next to your store name to open your public-facing store page on the Local Pass customer app. This is exactly what customers see when they find your store.

## Copying your Store ID

Click **Copy ID** to copy your unique store identifier to your clipboard. You may need this when contacting Local Pass support or setting up integrations.

## Recent orders

The **Recent Orders** panel shows your last 5 completed or cancelled orders. Each entry displays:

- Customer name
- Order number
- Fulfilment type (pickup or delivery)
- Order status
- Date and time
- Order subtotal

Click any order to open the full order details.

## Recent reviews

The **Recent Reviews** panel shows your overall star rating and a breakdown across four categories:

| Rating category | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Food** | Quality of your food or products |
| **Atmosphere** | The experience at your location |
| **Service** | How customers rated their service experience |
| **Value for Money** | Whether customers felt they received good value |

Click the arrow icon next to the reviews heading to view all your reviews in full.

## Setup checklist

If you've recently created your store, your dashboard will show a checklist to help you go live. Each item shows a tick when complete.

| Task | How to complete it |
| --- | --- |
| **Create account** | Completed automatically ✓ |
| **Set up payment options** | Connect a Stripe account via **Payments** |
| **Create your first product** | Go to **Menu** (restaurants) or **Inventory** (other businesses) |
| **Review delivery & pickup settings** | Go to **Fulfilment Settings** |
| **Upload certifications**_(optional, agrifood only)_ | Go to **Store Settings** |

Once all required steps are complete, the checklist will be replaced by your **announcements feed** — updates and tips from the Local Pass team.

## Navigation

Use the **side navigation bar** (desktop) or **bottom navigation bar** (mobile) to move between sections of your dashboard:

- **Dashboard** — Home overview
- **Orders** — Manage incoming and past orders
- **Menu / Inventory** — Add and manage your products
- **Payments** — Connect Stripe and view payouts
- **Fulfilment Settings** — Manage delivery and pickup options
- **Store Settings** — Update your profile, certifications, and more
- **Reviews** — View all customer feedback
