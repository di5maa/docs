<Info>
  Changes made here update your live menu immediately after clicking **Publish**. Ensure all details are correct before saving.
</Info>

## Basic Information

When you open the product editor (Add or Edit mode), start by configuring the core identity of your item.

### Core Details

- **Name:** The display name of the item as it appears to customers. Use clear, capitalized names (e.g., "Double Cheeseburger").
- **Description:** A detailed explanation of the dish, ingredients, and potential allergens.
- **Preparation Time:** An approximate time (in minutes) it takes to prepare this specific item. This helps calculate accurate delivery/pickup estimates.

### Pricing Strategies

Manage how your product is priced and displayed.

1. **Regular Price:** The standard selling price.
2. **Promotion Price (Optional):** If you enter a value here, the system will:
   - Cross out the regular price.
   - Display the promotion price.
   - Show the item as "On Sale."

     <Info>
       Promotion price is a separate value from a standalone **Promotion** that you run at the store level — see [Start here](/restaurants/promotions-manager/start-here).
     </Info>

## Media & Gallery

Visuals are the #1 driver for menu conversion. You can manage your product imagery directly from this form.

- **Upload Photo:** Upload a new image directly from your device.
- **Gallery:** Select from images you have previously uploaded to your media library — see [Media Gallery](/merchants/media-gallery).

<Warning>
  **File Requirements:** The system supports **JPEG** and **JPG** formats. Ensure your images are high-quality and centered, as they may be cropped for different device sizes.
</Warning>

## Item Flags

Four independent toggles that change how the item is treated elsewhere in the app — none of them affect the item's core listing, they just add it to a specific surface or behavior.

| Flag | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Featured item** | Adds a highlighted badge to this item on your menu. |
| **Cross-sell item** | Suggests this item to customers at checkout — typically drinks, sides, or add-ons someone might want alongside what's already in their cart. |
| **Soon-to-go** | Marks the item down to move stock fast — opens a quick percent-off calculator (10/20/30/50%) rather than making you type a new price by hand. See [Start here](/restaurants/promotions-manager/start-here) for how this relates to your other promotion tools. |
| **Catering menu** | Makes this item available on your [catering page](/merchants/catering). |

<Tip>
  **Catering menu can be toggled from two places, and they stay in sync.** You can turn it on here, or from the Menu tab on your Catering page itself — whichever is more convenient in the moment. Either way flips the same switch.
</Tip>

## Dietary Labels and Allergens

Two more sections live right below Item Flags, and both matter specifically **because they're shown to customers on your catering page**, not just your regular menu — someone booking catering for a group is far more likely to be checking dietary fit for multiple people than someone ordering one meal for themselves.

- **Dietary Labels** — select from a standard list (Gluten-Free, Vegan, Vegetarian, Local, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, and more) describing what the dish **is suitable for**.
- **Allergens** — toggle **Contains Allergens** on, then add free-text tags for what the dish **contains** (e.g. `nuts`, `dairy`, `gluten`, `shellfish`).

<Warning>
  These two sections describe opposite things and it's easy to set one without meaning to touch the other — a "Nut-Free" dietary label and a "Contains nuts" allergen tag can both technically be set on the same item if you're not paying attention. Double check both before publishing.
</Warning>

## Item Badges

Separate from Dietary Labels, Item Badges are **handling and content warnings** — practical, at-a-glance information about how the item should be treated, not what diet it fits. They show as chips directly on the item in the customer app.

| Badge | What it signals |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Contains dairy** | Milk, cream, butter, cheese |
| **Heat sensitive** | Can spoil in warm weather |
| **Keep refrigerated** | Refrigerate on arrival |
| **Keep frozen** | Must stay frozen |
| **Best same day** | Quality drops after today |
| **Fragile** | Handle with care in transit |
| **Contains nuts** | Peanuts or tree nuts |
| **Contains eggs** | — |
| **Contains gluten** | — |
| **Contains shellfish** | — |
| **Contains alcohol** | ID may be required |
| **Raw / undercooked** | Raw or lightly cooked ingredients |
| **Spicy** | — |
| **Made fresh daily** | — |

<Tip>
  **Why this matters beyond the customer app:** several of these badges are really instructions for whoever handles the order after you — "Keep frozen" and "Heat sensitive" tell a driver how to transport it, "Fragile" tells them how to carry it, and "Best same day" sets expectations if a delivery is delayed. Tag honestly rather than sparingly; an accurate badge is what prevents a spoiled or crushed item from becoming a refund.
</Tip>

Don't see the badge you need? Type your own — it's added as a plain-text chip rather than one of the curated icons, but it still shows on the item.

## Menu Options & Modifiers

If your product has variations (like sizes) or add-ons (like toppings), use the **Menu Options** configuration tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Option Group">
    Click the **"This item has options"** button (or the **\+** icon) to create a container for your choices.
  </Step>
  <Step stepNumber={2} title="Configure Logic">
    Define how the customer interacts with this group:

    - **Name:** e.g., "Select Size", "Choice of Sauce".
    - **Required:** Toggle **ON** if a choice is mandatory (e.g., Size). Toggle **OFF** for optional add-ons.
    - **Multiple Selection:** Toggle **ON** to allow selecting more than one option (e.g., "Add Extra Toppings"). Toggle **OFF** for single-choice lists (e.g., "Cook Style").
  </Step>
  <Step stepNumber={3} title="Add Choices">
    Add specific items to the group:

    - **Name:** "Small", "Medium", "Large".
    - **Price:** Enter the _additional cost_ for this choice. Enter `0.00` if it's free.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Managing Choices

- **Reorder:** Use the **Up/Down arrows** to change the order in which choices appear to the customer.
- **Remove:** Click the **Trash** icon to delete a specific choice or an entire option group.

## Publishing Changes

Once configuration is complete:

1. Click the **Publish** button at the bottom of the form.
2. The system will validate your entries (e.g., ensuring a name and price exist).
3. A confirmation dialog will appear: _"Product has been published/updated."_
4. The item is now live.

### Status Indicators

In the top right corner of the editor, you will see a status badge:

- **Draft:** The item is being created.
- **Published:** The item is live.
- **Out-of-stock:** The item is currently unavailable to customers. (To change this, use the main inventory toggle on the menu list page).
- **Archived:** The item is removed from your active menu but kept in your history. Use the bulk actions on Menu Management to archive or restore items.
