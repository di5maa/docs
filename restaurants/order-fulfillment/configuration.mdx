Local Pass provides flexible options to help you serve your customers, whether they prefer to pick up their food or have it delivered directly to their doorstep. We understand that every business operates differently, which is why we support three primary fulfillment methods.

## Fulfillment Methods

You can enable any combination of the following methods based on your business needs:

### 1. Pickup

Allow customers to place orders online and collect them directly from your counter. This is an excellent way to reduce wait times during peak hours and let customers skip the line. It requires no logistics setup and is the fastest way to start selling.

<Note>
  Pickup location is automatically set up for your store based on the business address you have provided during registration, along with business hours. You can change default location in Pickup settings. 
</Note>

### 2. Delivery via Local Pass Network

Tap into our network of reliable, vetted local drivers. When you choose this option, Local Pass handles the entire logistics process—from finding a driver to tracking the delivery. This allows you to offer delivery without the headache of managing staff or vehicles.

<Note>
  Delivery is automatically disabled for your store. Ensure you enable delivery settings if you want to accept offer deliveries via Local Pass driver network.  
</Note>

### 3. Own Delivery (Self-Delivery)

Do you already have your own team of drivers? You can use Local Pass to process orders while managing the delivery yourself. This option gives you full control over the customer experience and allows you to maintain your own delivery standards and branding.

## Next Steps

To configure delivery zones, fees, and preparation times, please navigate to the specific settings page for your chosen method.
