**Where to find it:** Main Navigation → Orders

The Order Fulfillment Dashboard is your real-time command centre for all active orders. It organises orders by time of day so you always know what needs attention first and nothing slips through the cracks.

## Quick Stats (Top of Page)

Five summary cards give you an instant snapshot of your day:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs Action** | Orders still "Placed" or "Pending" waiting on your acceptance |
| **Deliveries** | Number of delivery orders due today |
| **Pickups** | Number of pickup orders due today |
| **Shipping** | Number of shipping orders due today |
| **Today's Revenue** | Total revenue from today's orders |

A red **Needs Action** count is your highest priority — these are customers waiting on you right now.

## Views

Switch between different layouts using the view toggle buttons:

| View | Best used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Card** | Default grouped view, organised by time of day |
| **Compact** | Dense row list — great for high-volume days |
| **Calendar** | Viewing all orders scheduled for a specific date |
| **Route** | Coming soon — AI-powered delivery routing |

## Filters

### Smart Filters

Located just below the search bar:

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every active order |
| **Needs Action** | Orders awaiting your acceptance |
| **Due Soon** | Orders due within the next 90 minutes |
| **Overdue** | Orders past their fulfillment time that are not yet complete |

### Status Filters

A second row of chips lets you narrow orders by status: All, Placed, Preparing, Packaged, Ready, Out for Delivery, Shipped

### Search

Type any order number or customer name to jump straight to a specific order.

## How orders are grouped

In **Card view**, orders are automatically grouped by time of day:

| Group | Time range |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Past their scheduled time and not yet complete |
| **Morning** | Fulfillment time before 12:00 PM |
| **Afternoon** | Fulfillment time from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM |
| **Evening** | Fulfillment time after 5:00 PM |

## New order alerts

When a new order arrives while the dashboard is open, a **red alert banner** appears at the top of the screen. It shows how many new orders just came in and plays a notification sound.

Tap **View** on the banner to jump straight to those orders. Tap **X** to dismiss the banner without changing your current filter.

## Expanding order details

Tap the **chevron arrow (˅)** on any order card to expand it and see a full item breakdown including:

- Item name and quantity
- Selected options or customisations
- Item photos
- Price per item

Tap the chevron again to collapse.

## Setting an ETA

Tap the **"Set ETA"** chip on any delivery or pickup order card to set a specific ready time. A time picker will open — select the time and confirm. The customer will receive an automatic push notification with their updated pickup/delivery time.

## Printing

### Daily Picklist

Tap the **Picklist** button (visible on tablet and desktop) to generate and print a summary of all orders for the current day's filter. This is useful to hand to your kitchen or packing team.

### Shipping Labels

Tap the **printer icon** on any delivery or shipping order to print a formatted A6 shipping label. The label includes:

- Your business name and address (return address)
- Customer name and delivery address
- Order number and date
- A QR code for the order

---

## Bulk actions

Bulk mode lets you action multiple orders at once.

**To enter bulk mode:**

- Long-press any order card, **or**
- Swipe left on any order card

**In bulk mode you can:**

- Tap multiple order cards to select them
- **Advance** — move all selected orders to their next status at once
- **Labels** — print shipping labels for all selected delivery or shipping orders
- **All** — select or deselect all orders at once
- Tap **X** to exit bulk mode

## Desktop sidebar

On desktop screens, a right-hand **Upcoming** sidebar shows a 7-day forecast of your orders by type:

- **D** — Delivery orders (blue)
- **P** — Pickup orders (amber)
- **S** — Shipping orders (teal)

This gives you a quick view of how busy the coming days will be.
