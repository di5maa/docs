title: "Delivery zones"
---

**Where to find it:** Fulfillment Settings → Delivery Settings → **Custom Delivery Zones** (this is a section on the Delivery Settings page, not a separate screen)

<Note>
  Custom zones — and the standard delivery fee/radius fields they override — only appear once **Use Own Delivery Fleet** is turned on. See [Delivery](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/delivery) for why.
</Note>

Delivery zones define the geographic areas you are willing to deliver to. Instead of using a simple radius circle, Local Pass calculates zones using **actual drive time from your business address** (via Mapbox) — giving you a much more accurate and realistic picture of what is reachable than a straight-line distance would.

For example, a 15-minute drive-time zone near a waterfront will look very different from one in the middle of a grid-based city.

---

## Adding a new delivery zone

1. Turn on **Custom Delivery Zones**, then tap **Add Custom Zone**
2. Fill in the zone details:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Zone Name** | A label for this zone (e.g., "City Centre", "North End", "University District") |
| **Delivery Fee (\$)** | The fee charged to customers ordering from within this zone |
| **Min Order (\$)** | The minimum order value required to qualify for delivery to this zone |

3. Use the **Drive Time Range slider** to set your delivery radius in minutes (5 to 60 minutes, in 5-minute steps)
6. Tap **Save Zone**

<Tip>
  Start with your most common delivery area. You can always add more zones later with different fees and minimums.
</Tip>

---

## Editing an existing zone

Tap the **edit icon** on a zone card to open it back up — the saved name, fee, minimum order, and drive time are all pre-filled and ready to adjust. Move the **Drive Time Range slider** to change the radius, then tap **Save Zone** again.

Tap the **trash icon** on a zone card to delete it. If you delete your last remaining zone, Custom Delivery Zones turns itself back off.

## Map availability

| Platform | Map preview |
| --- | --- |
| **Mobile (iOS/Android)** | Full interactive map with the zone polygon drawn on screen |
| **Web** | No map preview — the dashboard tells you so directly: _"Mapbox visualization is active in the mobile app. For the web dashboard, define your radius limits here. Zones match customer delivery distance dynamically."_ |

<Note>
  On web, the zone boundary is calculated and saved correctly even though the map isn't displayed. You can view the zone visually on the mobile app after saving.
</Note>

---

## Tips for setting up your zones

- **Create multiple zones** with different fees — for example, a lower fee for nearby neighbourhoods and a higher fee for areas further away
- **Use minimum order amounts** to ensure delivery to distant zones remains profitable
- **Shorter drive times** (e.g., 10–15 min) are better for hot food that needs to arrive quickly; **longer times** (e.g., 30–45 min) work for packaged goods or cold items

## Drive time slider reference

| Slider position | Approximate coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 5 minutes | Very local — a few blocks |
| 15 minutes | A typical urban neighbourhood |
| 30 minutes | A large portion of a small city |
| 45–60 minutes | Regional — suitable for farms or rural businesses |
