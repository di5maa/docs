# Delivery Zones

**Where to find it:** Settings → Delivery → Zones

Delivery zones define the geographic areas you are willing to deliver to. Instead of using a simple radius circle, Local Pass calculates zones using **actual drive time from your business address** — giving you a much more accurate and realistic picture of what is reachable.

---

## How drive-time zones work

When you set a zone, the system calls a mapping service to draw a polygon around your location based on how far a driver can travel in your chosen number of minutes. This accounts for real roads, one-way streets, and geographic features like water or mountains — rather than just a straight-line distance.

For example, a 15-minute drive-time zone near a waterfront will look very different from one in the middle of a grid-based city.

---

## Adding a new delivery zone

1. Go to Settings → Delivery → Zones and tap **Add Delivery Zone**
2. Fill in the zone details:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Zone Name** | A label for this zone (e.g., "City Centre", "North End", "University District") |
| **Delivery Fee** | The fee charged to customers ordering from within this zone |
| **Min Order** | The minimum order value required to qualify for delivery to this zone |

3. Use the **Drive Time slider** to set your delivery radius in minutes (5 to 60 minutes of driving)
4. The map *(mobile only)* will automatically redraw the zone boundary as you adjust the slider
5. Wait for the green **"Zone Calculated"** badge to appear in the map corner — this confirms the boundary data has been saved
6. Tap **Save Zone**

<Tip>
  Start with your most common delivery area. You can always add more zones later with different fees and minimums.
</Tip>

---

## Editing an existing zone

When you open an existing zone to edit it:
- The saved zone name, fee, and minimum order will be pre-filled
- The saved drive time and boundary will be shown on the map
- The zone is immediately ready to save if you don't need to change anything

To update the boundary, simply move the **Drive Time slider** — the map will recalculate and draw the new boundary automatically. Wait for the **"Zone Calculated"** badge before saving.

---

## Map availability

| Platform | Map preview |
|---|---|
| **Mobile (iOS/Android)** | Full interactive map with polygon drawn on screen |
| **Web** | No map preview, but zone calculation still works correctly |

<Note>
  On web, the zone boundary is calculated and saved correctly even though the map is not displayed. You can always view the zone visually on the mobile app after saving.
</Note>

---

## Tips for setting up your zones

- **Create multiple zones** with different fees — for example, a lower fee for nearby neighbourhoods and a higher fee for areas further away
- **Use minimum order amounts** to ensure delivery to distant zones remains profitable
- **Test your zones** by checking where the boundary falls relative to key landmarks in your area (e.g., "does this zone include the university campus?")
- **Shorter drive times** (e.g., 10–15 min) are better for hot food that needs to arrive quickly; **longer times** (e.g., 30–45 min) work for packaged goods or cold items

## Drive time slider reference

| Slider position | Approximate coverage |
|---|---|
| 5 minutes | Very local — a few blocks |
| 15 minutes | A typical urban neighbourhood |
| 30 minutes | A large portion of a small city |
| 45–60 minutes | Regional — suitable for farms or rural businesses |
