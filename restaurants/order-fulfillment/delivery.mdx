title: "Delivery"
---

Go to **Fulfillment Settings → Delivery** to configure everything related to how orders reach your customers.

## Enable or disable delivery

At the top of the page you'll see a toggle labeled **Offering delivery / Not offering delivery**. Toggle it on to activate delivery. Toggling it off hides delivery from customers immediately — useful if you run out of drivers or close early.

## Delivery hours

By default, delivery is available during your normal business hours.

If you want to restrict delivery to different hours, turn off the **Delivery available during normal business hours** toggle. A weekly schedule will appear where you can set open and close times per day.

<Note>
  If a day is marked as closed in your delivery hours, customers will not see delivery as an option that day, even if your restaurant is open for pickup.
</Note>

## Default delivery settings

The **Default delivery** section sets your baseline delivery rules. All orders default to these unless you add delivery zones (see below).

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Min order \$** | The minimum order value required for delivery |
| **Radius (in min)** | Maximum drive time in minutes from your location |
| **Delivery fee (\$)** | The flat fee charged to the customer |
| **Free delivery from** | Orders above this amount get free delivery |

<Tip>
  Learn about delivery zones [here →](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/delivery-zones)
</Tip>

To edit these fields, click the **Edit** button in the Default delivery section. A panel will slide up where you can update each value.

<Warning>
  If you leave the delivery fee at \$0.00, customers will not be charged for delivery. Set this intentionally.
</Warning>

## Delivery zones

Delivery zones let you charge different fees or set different minimums based on how far the customer is from you.

**To enable zones:**

1. Toggle on **Enable delivery zones**
2. Click **Add radius** to create your first zone
3. Give the zone a name, set a radius in drive-time minutes, a fee, and a minimum order
4. Repeat for each tier you want (e.g. 0–15 min, 15–30 min)

<Tip>
  A common setup is a low fee for close customers and a higher fee (or no delivery) for customers farther away. Start with two zones and adjust based on driver feedback.
</Tip>

Once zones are saved, the zone rules override your default delivery settings for matching customers.

## Own delivery vs. platform delivery

The **Own Delivery** toggle controls whether you use your own drivers.

- **On** — your drivers handle deliveries, and the Driver Fleet page becomes active
- **Off** — delivery is handled outside this system (e.g. third-party couriers)

If you turn on Own Delivery, a **delivery fleet** section appears at the bottom of the page. See [Driver Fleet](/order-settings/driver-fleet) for setup instructions.

## Saving changes

Tap the floating **Save** button at the bottom right of the screen to save all changes. Changes to fees and hours take effect immediately after saving.
