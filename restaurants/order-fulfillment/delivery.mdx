title: "Delivery"

Go to **Fulfillment Settings → Delivery Settings** to configure everything related to how orders reach your customers.

## Enable or disable delivery

At the top of the page you'll see a toggle labeled **Offering Delivery**. Turn it on to activate delivery. Turning it off hides delivery from customers immediately — useful if you run out of drivers or close early.

## Use Own Delivery Fleet

Right below it is **Use Own Delivery Fleet**, described in-app as: _"By enabling this, local dispatch networks will not process your deliveries."_

<Warning>
  **This toggle does more than route your deliveries — it's also what unlocks your fee and zone settings.** With it off, Local Pass's own dispatch network handles your deliveries and this page shows only the on/off toggle and your delivery hours — there's no fee, minimum order, radius, or zone configuration here at all while it's off. Turn it on to reveal **Driver Fleet** and **Standard Delivery Rules** (fees, radius, zones) below.
</Warning>

## Driver Fleet

Appears once **Use Own Delivery Fleet** is on: generate an invite code or manage your restaurant's own drivers. See [Driver Fleet](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/driver-fleet) — and note the app-only caveat on that page, since the web link here currently doesn't open.

## Standard Delivery Rules

Also appears only with Use Own Delivery Fleet on. These are your baseline delivery rules — calculated using Mapbox's dynamic drive-time routing — and apply whenever a custom zone (below) doesn't match.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Min order \$** | The minimum order value required for delivery |
| **Delivery fee (\$)** | The flat fee charged to the customer |
| **Max Drive Time** | A slider from 5 to 60 minutes (5-minute steps) — your maximum delivery radius, by drive time rather than straight-line distance |
| **Free delivery from (\$)** | Orders above this amount get free delivery |

<Warning>
  If you leave the delivery fee at \$0.00, customers will not be charged for delivery. Set this intentionally.
</Warning>

## Custom Delivery Zones

Toggle **Custom Delivery Zones** on to override the standard rules above for specific drive-time radii — for example, a lower fee close by and a higher fee further out.

1. Toggle it on, then tap **Add Custom Zone**
2. Give the zone a name, set a **Delivery Fee**, a **Min Order**, and a **Drive Time Range** (same 5–60 minute slider)
3. Tap **Save Zone**
4. Repeat for each tier you want (e.g. 0–15 min, 15–30 min)

See [Delivery zones](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/delivery-zones) for more on how drive-time zones are calculated, including why the map only shows on mobile.

## Delivery hours

By default, delivery is available during your normal business hours — this section is visible whenever delivery is on, regardless of the Own Delivery Fleet toggle.

Turn off **Deliver during normal business hours** to set a separate weekly schedule for delivery specifically, with its own open/close times per day.

<Note>
  If a day is marked as closed in your delivery hours, customers will not see delivery as an option that day, even if your restaurant is open for pickup.
</Note>

## Saving changes

Tap **Save Settings** at the top of the page. Changes to fees and hours take effect immediately after saving.
