**Where to find it:** Main Navigation → Drivers

The Driver Fleet table gives you a live overview of all drivers registered to your fleet. You can see who is available, who is on a delivery, how each driver is performing, and invite new drivers to join.

---

## Quick Stats

Four summary cards at the top of the page show your fleet at a glance:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Drivers** | All drivers registered to your fleet |
| **Available** | Drivers currently online and not on a delivery |
| **On Delivery** | Drivers actively completing a delivery |
| **Offline** | Drivers not currently on shift |

---

## Searching and Filtering

**Search bar:** Type a driver's first name, last name, or driver ID number to find them quickly.

**Status filters:**

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| All | Every driver in your fleet |
| Online | Drivers on shift and available |
| Busy | Drivers currently on a delivery |
| Offline | Drivers not on shift |

**Vehicle filters:**

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| All | Every vehicle type |
| Vehicle | Car or van drivers |
| Bike | Bicycle couriers |
| Foot | Walking couriers |

---

## Sorting

Use the **Sort** dropdown to organise your driver list by:

- **Name** — Alphabetical
- **Status** — Groups available, busy, and offline drivers
- **Quality** — Acceptance rate (highest to lowest)
- **Distance** — Total kilometres delivered
- **Rating** — Average customer rating

Use the **↑ / ↓ arrow button** to switch between ascending and descending order.

---

## Driver Cards (Mobile) and Rows (Desktop)

Each driver in the list shows:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Profile photo** | Driver's profile picture or their initial if no photo |
| **Name** | First and last name |
| **Driver ID** | Their unique fleet number |
| **Phone** | Contact number (desktop view) |
| **Vehicle** | Make and model of their vehicle |
| **Status badge** | AVAILABLE (green) / ON DELIVERY (red) / OFFLINE (grey) |
| **Quality %** | Acceptance rate — green 90%+, amber 75–89%, red below 75% |
| **Distance** | Total distance delivered (km) |
| **Rating** | Average customer star rating |

---

## Driver Details

Tap any driver card or row to open the **Driver Details** panel. This shows:

- Full contact information (name, phone, email)
- Driver ID number
- Vehicle details: make, model, year, plate number
- Performance stats:
  - Quality (acceptance rate)
  - Overall rating
  - Total distance delivered
  - Current wallet balance (earnings)

---

## Inviting New Drivers

Tap the **Invite** button in the top-right corner to open the invitation panel.

### Option 1 — Share the Registration Code

A unique registration code is shown at the top of the invite panel. Copy it by tapping the **copy icon** and share it with your driver via text or email. They enter this code when signing up on the Local Pass driver app to automatically join your fleet.

### Option 2 — Send SMS Invites

1. Enter one or more phone numbers in the text field — one number per line
2. Use standard Canadian format (e.g., 604-555-0100)
3. Tap **Send Invites**

The system will send each person an SMS containing your fleet registration code and a link to download the driver app. A confirmation message will show how many invites were sent successfully.

<Note>
  You can send invites to multiple drivers at once by entering each phone number on a separate line.
</Note>

---

## Refreshing the fleet list

Tap the **refresh icon** (dark button, top-right) to reload the latest driver data. Use this if you've just added a new driver and want to confirm they appear in the list.

---

## Tips for managing your fleet

- Monitor the **Quality %** column regularly — drivers below 75% may need coaching or review
- The **On Delivery** count in the stats cards tells you instantly how stretched your fleet is at any moment
- Use **vehicle filters** to find the right driver type for specific order sizes (e.g., bike couriers for small orders, vehicle drivers for bulk deliveries)
