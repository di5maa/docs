Go to **Fulfillment Settings → General** to control which geographic areas your restaurant appears in on the platform.

## Service locations

Service locations determine where customers can discover and order from your restaurant. If your restaurant is not listed under a service location, customers in that area will not see you.

### Setting your service locations

1. Click the **Select your province** dropdown and choose your province
2. A second dropdown appears — select one or more cities within that province
3. Selected cities appear as tags below the dropdowns
4. Click **Save** when you're done

<Note>
  You must save from this page for the changes to take effect. The Save button appears in the top right once you've made a selection.
</Note>

### Changing your service locations

To update your locations, select a new province and city combination. Your previous selections will be replaced when you save.

If you operate in multiple provinces, contact support — multi-province configuration requires additional setup.

<Warning>
  Removing a service location means customers in that area will no longer find your restaurant. Only change this if you've intentionally stopped serving that region.
</Warning>
