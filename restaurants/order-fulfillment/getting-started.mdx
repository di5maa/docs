Order settings control everything about how customers receive their orders. Before you start taking orders, you need to configure at least one fulfillment method — delivery, pickup, or both.

## What you can configure

**Fulfillment Settings** is one hub screen with cards linking to each area, plus a Service Areas section built right into the same page:

- **Delivery Settings** — Enable delivery, set your hours, and (if you use your own drivers) fees, radius, and zones.
- **Pickup Settings** — Enable in-store or curbside pickup and set your pickup location(s).
- **Driver Fleet** — Only shown once you've turned on **Use Own Delivery Fleet** on the Delivery page. See the note on that page about where this currently works.
- **Service Areas** — Not a separate page — it's a section at the bottom of this same Fulfillment Settings screen. Pick which of six Vancouver Island regions your restaurant appears in.

## Recommended setup order

Complete these steps in order to avoid missing orders or misconfigured fees.

1. Go to **Fulfillment Settings → Delivery Settings**
2. Toggle delivery on. If you're using your own drivers, also turn on **Use Own Delivery Fleet** — this is what unlocks the fee, radius, and zone fields (see [Delivery](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/delivery) for why that gate exists)
3. Go to **Pickup Settings** and toggle pickup on if you offer it
4. If you use your own drivers, generate a registration code from the Delivery page and set up your fleet
5. Scroll to **Service Areas** at the bottom of the Fulfillment Settings screen and confirm your regions are selected

<Note>
  If delivery is on but "Use Own Delivery Fleet" is off, your delivery fee/minimum order/radius fields won't appear on this screen at all — that combination is currently only configurable elsewhere. See [Delivery](/restaurants/order-fulfillment/delivery) for what to expect.
</Note>

## Finding fulfillment settings

From the dashboard, select **Fulfillment Settings** in the left navigation. On mobile, tap the menu icon at the top left to open the drawer, then select **Fulfillment Settings**.

<Tip>
  Changes to delivery settings take effect immediately after you tap **Save Settings**. If you're mid-service, make changes between rushes to avoid confusion.
</Tip>
