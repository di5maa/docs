title: "Configuration"
---

Order settings control everything about how customers receive their orders. Before you start taking orders, you need to configure at least one fulfillment method — delivery, pickup, or both.

## What you can configure

There are four sections under **Fulfillment Settings**:

- **Delivery** — Enable delivery, set your hours, fees, and radius. If you use your own drivers, you can also manage a driver fleet from here.
- **Pickup** — Enable in-store or curbside pickup and set your pickup location.
- **Driver Fleet** — Invite and monitor your own delivery drivers. This section only appears once you've generated a driver registration code.
- **General** — Set the service locations where your restaurant is active.

## Recommended setup order

Complete these steps in order to avoid missing orders or misconfigured fees.

1. Go to **Settings → Fulfillment Settings → Delivery**
2. Toggle delivery on and configure your default delivery settings (fee, minimum order, radius)
3. Go to **Pickup** and toggle pickup on if you offer it
4. If you use your own drivers, generate a registration code from the Delivery page and set up your fleet
5. Return to **General** and confirm your service locations

<Note>
  If you skip the delivery fee or minimum order amount, customers will see \$0.00 at checkout. Set these before going live.
</Note>

## Finding fulfillment settings

From the dashboard, select **Fulfillment Settings** in the left navigation. On mobile, tap the menu icon at the top left to open the drawer, then select **Fulfillment Settings**.

The settings are split into sub-pages — use the side menu on desktop or navigate between pages on mobile.

<Tip>
  Changes to delivery settings take effect immediately. If you're mid-service, make changes between rushes to avoid confusion.
</Tip>
