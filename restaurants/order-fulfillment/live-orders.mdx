Welcome to your **Receive Orders** dashboard! This is your restaurant's live command center. From this screen, you will manage all incoming customer orders, communicate preparation times, dispatch delivery drivers, and track active pickups.

This guide breaks down everything you need to know to smoothly fulfill orders using the Local Pass Business platform.

## 1. Controlling Your Store's Status

During a lunch rush or unexpected kitchen delay, you might need to pause incoming orders to catch up. You can control this directly from the top right of your dashboard.

- **ACCEPT ORDERS:** Your store is open and actively receiving new orders.
- **STOP ORDERS:** If you need a breather, click this button. Your store will still be visible to customers on the app, but they will be temporarily prevented from placing new orders. Click **Accept Orders** to turn your store back on when you are ready.

<Tip>
  💡 **PRO TIP**: Managing Inventory on the Fly Run out of an ingredient? Click the Out-of-stock Items button at the top of the screen to quickly jump to your menu settings and temporarily hide items so customers can't order them.
</Tip>

## 2. Navigating the Order Dashboard

Your dashboard is split into two main sections:

1. **The Order Queue (Left Sidebar):** This lists all of your active and upcoming orders. A notification bell at the top will show you how many _new, unaccepted_ orders are currently waiting for your attention. Click on any order in this list to view its details.
2. **Order Details (Main Screen):** When you select an order, this panel displays everything you need to prepare the meal: the customer's name and phone number, the fulfillment type (Pickup vs. Delivery), the ordered items (including special requests or extra options), and the financial breakdown.

## 3. Step-by-Step: Fulfilling an Order

When a new order chimes in, follow these steps to process it:

### Step 1: Review the Order & Adjust Prep Time

Look over the items, customer notes, and the requested fulfillment type. The system automatically assigns an **Estimated Ready Time**.

If your kitchen needs more (or less) time to prepare the food:

- Use the large **[- 5]** and **[+ 5]** buttons to adjust the ready time in 5-minute increments.
- ⚠️ **Important:** After adjusting the time, a **SAVE** button will appear. You _must_ click Save to lock in the new time.
- _What happens next?_ The system will automatically send a push notification to the customer apologizing for the slight delay and updating their pickup/delivery ETA.

### Step 2: Accept the Order

When your kitchen starts cooking, click the blue **Accept Order** button at the bottom of the screen.

- **For Pickup Orders:** The customer is notified that their food is being prepared.
- **For Delivery Orders:** Accepting the order automatically dispatches a driver.
  - _If using Local Pass Drivers:_ Our system immediately starts pinging nearby couriers to head to your restaurant.
  - _If using your Own Delivery Fleet:_ The system automatically alerts the active drivers registered to your restaurant's fleet.

### Step 3: Handoff and Completion

Once the food is boxed and ready to go, the final step depends on the order type:

- **For Pickup Orders:** Click **Ready for Pickup**. This pings the customer's phone, telling them to come inside and grab their food.
- **For Delivery Orders:** You will see a live "Driver Arrival Estimate" on the screen. Once the driver arrives and you hand them the food, click **Order Picked Up**. This updates your dashboard to "Completed" and tells the customer their food is officially on the way!

## 4. Cancelling an Order

If you are unable to fulfill an order (e.g., you are completely out of a requested item and cannot reach the customer), you can cancel it directly from the dashboard.

1. Select the order and click the red **Cancel** button at the bottom of the screen.
2. A confirmation window will appear showing the total refund amount.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

**What happens when you cancel?**

- The customer receives an immediate push notification and an email explaining that the restaurant had to cancel the order due to fulfillment issues.
- A full refund is automatically issued to the customer's original payment method.

> 🛑 **NOTE ON FEES:** Because credit card processing networks do not refund initial transaction fees, standard Stripe processing fees (approx. 2.9% + \$0.30) for the cancelled order will be deducted from your merchant payout balance as a cancellation fee.

## 5. Need Help with an Active Order?

If you run into an issue with a driver, a customer dispute, or a system error on a live order, click the **Headset Icon (Support)** button at the top of the order details.

This instantly opens a live chat with Local Pass Support, automatically attaching the specific Order ID and details to your message so our team can help you resolve it immediately.
