title: "Pickup"
---

Go to **Fulfillment Settings → Pickup** to control how customers collect orders in person.

## Enable or disable pickup

At the top of the page, toggle **Offering pickups / Not offering pickups** on or off. Turning this off removes pickup as an option at checkout for all customers.

## Default pickup location

Your default pickup location is the address customers are directed to when they choose pickup. This is usually your restaurant's main address.

**If you haven't set one yet**, you'll see a prompt saying your default pickup location is not set, with a **Create now** link. Click it to open the pickup location setup flow.

**If you already have one**, it appears as a card showing the location name, address, and your pickup hours for each day of the week.

### Editing your pickup location

Click the **more options icon** (three dots) on the location card and select **Edit**. From there you can update:

- Location name
- Address
- Business hours (open/close times per day, or mark days as Closed)

<Note>
  Pickup hours are separate from your restaurant's delivery hours. A customer can be allowed to pick up on days you don't offer delivery.
</Note>

## Saving changes

Pickup settings save automatically when you make changes to the toggle. For location edits, save inside the location editor before closing the panel.
