---
title: "Pickup"
---

The **Pickup** settings page allows you to control exactly how and where customers collect their orders. Whether you operate from a single storefront or manage a chain of locations, you can customize your availability and hours here.

## General Availability

At the top of the page, you will see a main status toggle:

- **Offering Pickups:** Turn this **ON** to allow customers to select "Pickup" at checkout.
- **Not Offering Pickups:** Turn this **OFF** to disable pickup orders entirely (e.g., if you are renovation-only or delivery-only for a period).

## Default Pickup Location

You will see your **Default Pickup Location** card, displaying:

1. **Location Name**
2. **Address**
3. **Location Hours:** The specific hours customers can pick up orders (these can differ from your general store opening hours).

**To Edit:** Click the **Menu icon** (three vertical dots) on the location card. This opens a dialogue where you can:

- Update the **Address**.
  - Update business address and location name.
- Rename the **Location**.
  - Located in the same box, update your business location name here. 
- Modify the specific **Pickup Hours** for each day of the week.
