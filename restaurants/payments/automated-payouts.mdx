---
title: "Automated payouts"
---

Local Pass runs a Firebase Cloud Function every **night at 3:00 AM Pacific Time** (America/Vancouver) to automatically process payouts for all active vendors. No vendor or admin action is required — eligible wallets are settled and Stripe transfers are initiated without intervention.

<Note>
  **There's no manual cash-out.** Payouts only happen on this nightly schedule — there's no button anywhere (app or web) to trigger one early. See [Payouts & the Wallet page](/payments/manual-payouts) for what vendors can actually see and do.
</Note>

## Technical specification

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Schedule** | Every night at 03:00 |
| **Timezone** | `America/Vancouver` (Pacific Time) |
| **Min payout** | \$5.00 CAD |
