---
title: "Automated payouts"
---

Local Pass runs a Firebase Cloud Function every **Monday at 3:00 AM Pacific Time** (America/Vancouver) to automatically process payouts for all active vendors. No vendor or admin action is required — eligible wallets are settled and Stripe transfers are initiated without intervention.

<Tip>
  The Monday 3 AM schedule follows industry-standard weekly settlement timing. Vendors who prefer more frequent access to their funds can use [Manual Cash Out](/payments/manual-payouts) at any time.
</Tip>

## Technical specification

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Schedule** | Every Monday at 03:00 |
| **Timezone** | `America/Vancouver` (Pacific Time) |
| **Min payout** | \$5.00 CAD |
