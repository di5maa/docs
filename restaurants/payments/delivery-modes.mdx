## Overview

Local Pass supports three fulfilment modes: Local Pass Delivery, Pickup, and Vendor's Own Delivery. The mode determines which lines show up in your wallet's Payment Breakdown when an order completes, and in turn the net amount transferred at payout time.

<Note>
  The delivery mode doesn't change the nightly payout schedule or the transfer process — it only determines which fee lines apply to a given order.
</Note>

---

## Mode comparison

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Local Pass Delivery">
    ### Local Pass Delivery

    A customer places an order and selects standard delivery. Local Pass dispatches a driver from its network to collect the order from the vendor and deliver it to the customer.

    **How it works:**

    - Customer pays for delivery at checkout
    - Local Pass assigns a driver from its network
    - Driver collects from the vendor's location
    - Vendor is not involved in the logistics

    **Wallet impact:**

    | Line | Included? | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Subtotal** | ✅ Yes | Order subtotal |
    | **Tax** | ✅ Yes | Customer-paid tax |
    | **Platform fee** | ✅ Yes | Local Pass service fee |
    | **Driver payouts** | ⚠️ Conditional | Included if you contribute to driver costs |
    | **Cancellation fees** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if you cancel the order |
    | **Discounts** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if a promotion applied |

    <Note>
      **Driver payouts** are deducted **after** the nightly transfer completes, not from the transfer amount itself. They're tracked separately for driver cost accounting.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pickup">
    ### Pickup

    The customer places an order and collects it directly from the vendor's location. No driver is dispatched.

    **How it works:**

    - Customer selects pickup at checkout
    - Order is prepared by the vendor
    - Customer arrives and collects their order
    - No delivery logistics involved

    **Wallet impact:**

    | Line | Included? | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Subtotal** | ✅ Yes | Order subtotal |
    | **Tax** | ✅ Yes | Customer-paid tax |
    | **Platform fee** | ✅ Yes | Local Pass service fee |
    | **Driver payouts** | ❌ No | No driver involved |
    | **Cancellation fees** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if you cancel the order |
    | **Discounts** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if a promotion applied |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Vendor's Own Delivery">
    ### Vendor's Own Delivery

    The vendor uses their own vehicle, staff, or courier to deliver the order. Local Pass handles the order placement and payment, but not the physical logistics.

    **How it works:**

    - Customer selects vendor delivery at checkout
    - Local Pass processes payment as normal
    - Vendor arranges delivery using their own resources
    - No Local Pass driver dispatch

    **Wallet impact:**

    | Line | Included? | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Subtotal** | ✅ Yes | Order subtotal |
    | **Tax** | ✅ Yes | Customer-paid tax |
    | **Platform fee** | ✅ Yes | Local Pass service fee |
    | **Driver payouts** | ❌ No | You handle your own delivery costs |
    | **Cancellation fees** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if you cancel the order |
    | **Discounts** | ⚠️ Conditional | Only if a promotion applied |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Driver payouts explained

The **Driver payouts** line appears in your wallet **only for Local Pass Delivery** orders where you contribute toward driver costs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is it?">
    When you use your own free-delivery offer on a Local Pass Delivery order, the customer pays \$0 for delivery at checkout. Local Pass covers the driver's cost on your behalf, and that amount is then deducted from your wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is it deducted?">
    Driver payouts are **not subtracted from the nightly transfer amount itself**. They're deducted from your wallet balance **after** a successful transfer, so your payout record always reflects the transfer on its own, with driver costs tracked separately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it shown to vendors?">
    Yes. Whenever a driver payout applies, a "Driver payouts: −\$X.XX" line item appears in the Payment Breakdown section of the Wallet page, along with an info button explaining it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
