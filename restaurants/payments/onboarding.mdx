</Warning>

## Connecting your payment account

Local Pass works with Stripe, a widely used global payment processor, to handle vendor payouts securely. Setting this up gives you:

- A dashboard to view your own payouts, balance, and transaction history
- Direct bank account linkage for receiving CAD transfers
- Identity verification handled entirely by the payment processor — Local Pass never sees your sensitive personal or financial data
- Bank-level security and compliance standards

<Note>
  Vendors can access deeper transaction detail (individual payouts, disputes, bank statements) directly from their payment account dashboard at any time. Local Pass shows a summary view only.
</Note>

## Onboarding paths

When a vendor opens **Dashboard → Payments → Payment Setup**, they choose one of two paths:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="New payment account">
    ### For vendors with no existing payment account

    Local Pass creates the account automatically and redirects the vendor to a secure, hosted setup flow.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select 'I don't have a payment account'">
        Tap the option on the Payment Setup screen inside the vendor dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Local Pass creates the account">
        An account is generated to be used for onboarding.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Onboarding link generated">
        You'll get a time-limited link and be redirected to complete setup.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete setup">
        You're redirected to a secure hosted page to provide identity, business, and banking information. All data entry happens off the Local Pass platform, on the payment processor's own secure domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Return to Local Pass">
        After completing (or exiting) the setup flow, you're returned to the Local Pass completion page. Verification approval is reflected automatically once it comes through.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Existing payment account">
    ### For vendors who already have a payment account

    Vendors can link an existing account by entering their Account ID directly.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select 'I have a payment account'">
        An input field appears prompting you for your existing Account ID.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the Account ID">
        The ID follows the format `acct_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`. You can find this in your payment account's own dashboard under Settings → Account Details.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Local Pass validates & generates onboarding link">
        If successful, the account is linked and you're redirected to complete any additional requirements.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Account connected">
        Once verification is confirmed, your record is updated and payouts can be initiated.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If the onboarding link fails, the most common cause is an invalid account ID. Double-check the format starts with `acct_` and was copied from the correct account.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What information you'll need

Identity and banking verification (KYC — Know Your Customer) is handled entirely by the payment processor's own infrastructure. Local Pass never stores, views, or processes any of the following.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Personal information">
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Legal name** | Must match government-issued photo ID |
    | **Date of birth** | Required for identity verification |
    | **Home address** | Current address in Canada |
    | **Phone number** | Used for 2FA and account recovery |
    | **Email address** | Pre-filled from your Local Pass account |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Business information">
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Business type** | Defaulted to `Individual` by Local Pass. Sole proprietors and small businesses qualify. |
    | **Business description** | Brief description of what you sell |
    | **SIN / Tax number** | Required for tax compliance — collected directly by the payment processor, never seen by Local Pass |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Banking details">
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Bank account** | Canadian institution number, transit number, and account number |
    | **Government-issued ID** | A photo of a driver's licence or passport may be requested for higher-volume accounts |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Local Pass **never stores, views, or processes** any sensitive personal or financial data. All verification information is submitted directly to and held by the payment processor on their own compliant infrastructure.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **If you don't have a website, use [LOCALPASS.](http://LOCALPASS.ca)CA or your store link**
</Note>

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Account not verified / restricted">
    Additional documents may be required for identity verification. Log in to your payment account dashboard to see what's required and resolve any outstanding items. Local Pass cannot intervene in the verification process itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invalid Account ID">
    The entered `acct_XXXXXX` ID doesn't exist or isn't a valid account. Double-check the ID from your payment account dashboard under **Settings → Account Details**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Onboarding link expired">
    Onboarding links are time-limited. If you waited too long before clicking the link, re-initiate the setup flow from **Dashboard → Payments → Payment Setup** to generate a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bank details rejected">
    Incorrect branch, institution, or transit numbers. Update your banking information directly inside your payment account dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account already connected to another platform">
    Some payment accounts can't be connected to multiple platforms simultaneously. You may need to create a new, separate account for Local Pass.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
