## How it works

Local Pass uses a **wallet-based settlement model**. Revenue accumulates in a vendor's merchant wallet as orders are completed. Funds are then transferred to the vendor's connected Stripe Express account — either automatically every Monday or manually on demand.

## Payout methods at a glance

|  | Auto Weekly | Manual Cash Out | Instant Payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | Every Monday 3 AM PT | Vendor taps "Cash Out" | Vendor taps "Instant Payout" |
| **Fee** | None | None | \$1.00 CAD |
| **Arrival** | 2–7 business days | 2–7 business days | Within minutes |
| **Minimum** | \$5.00 CAD | Wallet \> \$0 | Wallet \> \$0 |
| **Eligibility** | All active vendors | All vendors | `Debit card must be connected to your Stripe account` |

## Prerequisites checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe Express">
    Complete onboarding at **Dashboard → Payments → Stripe Setup**. See [Stripe Onboarding](/payments/stripe-onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stripe verification approved">
    Stripe reviews the submitted identity and banking details. This can take a few minutes to a few business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish at least one product">
    Products cannot be published without a connected Stripe account. The wallet will not accumulate a balance until orders are placed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your first payout">
    Once orders complete, your wallet balance builds. The next Monday at 3 AM PT, the automated run will process the transfer — or you can cash out manually at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
