title: "Add new promotion"
---

## 1. Internal Details

- **Title (Internal):** Enter a name for your own reference (e.g., "Summer Lunch Special"). Customers never see this.
- **Description (Internal):** Add optional notes about the promotion's goal or target audience.

## 2. Choose a Promotion Type

Pick one of the three types offered for a brand-new promotion: **Discount on Products**, **Buy X Get Y**, or **Amount off Order**. (Free Shipping isn't offered here yet — see [Start here](/restaurants/promotions-manager/start-here) for why.)

<Note>
  You can't change the Type after creating the promotion. If you need a different type, create a new promotion instead.
</Note>

## 3. Configuring the Offer

The configuration fields will change depending on the **Promotion Type** you selected.

### Discount on Products

1. **Select Products or Categories:** Tap **Browse** to open the picker. Switch between Products and Categories, and select as many as you need.
2. **Discount Value:** Set a **Percentage (%)** or **Fixed Amount (\$)** off the selected items.
3. **Minimum Requirement (optional):** Require a minimum spend or item quantity before the discount applies.

### Buy X Get Y

1. **Customer Buys:** Tap **Browse** to select the products or categories a customer must buy to trigger the deal, or choose **Any product**.
2. **Customer Gets:** Select the reward products or categories the same way, then define the reward:
   - **Percentage:** (e.g., "Buy a Burger, get the second 50% off").
   - **Fixed Amount:** (e.g., "Buy a Pizza, get \$5.00 off the next one").
   - **Free:** (e.g., "Buy one, get one free").
3. **How Many:** Set the quantity a customer needs to buy to trigger the reward.
4. **Minimum Requirement (optional):** Require a minimum spend or item quantity in addition to the qualifying products.

<Warning>
  **You must select at least one product or category for both the "Buy" and "Get" side**, unless that side is set to "Any product." Leaving a required side empty will block you from saving.
</Warning>

### Amount off Order

1. **Discount Type:** Choose between a **Percentage (%)** or a **Fixed Amount (\$)**.
   <Warning>
     A percentage discount only applies in the Local Pass app — it does not work on localpass.ca. Use a fixed dollar amount if you want the promotion to also work on the website.
   </Warning>
2. **Discount Value:** Enter the numerical value (e.g., "15" for 15% off, or "5" for \$5 off).
3. **Minimum Requirement (optional):** Require a minimum spend or item quantity before the discount applies.

## 4. Scheduling

- **Start Date:** Select the date and time the promotion becomes valid. If left blank, it defaults to "Now".
- **End Date:** (Optional) Select a date and time for the promotion to expire automatically.
- **Restrict by Day & Time:** (Optional) Limit the promotion to specific days of the week and a start/end time — useful for something like a weekday lunch special.

## 5. Usage Limits

- **Limit to one use per customer:** Ensures a specific customer cannot redeem the same offer more than once.

## 6. Visibility

- **Featured:** Toggle this **ON** to pin the promotion to the "Featured Promotions" carousel on your storefront. This is highly recommended for time-sensitive or high-value offers to increase visibility.

## 7. Saving and Status

When you are finished configuring the promotion, choose one of the following actions:

- **Save as Draft:** (Gray Button) Saves your settings but keeps the promotion **inactive**. It will not be visible to customers or applied to carts. Use this for preparing campaigns in advance.
- **Publish:** (Green Button) Saves the promotion and immediately sets it to **Active** (provided the Start Date has arrived).
