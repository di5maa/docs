title: "Editing an Existing Promotion"
description: "If you need to update the terms, duration, or details of a campaign, you can edit an existing promotion. To access the editor, navigate to your Promotions list and select the promotion you wish to modify."
---

## 1. Modifying Internal Details

The following internal details can be updated to help you organize your campaigns:

- **Internal Title:** Update the name used for your own reference.
- **Description:** Modify the internal notes regarding the promotion.

<Note>
  **You can't change the promotion's Type** (e.g., turning a "Buy X Get Y" into an "Amount off Order") once it's created. Create a new promotion instead if you need a different type.
</Note>

## 2. Updating Offer Logic

Depending on the Promotion Type, you can adjust the specific rules described in [Add new promotion](/restaurants/promotions-manager/add-new-promotion):

- **Discount Value:** Update the **Percentage (%)** or **Fixed Amount (\$)** being offered.
- **Minimum Requirement:** Adjust the minimum purchase amount or item quantity required to trigger the discount.
- **Products or Categories:**
  - Tap **Browse** to change which products or categories are eligible.
  - Use the **trash icon** next to a listed item to remove it from the promotion.

<Warning>
  On Buy X Get Y and Discount on Products, you can't save with an empty picker on a required side — add at least one product or category, or switch that side to "Any product."
</Warning>

## 3. Scheduling and Duration

- **Start Date:** Modify when the promotion begins.
- **End Date:** Enable or disable the end date switch. If enabled, pick a new expiration date and time.
- **Restrict by Day & Time:** Turn scheduling on or off, and adjust which days and hours it applies to.

## 4. Usage Limits and Visibility

- **Per Customer Limit:** Toggle "Limit to one use per customer" to restrict or allow repeat redemptions by the same customer.
- **Featured Status:** Toggle **Featured** on or off. Turning this ON puts the promotion in the featured carousel on your storefront.

## 5. Saving Changes

When you are done editing, you have two options to save your changes:

### Save as Draft

Clicking **Save as Draft** will:

1. Save all your changes.
2. **Deactivate** the promotion immediately.
3. Set the status to **Draft**. _Use this if you want to pull a live promotion offline to work on it without deleting it._

### Publish

Clicking **Publish** will:

1. Save all your changes.
2. **Activate** the promotion immediately (assuming the Start Date has passed).
3. Ensure the promotion is live and usable by customers.

<Note>
  **There's no separate "pause" action.** Saving as Draft is how you take a promotion offline temporarily. Deleting a promotion removes it permanently and can't be undone.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What's happening |
| :-- | :-- |
| **My promotion shows "Inactive" in a list, but I know I published it** | Open it in Edit and check its Status directly — that's the source of truth. If it says Active there, customers can already redeem it, even if a list view elsewhere hasn't caught up yet. |
