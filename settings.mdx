---
title: "Settings"
---

The Settings page is your control centre for managing your Local Pass account. From here you can view your account details, access personal settings, manage your favourite locations, find support, and sign out.

---

## Getting to Settings

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Settings**.

---

## Your Account Overview

At the top of the Settings page, your profile photo, full name, and email address are displayed. This gives you a quick confirmation that you are signed in to the correct account.

---

## Personal Account

This section contains the core settings for your user account.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Displays your registered email address |
| **Phone** | Displays your registered phone number |
| **Personal details** | Opens your full profile editor (name, photo, address, birthday, gender) |
| **Share with a friend** | Opens the referral screen so you can invite friends to Local Pass |
| **Personalize Local Pass** | Lets you customise the design of your Local Pass card |
| **Activity history** | Shows your past rides and food orders |
| **Inbox** | Opens your messages and support conversations |

Tap any row to open that setting.

## Favourite Locations

This section gives you quick access to manage the addresses saved to your account.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Shows your current default address and lets you update it |
| **Favourite merchants** | Shows the list of food vendors you have saved as favourites |
| **View all** | Opens a panel showing all your saved favourite locations |

### Updating Your Home Address

Tap **Home** to open the address update panel. Your current default address is shown next to the label. See [Updating Your Default Address](./updating-your-address) for step-by-step instructions.

---

## Support

This section provides links to help resources, legal documents, and ways to contact the Local Pass team.

| Link | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Report bug** | Opens an email to report a technical issue |
| **Documentation** | Opens the Local Pass help documentation in your browser |
| **Terms of service** | Opens the Local Pass Terms of Service |
| **Privacy policy** | Opens the Local Pass Privacy Policy |
| **Contact Local Pass team** | Opens the Local Pass contact page |
| **Provide feedback** | Opens an email to send feedback |
| **App version** | Shows the current app version (for example, V 1.0.13\+111) |

---

## Danger Zone

The **Danger Zone** section appears at the bottom of the Support block. It contains the option to permanently delete your account.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, orders, and history will be removed. See [Deleting Your Account](./deleting-your-account) for the full process.
</Warning>

---

## Follow Us

At the bottom of the Danger Zone block, you will find links to the Local Pass social media pages.

- Tap the **Facebook** icon to visit the Local Pass Facebook page.
- Tap the **Instagram** icon to visit the Local Pass Instagram page.

---

## Signing Out

If you want to sign out of your account:

1. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page.
2. Tap **Log out**.
3. You will be redirected to the sign-in screen.

<Tip>
  Signing out does not delete your account or any of your data. You can sign back in at any time using your email and password or Google account.
</Tip>
