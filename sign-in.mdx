Welcome to Local Pass — your all-in-one app for local living. This guide walks you through how to sign in or create a new account for the first time.

---

## Getting to the Sign-In Screen

When you open the Local Pass app, you will land on the sign-in screen. You will see the Local Pass logo and the tagline **"Your Super App For Local Living."**

From here you have several options depending on whether you already have an account.

---

## Sign-In Options

### Continue with Google

This is the fastest way to get started if you have a Google account.

1. Tap **Continue with Google**.
2. Select your Google account from the list that appears.
3. You will be taken directly to the Local Pass home screen.

<Note>
  If this is your first time signing in with Google, you will be redirected through a short onboarding flow before reaching the home screen.
</Note>

---

### Continue with Email

If you prefer to use an email address and password:

1. Tap **Continue with email**.
2. Enter your **email address** in the first field.
3. Enter your **password** in the second field.
4. Tap the eye icon on the right side of the password field if you want to show or hide your password.
5. Tap **Login**.

---

### Forgot Your Password?

If you cannot remember your password:

1. Tap **Continue with email**.
2. Type your email address into the email field.
3. Tap **Forgot Password?**
4. A password reset link will be sent to your email address.
5. Open the email and follow the link to set a new password.

<Warning>
  You must enter your email address in the field before tapping "Forgot Password?" or the app will show an error asking for an email.
</Warning>

---

### Driver Login

If you are a licensed taxi driver registering as a driver on Local Pass, tap **Driver Login** instead of the standard sign-in options. This takes you to the dedicated driver sign-in screen.

---

## Creating a New Account

If you do not have a Local Pass account yet:

1. From the sign-in screen, tap **Create Account**.
2. You will be taken to the account creation page where you can register with your name, email, and a password.

---

## Signing In with a Referral Code

If a friend shared a referral link with you, open that link on your device. The app will automatically recognise the referral code and display a message such as:

> **"\[Friend's name\] invited you to experience Local Pass!"**

You will also see a note confirming that you will receive **3 free deliveries** as a welcome bonus when you join through a referral.

<Tip>
  Make sure to create your account through the referral link to claim your free deliveries. The bonus is applied automatically during sign-up.
</Tip>

---

## Troubleshooting Sign-In Issues

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Email required!" message | Make sure you have typed your email before tapping Login or Forgot Password |
| Cannot sign in with Google | Check your internet connection and try again |
| Password not working | Use the Forgot Password option to reset it |
| App not loading | Close and reopen the app, then try again |
