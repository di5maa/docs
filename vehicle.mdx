Your vehicle profile tells Local Pass — and your customers — what type of vehicle you're driving. Keep it accurate and up to date.

## Basic Vehicle Info

Go to **Driver Profile → Vehicle Profile** and tap **Edit** to update:

- **Make** — the manufacturer (e.g., Toyota, Ford)
- **Model** — the specific model (e.g., Corolla, F-150)
- **Year** — the model year
- **Plate Number** — your licence plate
- **Vehicle Number** — your vehicle identification number

Tap **Save & Exit** when done.

## Vehicle Type

Your vehicle type determines which ride and delivery categories you appear in:

| Type | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Standard** | Regular car — most common |
| **XL** | Larger vehicle, van, or SUV |
| **Van Accessible** | Wheelchair-accessible van |

## Additional Attributes

Tap **Additional Attributes** to add optional features that customers can filter by. These help match you with the right orders.

### Electric Vehicle (EV)

Enable this if your vehicle is fully electric. You'll also be asked to provide:

- **Range** — your vehicle's range in km on a full charge
- **Charging Type** — select from Type 1, Type 2, CHAdeMO, Tesla Supercharger, or CCS

This information helps the app show you nearby charging stations when needed.

### Accessibility & Comfort

| Attribute | When to Enable |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Accessible (Ramp)** | Your vehicle has a wheelchair ramp or is otherwise accessible |
| **Child Seat** | Your vehicle has an installed, secured child seat |
| **Pet-Friendly** | You're comfortable transporting pets |

### Sports Equipment

Enable **Sports Equipment** if your vehicle can transport large gear. Then enable any that apply:

- **Snowboard**
- **Ski**
- **Bicycle**

Tap **Save** at the bottom of the Additional Attributes screen. Changes apply immediately to your profile.

<Note>
  Providing accurate attributes ensures you get matched with orders that fit your vehicle. Misrepresenting your vehicle's capabilities may result in complaints or account review.
</Note>

## Pre-Trip Vehicle Inspection

Before you can go online for **Rides**, you need to complete a Pre-Trip Inspection Log. This isn't optional — see [Getting Started with Rides](/drivers/rides/getting-started) for what happens if you try to go online without one.

### What's checked

Eight items, each a simple pass/fail switch:

**Camera · Body · Fluids · Brakes · Lights · Tires · E-Brake · Wipers**

If something isn't right, leave that item's switch off and describe the issue under **Remarks** rather than just leaving it unmentioned — the remarks field exists specifically to capture concerns the pass/fail switches can't.

### Signature

You'll sign directly on screen before submitting. The completed log — vehicle number, plate number, every item's result, your remarks, and your signature — is saved as a permanent record.

<Warning>
  Inspection results are part of your compliance record. Treat this the way you would a real pre-trip vehicle check, not a formality to tap through — an inaccurate log is a real problem if something goes wrong on a trip afterward.
</Warning>

### How long it lasts

Each completed inspection is recorded as valid for the next 12 hours. In practice, though, the app only re-prompts you to redo it when you restart the app entirely — if you stay logged in and just toggle offline and back online again within the same app session, you won't be asked to repeat it even past the 12-hour mark. Don't rely on that as a reason to skip a real check before a shift that's genuinely different from your last one (new vehicle, different day, anything's changed).

### Reviewing past inspections

Your inspection history is available from your driver profile, so you (or, if ever asked, a regulator) can see when each check was completed and what it found.
