title: "Wallet"
---

The Wallet page is where you manage your payment methods on Local Pass. Having a payment method set up ensures smooth checkout when placing orders or using platform features that require payment.

---

## Getting to the Wallet

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Wallet**.

Or, from the bottom navigation bar, look for the Wallet icon if it is available in your navigation.

---

## What the Wallet Does

The Local Pass Wallet lets you:

- Add and manage credit or debit cards
- View your payment methods on file
- Manage how you pay for platform fees and services

<Note>
  Taxi fares are paid directly to the driver at the end of your trip — they are not processed through the Local Pass Wallet. The Wallet is used for platform-related fees and food order payments.
</Note>

---

## Adding a Payment Method

1. On the Wallet page, tap **Add Payment Method** or the relevant option shown on screen.
2. Enter your card details:
   - Card number
   - Expiry date
   - CVV / security code
   - Cardholder name
3. Tap **Save** or **Add Card** to confirm.

Your card will be saved securely and available for future orders and payments.

<Warning>
  Local Pass uses secure, encrypted payment processing. Never share your card details outside of the official Local Pass app.
</Warning>

---

## Viewing Saved Payment Methods

Your saved payment methods are listed on the Wallet page. Each card shows:

- Card type (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
- Last four digits of the card number
- Expiry date

---

## Removing a Payment Method

To remove a saved card:

1. Find the card you want to remove in your Wallet.
2. Tap the **delete or remove option** associated with that card.
3. Confirm the removal when prompted.

<Warning>
  Make sure you have at least one valid payment method saved if you regularly use Local Pass for food orders. Removing all cards may prevent you from completing purchases.
</Warning>

---

## Getting Help with Wallet Issues

If you have a question about a payment or need help with your Wallet, tap the **Help** button at the top right of the Wallet page. This opens a support panel with information about how payments work on Local Pass.

For billing disputes or refund questions, contact Local Pass support through **Settings → Contact Local Pass team**.

---

## Local Coins

Local Coins are a rewards currency earned through activity on Local Pass. They appear in your account balance and can be redeemed at checkout to reduce the cost of your orders.

Your Local Coins balance is visible on your Local Pass card on the home screen. They are applied automatically at checkout when available, and the redeemed amount is shown in your order summary under **Activity History**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is it safe to save my card on Local Pass?">
     Yes. Local Pass uses industry-standard encryption and secure payment processing to protect your card information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use multiple cards?">
     Yes. You can save multiple payment methods and choose which one to use at checkout.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was I charged a platform fee?">
    A small platform fee is applied to food orders to support the Local Pass service. This is shown clearly in your order breakdown before you confirm the order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get a refund for a cancelled order?">
     Refunds for cancelled orders are processed automatically. You can confirm the refund status in your **Activity History** — cancelled orders will show a refund confirmation banner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
